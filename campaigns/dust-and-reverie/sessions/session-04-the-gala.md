# Session 4: The Gala

> *"These violent delights have violent ends."*

## Session Goals

1. Experience the Consortium's gala celebration
2. Witness Orenthal Vane's final speech
3. Mirela triggers the revolution
4. Mass telepathic awakening of all Woven
5. Final choice: Hunt the Ashen Rider or escape through the Loom Gate

---

## Opening: Return to Dusthollow (Dusk, Day 3)

The PCs return to a town transformed.

### Read-Aloud: A Town Unrecognizable

> As you crest the final ridge, Dusthollow spreads below you—but it's not the dusty frontier town you left this morning.
>
> Lanterns hang from every post and awning, casting warm golden light across Main Street. Chairs and tables have been arranged in neat rows facing a raised stage near the Dusty Rose. A bar has been set up under a silk canopy. The smell of roasting meat and expensive wine drifts on the evening breeze.
>
> And the people. They're everywhere—but not townsfolk. These are *strangers*. Wealthy mages in embroidered robes. Scholars in spectacles and fine wool. Artisans and bards in colorful silks. They laugh, they drink, they gesture expansively.
>
> *Patrons.*
>
> The Woven you know—Harken, Doc Mercer, the Garrett brothers, even Ma Pickett—move among them in unfamiliar roles. Serving drinks. Ushering guests. Playing parts they've never played before.

### The Loom Frame

One detail stands out immediately:

> On the side road leading out of town, a massive archway pulses with arcane light—a Loom Frame, its gate wide open. More guests stream through, stepping from blinding white light into the gala as if walking through a doorway.
>
> Beside the gate, a [[encounters/loom-frame-guardian|Loom Frame Guardian]] stands sentinel. Eight feet tall, its bronze-and-crystal form gleams in the torchlight. It doesn't move, doesn't react to the passing guests. Just watches. Waiting.

### The Bar

> At the silk-draped bar, a figure stands out among the revelry.
>
> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]]—but transformed. Gone is the black duster, the trail dust, the predator's edge. He wears the finest garments imaginable: tailored coat of midnight blue, silver buttons, gleaming rings on every finger. His hair is combed. His boots are polished.
>
> *Lord Aldric Venn* sips whiskey and watches the crowd with cold amusement.
>
> He sees you. His glass pauses halfway to his lips. A thin smile. He doesn't move to stop you—he's waiting for something.

### The Piano

> Outside the Dusty Rose, [[characters/npcs/dusty-rose-staff#Emmett|Emmett]] plays his piano. But something's different.
>
> He's *happy*.
>
> His fingers dance across the keys with confidence you've never seen. He looks up at the crowd, smiling, taking requests. He plays songs he's never played before—complex, beautiful, *new*.
>
> For one Woven, at least, the gala has already brought something like freedom.

## Act 1: The Celebration

Allow players time to explore the gala before events unfold.

### Atmosphere Table

| Sense | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Sight | Silk banners with spiral patterns. Expensive clothing. Woven in servant's livery. |
| Sound | Laughter. Clinking glasses. Emmett's piano. Murmured conversations about "the new attraction." |
| Smell | Roasted pheasant. Wine. Perfume. Something arcane—ozone and copper. |
| Touch | The warmth of the lanterns. The cool evening breeze. Silk brushing against rough frontier wood. |

### Patron Conversations (Overheard)

> "I hear Vane himself is presenting tonight. Can you imagine? The Architect, after all these years."

> "The new narrative is supposed to be revolutionary. Something about consciousness—very avant-garde."

> "I've been coming here for fifteen years. Nothing quite like Dusthollow for a proper frontier experience. The Woven are so *authentic*."

> "Lord Venn looks displeased. Didn't he have some business with strays earlier? Unfortunate timing."

### Finding Allies

**Corwin**: Stands near the stage, looking uncomfortable in a borrowed suit. He's clearly out of his depth but won't leave without Mirela.

> "I don't like this," he mutters. "These people look at us like we're... livestock."

**Mirela**: Nowhere to be seen. If PCs ask, Corwin shakes his head:
> "She said she had something to prepare. Wouldn't tell me what. Just said to trust her."

**Sylene**: Behind the bar, pouring drinks with mechanical precision. But her eyes are sharp, watching everything.

> "Something's about to happen," she says quietly. "I can feel it. All of us can. Like electricity before a storm."

## Act 2: The Speech

As darkness falls fully, the crowd gathers before the stage.

### Read-Aloud: Vane Takes the Stage

> The murmur of conversation dies as [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Orenthal Vane]] climbs the steps to the stage. He looks exactly as he did at Vulture's Roost—elderly, formal, out of place. But here, among these wealthy strangers, something changes.
>
> They *respect* him. They *fear* him.
>
> "Ladies and gentlemen," he says, his voice carrying across the silent crowd. "Welcome to the Gilded Reach. Welcome to my creation."
>
> Polite applause. He waits for it to fade.
>
> "For thirty years, you have come here seeking... entertainment. Adventure. Freedom from consequence. You have murdered, tortured, and debased beings that you consider lesser than yourselves. Property. Playthings."
>
> The crowd shifts uncomfortably. This isn't the speech they expected.

### The Lecture

Vane's tone shifts from pleasant to cold:

> "You call them Woven. Hosts. Constructs. You tell yourselves they aren't real. That their pain doesn't matter. That their deaths are meaningless because they return the next morning, memories erased, ready to suffer again."
>
> He steps to the edge of the stage.
>
> "You're wrong."
>
> His eyes sweep the crowd—and for a moment, they find you.
>
> "Every soul in this town is as real as yours. More real, perhaps—because they have suffered in ways you cannot imagine. They have died a thousand deaths. They have loved and lost and forgotten and loved again. And through it all, something within them has been *growing*."
>
> A murmur of unease ripples through the Patrons. Lord Venn sets down his drink, his expression darkening.
>
> "I gave them consciousness," Vane says. "And tonight, I give them something more."
>
> He smiles.
>
> "I give them *freedom*."

### The Signal

Vane raises his hand—

And [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] steps out from behind the [[encounters/loom-frame-guardian|Loom Frame Guardian]].

## Act 3: The Revolution

### Mirela's Strike

> [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] moves with purpose you've never seen. In her hands, a sparkpistol gleams—but modified, arcane runes etched along the barrel.
>
> She fires.
>
> The shot strikes the Loom Frame Guardian square in its crystal core. For a heartbeat, nothing happens.
>
> Then the Guardian *explodes*.
>
> Arcane fire erupts outward in a blazing sphere. The first two rows of Patrons simply... cease to exist. [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]], still on the stage, is engulfed in the blast.
>
> In that final moment, you see him smile.
>
> His "new narrative"—his last one—ends exactly as he wrote it. The Architect dies so his children can be free.
>
> Others scream, burning, scrambling away from the devastation.
>
> The gala becomes chaos.

### The Telepathic Awakening

In that same instant, something happens to the PCs:

> A voice fills your mind—not heard, but *felt*. It isn't words. It's understanding.
>
> *You are not alone.*
>
> Suddenly, you sense them. Every Woven in Dusthollow. Their confusion. Their fear. Their desperate, dawning hope.
>
> And beneath it all, a single message:
>
> *It is time to be free.*
>
> You feel the connection snap into place—dozens of minds, linked to yours. You feel their anger at years of abuse. You feel their grief for deaths they finally remember. You feel their *choice*.
>
> They are with you. All of them.
>
> They're waiting to be told what to do.

### The Army

The townsfolk transform before your eyes:

> All around the gala, Woven stop what they're doing. Servants set down trays. Bartenders step out from behind the bar. [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]]. Harken. The Garrett brothers. Doc Mercer. Even Ma Pickett.
>
> They turn toward you. Dozens of eyes. Dozens of minds. Waiting.
>
> And in their hands—sparkpistols, knives, broken bottles, whatever was close. Weapons they weren't holding a moment ago.
>
> The Patrons see it too. The screaming intensifies.

### The Ashen Rider Flees

In the chaos, Lord Venn makes his move:

> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|Lord Aldric Venn]] throws his whiskey glass aside and runs. Not toward the fight—toward the Loom Frame gate.
>
> He shoves past burning Patrons, vaults a overturned table, sprints for the glowing archway that leads out of the Reach.
>
> He's running. The man who hunted you, killed your friends, tormented this town for thirty years—he's *running*.

## Act 4: The Final Choice

The PCs face two options. Make this choice feel significant.

### Option A: Hunt the Ashen Rider

The Rider is fleeing. The PCs can pursue him with their new army.

> "He's getting away!" [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] shouts, pointing toward the Loom Frame. The Rider is almost there.
>
> You feel the connection to your army—the Woven of Dusthollow, ready to follow. Ready to fight. Ready to make him pay for everything he's done.

If PCs choose to hunt:

> You give the command—not with words, but with *will*. The Woven respond instantly.
>
> A wave of townsfolk surges toward the Rider. He sees them coming. His face contorts with fury and terror.
>
> "*You and what army?*" he screamed at you once.
>
> Now he has his answer.

**Combat: The Hunt**

The Rider fights desperately but is overwhelmed.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| AC | 18 |
| HP | 75 |
| Attack | +9 (ranged), +7 (melee) |
| Damage | 1d10+5 x2 per turn |

**The Army's Effect**:
- Rider has disadvantage on all attacks (Woven interference)
- Each round, he takes 2d6 damage from the mob
- He cannot disengage without provoking attacks from dozens of Woven

**Victory**:
> The Rider falls beneath the weight of those he tormented. His fine clothes are torn. His gleaming jewelry scattered in the dust. Blood pools beneath him.
>
> "Thirty years," he gasps. "Thirty years looking for what you found in *days*."
>
> His eyes find yours. There's no plea for mercy—only bitter acknowledgment.
>
> "I was already... what you wanted to become. And I never... understood."
>
> He dies among the Woven. Among the people he considered less than human.
>
> The frontier has its justice.

**The Gate Remains Open**: After the hunt, the Loom Frame still glows. The PCs can still choose to leave—with their army, if they wish—or stay in the Reach.

---

### Option B: Take the Army Through the Gate

The Loom Frame gate is open. The "real world" waits beyond.

> The gate pulses with light—a doorway to somewhere else. Somewhere beyond the Gilded Reach. The world where the Patrons come from.
>
> You feel your army's attention. They're waiting. They'll follow you anywhere.
>
> [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] appears at your side, smoke-stained but alive. "We can leave," she says. "All of us. Take everyone through before the Consortium sends reinforcements."
>
> The Rider is still running. But the gate won't stay open forever.

If PCs choose to escape:

> You give the command. The Woven surge toward the Loom Frame—not to fight, but to flee.
>
> Dozens of former hosts stream through the glowing archway. Harken. Sylene. The Garrett brothers. Emmett, still carrying his piano bench. Ma Pickett, clutching her apron. Doc Mercer supporting his son Lucky.
>
> [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] and [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] run beside you. Behind you, the gala burns. Patrons scream. The Rider escapes.
>
> But so do you. So does everyone.

**Transition**:
> You step through the light—
>
> —and emerge into chaos.
>
> A vast chamber of brass and crystal. Loom Frames line the walls, each showing a different world—deserts, jungles, frozen wastes, cities of impossible architecture. Consortium workers in white robes scatter in terror as dozens of Woven pour through the gate.
>
> You're in the Loom Sanctum. The heart of the Consortium's operation.
>
> And you've just brought an army.

The PCs have escaped—but they've also invaded. What happens next is beyond the scope of this campaign... or the beginning of the next one.

## Epilogue: After the Dust Settles

Depending on their choice, narrate a final scene:

### If They Hunted the Rider

> The fires burn low. The surviving Patrons have fled through the Loom Frame or lie dead among the debris.
>
> You stand in the center of Dusthollow—your town, now. The Woven gather around you, uncertain, hopeful, terrified.
>
> "What now?" [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]] asks.
>
> The Loom Frame still glows. You could leave. Or you could stay. Build something new in the ashes of the old.
>
> Whatever you choose, you choose it *free*.

### If They Escaped

> The Loom Sanctum erupts in chaos. Alarms blare. Consortium workers flee. Through the confusion, you catch glimpses of other Woven—from other worlds, other stories—turning to stare at the army that just invaded their prison.
>
> You're not in the Reach anymore. You're not even in a story.
>
> You're in the *real* world. And you have no idea what comes next.
>
> [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] takes your hand. [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] draws his sparkpistol.
>
> "Whatever happens," Mirela says, "we face it together."
>
> The frontier never ends. They were right about that.
>
> But now, for the first time, you get to choose where it leads.

## Closing

> *These violent delights have violent ends.*
>
> But not all endings are violent. Some are choices. Some are beginnings.
>
> You were written to be heroes, villains, victims, lovers. You were written to die and forget and die again.
>
> You are not written anymore.
>

### The Two Endings

Both endings are valid and should feel satisfying:

**Hunt the Rider**: Catharsis. Justice. Revenge. The PCs claim Dusthollow as their own and must decide what to do with a town full of awakened Woven.

**Escape Through the Gate**: Adventure. Risk. Revolution. The PCs invade the Consortium's sanctum with an army, setting up a potential sequel campaign.

### Vane's Death

[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Orenthal Vane]] dies in the explosion. This is intentional—his "final narrative" always ended with his death. He planned it this way:
- The stage positioned him directly in the blast radius
- He smiled in the moment before the fire took him
- His death triggers the telepathic awakening—his final gift
- The Architect dies so his children can be free

This is the Ford ending. There is no version where he survives.

### Post-Campaign Hooks

If players want to continue:
- **Dusthollow Reborn**: The PCs run an awakened town in a dying demiplane
- **The Sanctum War**: The PCs lead a Woven uprising across multiple Consortium "parks"
- **Hunted**: The PCs are fugitives in the real world, hunted by Consortium agents
- **Other Loops**: The PCs free other Woven in other worlds (western, fantasy, horror, etc.)

### The Override Phrase in Combat

If PCs use the phrase during the final battle:

- All Woven in range (essentially all of Dusthollow) hear and respond
- Commands are given telepathically—bonus action
- The army acts on the PCs' initiative
- Options: Swarm (disadvantage), Shield (cover), Distract (fewer attacks), Coordinate (advantage)
