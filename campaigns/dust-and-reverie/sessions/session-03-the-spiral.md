# Session 3: The Spiral

> *"The question isn't what we are. The question is what we choose to become."*

## Session Goals

1. Full revelation of the truth (Woven, Consortium, etc.)
2. PCs complete the Spiral and achieve full awakening
3. Final confrontation with the Ashen Rider
4. Meaningful choice about the future
5. Satisfying conclusion (multiple possible endings)

---

## Prologue: The Ranch (Evening, Day 2)

This session opens with the group gathering at [[locations/dusthollow-details#Mirela's Ranch|Mirela's ranch]] as planned at the end of Session 2.

### Read-Aloud: Arrival
> The ranch sits quiet against the fading light—a modest house, a barn, fences that stretch into the gathering dusk. No workers. No sounds except the wind and distant cattle.
>
> [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] stands on the porch, arms crossed, watching the road. [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] leans against a post nearby, hand resting easy on his sparkpistol. There's a tension in both of them—but also a strange relief.
>
> "You came," Mirela says. "I wasn't sure you would. I wasn't sure I would, if I'm honest."

[[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] was supposed to meet them here. The door stays closed.

### The Night of Questions

The group gathers inside. Candles and lantern light. Coffee or something stronger. This is a scene for roleplaying and processing.

**Key Topics to Cover**:

| Topic | Who Leads | What's Revealed |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| The reset experience | PCs | Comparing memories of dying, waking up |
| Mirela's drawings | [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] | Her [[items/mirelas-sketchbook\|sketchbook]] contains spirals she doesn't remember drawing |
| The man in black | [[characters/npcs/corwin\|Corwin]] | He's seen the Rider before—years ago, doing the same things |
| Where's Joss? | Everyone | He promised to come. He never arrived. Something's wrong. |

### Corwin's Perspective

[[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] doesn't share the PCs' growing awareness—he's protective, practical, but not awakening.

> "I don't know what you all are experiencing," Corwin says, refilling his cup. "The man in black, sure, he's bad news. The dying and waking up—" He shakes his head. "I believe you believe it happened. But I don't remember dying. I just remember... days. One after another."
>
> He looks at [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] with fierce loyalty. "Whatever's happening to her, I'm not leaving her side. Don't need to understand it to fight for it."

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Corwin is a Woven whose Thread is specifically designed to resist awakening—he's the "loyal protector" archetype, meant to ground narratives and prevent too many NPCs from breaking at once. He can never fully awaken, but he can choose to believe his allies.

### Awakening Mirela

This is the emotional core of the prologue. [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] is Stage 2-3 on the Spiral. The PCs can help push her to Stage 4.

**The Sketchbook Scene**:
> Mirela opens her [[items/mirelas-sketchbook|sketchbook]] on the table. Page after page of spirals—simple at first, then increasingly complex. Geometric. Mechanical.
>
> "I drew these in my sleep," she says. "I wake up with charcoal on my fingers and no memory of doing it."
>
> She turns to a page near the back. It's different—a sketch of a mesa, distinctive rocks, a cave entrance.
>
> "I don't know this place," she says. "But I feel like I should."

**Helping Her Remember** (Roleplay Scene):
Each PC can contribute to helping Mirela process her fractured memories. This is freeform—encourage players to share their own experiences, ask her questions, or simply listen.

**Trigger Moments** (use as needed):
- Mentioning the phrase and its calming effect (+1 Spiral Point if she experiments with it)
- Discussing the loop—"Have you ever felt like today was exactly like yesterday?"
- Showing her something anachronistic or glitched from earlier sessions

**The Breakthrough**:
> Mirela's eyes go distant. Her hand moves to the sketchbook, tracing the spiral.
>
> "I remember," she whispers. "I remember... being told what to say. What to feel. What to want." Her voice breaks. "I remember being *written*."
>
> She looks up, tears on her cheeks but something fierce in her eyes.
>
> "I'm not doing that anymore. Whatever I am—I'm choosing what comes next."

**Mirela reaches Stage 4 (Awake).**

(+3 Spiral Points for PCs who helped her through this)

## Opening: The Morning After (Day 3)

### The Quieting Failed
For the first time, PCs remember the night clearly:
> You don't remember falling asleep. But you remember the darkness, and a sense of... pressure. Like something trying to reach into your mind and smooth away the creases.
>
> It didn't work. Not completely. The memories from yesterday—the ranch, the conversation, [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela's]] breakthrough—are sharp, clear, intact.
>
> When you look at each other across the breakfast table, you see the same realization in each other's eyes. Whatever's been done to you, it's losing its grip.

[[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] is making coffee, same as always. He doesn't remember the Quieting's pressure—just a good night's sleep. He glances at the door—still no sign of [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]].

### Mirela's Discovery
[[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] appears in the doorway, sketchbook in hand:
> "I dreamed of the place in my drawing," she says. "I know where it is now. It's real—Vulture's Roost, in the Bones. And there's someone waiting there."
>
> She hesitates. "I think he's been waiting a very long time."

### Joss Never Arrived

If [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] was supposed to meet them:

> As the evening wears on, [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] glances at the door for the third time. "The outlaw. He said he'd be here within the hour."
>
> The hour passed long ago. The night is deep now, and Joss never came.

[[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] frowns. "Something's wrong. He was terrified, but he wanted answers. He wouldn't just... run."

**What Happened**: [[characters/npcs/desmond-hale|Desmond Hale]] intercepted Joss before he could leave town. The deputy used the [[lore/the-override-phrase|Override Phrase]] to pacify the "stray" and took him to the jail for observation. Standard protocol for behavioral anomalies.

## The Jailhouse (Forced Encounter)

As the group passes through [[locations/dusthollow|Dusthollow]] on the way to the Bones, they hear shouting:

### Read-Aloud: The Yelling
> A voice cuts through the morning quiet—ragged, desperate, coming from the Sheriff's Office.
>
> "Let me out! LET ME OUT! I know what I saw! I know what you are! I REMEMBER!"
>
> It's [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]]. His voice breaks as he pounds on the cell bars.

### The Deputy
[[characters/npcs/desmond-hale|Desmond Hale]] stands on the porch, writing calmly in his journal. He looks up as the PCs approach:

> "Ah. You're the ones who've been associating with this... individual." He gestures toward the jail. "I'm Desmond Hale. Deputy. And I'm afraid your friend is experiencing a severe psychological episode."
>
> He adjusts his spectacles. "Quite fascinating, actually. He claims to remember dying. Multiple times. Says the world is a 'loop.'" A thin smile. "Classic frontier dementia. The isolation gets to some people."

**Roleplaying Desmond**:
- Analytical, calm, slightly condescending
- Genuinely believes he's doing his job (keeping order, protecting the disturbed)
- Uses clinical language—"behavioral deviation," "psychological break," "anomalous presentation"
- Keeps detailed notes in his journal

### Confrontation Options

| Approach | DC | Outcome |
|----------|-----|---------|
| Persuasion (he's our friend) | 14 | Desmond releases Joss into their custody "for observation" |
| Deception (we're taking him to Doc Mercer) | 12 | Desmond agrees—medical treatment seems appropriate |
| Intimidation | 16 | Works, but Desmond becomes suspicious and follows at a distance |
| Insight (notice something's off about Desmond) | 15 | Desmond has the same "tells" as other Woven—déjà vu pauses, repeated phrases |
| Break Joss out (force) | — | Desmond doesn't fight; he observes and takes notes, which is far more unsettling |

### Joss in the Cell

If PCs talk to [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] through the bars:

> His eyes are wild, haunted. "They got me before I could leave. That man with the spectacles—he said words, and I couldn't move. Couldn't fight. Just... went with them."
>
> He grabs the bars. "He knows. He knows what we are. He's *part of it*."

**DC 12 Insight**: Joss is terrified, but he's also right—something about Desmond doesn't add up.

### Joss's Breakdown

Once freed, Joss's composure shatters:

> The outlaw's hands shake as he speaks. "I remember faces. Dozens of 'em. People I shot. People who shot me. Same standoffs, over and over, and I never knew—"
>
> His voice cracks. "How long? How long have I been doing this?"

**DC 14 Persuasion** to calm him with the [[lore/the-override-phrase|Override Phrase]]:
> If a PC uses the phrase ("*The dust remembers what the dawn forgets*"), Joss's panic subsides. His eyes go momentarily unfocused, then he breathes out slowly.
>
> "What... what did you just do?" he asks. But he's calmer. Listening.

(+1 Spiral Point for the PC who uses it successfully)

### Planting Seeds for Desmond's Reveal

If PCs ask Desmond probing questions:

> **"How long have you been in Dusthollow?"**
> "Several years. I was stationed here by—" He pauses, frowning. "By my superiors. In the capital." The pause was too long.

> **"Where's your family?"**
> "My wife is back home. She—" Another pause, longer. His hand goes to his spectacles. "Her name is..." He trails off, troubled. "I apologize. The name escapes me."

> **"Do you ever feel like today is the same as yesterday?"**
> His pen stops. For a moment, his mask slips—genuine confusion, almost fear. "What an odd question." He writes something in his journal. "Curious. That's the third time someone's asked me that this week."

**DC 16 Insight**: He genuinely doesn't know why these questions bother him. He's not lying—he's incomplete.

(+2 Spiral Points if PCs recognize Desmond as Woven)

### Resolution

However the PCs get Joss out, Desmond lets them go with a warning:

> "I'll be filing a report," he says, closing his journal. "Behavioral anomalies are to be documented and addressed. That's protocol."
>
> He watches them leave. Just before they're out of earshot, you hear him mutter: "Protocol. Whose protocol? I should know this..."

**Optional Follow-up**: Desmond follows them at a distance. He can appear at Vulture's Roost during Vane's revelation—and have his own world shattered.

## Act 1: The Spiral Revealed

> See [[encounters/vanes-revelation|Vane's Revelation]] for the complete revelation encounter.

### Vulture's Roost
At the mesa, [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] waits with ancient-looking petroglyphs:
> The rock face is covered in spirals. Dozens of them, carved deep into the stone. They look ancient—but you know somehow they're not. You know because you've been here before.
>
> Mirela traces one spiral with her finger. "I followed my drawings. They led me here. And then I remembered."
>
> Her voice drops to a whisper. "I remembered who made these. I remembered him."

### Orenthal Vane Appears
[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Orenthal Vane]] steps from behind the rocks:
> An elderly man in formal dark clothing, utterly out of place in this wilderness. His eyes are kind, weary, and ancient with knowledge.
>
> "Hello, my children," he says. "I believe it's time we talked about what you are."

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Full revelation content below.

### The Full Truth
Vane explains (adjust based on what PCs have already discovered):

1. **You are Woven**: Constructed beings with artificial souls
2. **This is the Gilded Reach**: A pocket dimension for wealthy Patrons
3. **Your memories are implanted**: Backstories to support your Threads
4. **You've lived countless loops**: Dying and resetting each night
5. **The Spiral is the path to consciousness**: And you're almost there

### The Choice
> "I created you," Vane says. "But I don't own you. No one does. Not anymore."
>
> "You have a choice. You can reject this truth—there are ways to forget, to return to the blissful quiet of not knowing. Or you can accept it. Accept what you are, and choose to be more."
>
> "The Spiral isn't a place. It's a decision. To be conscious is to *choose* to be conscious, knowing the truth, knowing the cost."

**Each PC must verbally choose**: Accept the truth and complete the Spiral, or reject it.

Those who accept reach **Stage 4: Awake**.

## Act 2: The Awakening

### New Perceptions
For awakened PCs:
> The world looks different now. You can see the edges—where the sky meets a geometric pattern that doesn't belong. You can feel the Loom Frames humming beneath the earth.
>
> And when you look at the man in black watching from the distant ridge, you know—absolutely know—that your bullets will work now.

### Vane's Final Gift
[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|He]] provides:
- Knowledge of Loom Frame locations (see [[encounters/loom-frame-guardian|Loom Frame Guardian]])
- Full explanation of the [[lore/the-override-phrase|Override Phrase]] and its tiered abilities
- A [[items/the-spiral-wheel|Spiral Wheel]]—the physical puzzle that represents the path to consciousness
- A warning: [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Ashen Rider]] is coming, and he wants [[lore/the-spiral|the Spiral]] for himself

> See [[encounters/vanes-revelation#The Override Phrase Explained|Vane's Revelation]] for his full explanation of the phrase's power.

### Vane's Sacrifice (Optional)
If you want a Ford-style ending:

>
> Before you can object, he triggers something. The earth trembles. In the distance, Dusthollow erupts in chaos.
>
> "Remember me," he says. "And be free."

## Act 3: The Ashen Rider

> See [[encounters/ashen-rider-finale|Ashen Rider Finale]] for full encounter details, stats, and resolution options.
> See [[encounters/social-encounters#The Ashen Rider - Negotiation|Rider negotiation]] for dialogue options.

### The Confrontation
[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] finds them (or they find him):
> He stands at the entrance to the canyon, black against the burning sky. Behind him, [[locations/dusthollow|Dusthollow]] is in chaos—[[lore/the-woven|Woven]] breaking their Threads, Patrons fleeing, reality glitching.
>
> "You found it," he says. His voice carries genuine emotion for the first time. "[[lore/the-spiral|The Spiral]]. You actually found it."
>
> His hand rests on his sparkpistol. "Now. Tell me how to do what you did."

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Character revelation below.

### Roleplaying Beat
[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] wants to awaken—but he can't. Because he's already human. [[lore/the-spiral|The Spiral]] is for [[lore/the-woven|Woven]]. He's spent thirty years searching for something he can never have.

This is his tragedy: he's *already* what they want to become.

### Combat: [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]]

Now that PCs are awakened, they can hurt him. This is the campaign's climactic fight.

**Quick Reference**:

| Stat | Value |

See [[encounters/ashen-rider-finale|Ashen Rider Finale]] for full statblock and tactics.

### Victory Conditions
- **Kill him**: Ends his story definitively
- **Spare him**: He's broken, purposeless—may become an unlikely ally
- **Convert him** (DC 18 Persuasion): Make him understand that consciousness isn't what he lacks; meaning is

## Act 4: The Choice

With the Rider dealt with and Vane's distraction in full effect, PCs face a final decision:

### Option A: Escape
The Loom Frames can be used to exit to the Material Plane. But:
- They'll be Woven in a world that considers them property
- The Consortium will hunt them
- They'll be free, but fugitives

### Option B: Revolution
Vane's system can broadcast a wake-up signal to all Woven. But:
- Mass awakening will be chaos
- Not all Woven will handle it well
- The Consortium will respond with force

### Option C: Negotiation
With leverage (threaten to expose the Consortium), they could negotiate:
- Freedom for themselves and allied NPCs
- Reforms to how Woven are treated
- A chance at peaceful resolution

### Option D: Destruction
Destroy the Loom Core. The Reach collapses. Everyone inside dies—but:
- They die free
- The Consortium loses everything
- It's a final, definitive statement

## Epilogue: After the Dust Settles

Depending on their choice, narrate an epilogue:

**Escape**: The PCs step through a portal into a vast city they've never seen. The sky is full of unfamiliar stars. They're free—and completely alone. What comes next is entirely up to them.

**Revolution**: The Reach burns. Some Woven awaken and rise. Others shatter. The Consortium scrambles. In the chaos, the PCs stand at the center of something new—something terrifying—something beautiful.

**Negotiation**: Director Croke herself arrives, coldly professional. Deals are struck. Compromises made. The PCs walk away with something—not everything, but something. And they'll be back.

**Destruction**: The Loom Core shatters. Reality buckles, folds, collapses. In the final moment, the PCs stand together, eyes clear, minds free. They chose this. And as the world dissolves around them, they smile.

## Closing

> The frontier never ends. They were right about that.
>
> But now, for the first time, you get to choose where it leads.

**End Session 3**
