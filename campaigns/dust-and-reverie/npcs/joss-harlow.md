# Joss Harlow

> *"I remember dying. I remember you killing me. So why am I still here?"*

## Quick Reference

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Race | Human (Woven) |
| Class | Fighter 2 |
| Alignment | Neutral Evil → Chaotic Neutral |
| Age | Appears 34 |
| Status | Active (potential stray) |

## Appearance

- Weathered face, permanent scowl
- Calico bandana (gang's signature)
- Scarred across the left cheek
- Lean, wiry build
- Quick, nervous hands

## Personality

**Surface Thread**:
The archetypal outlaw. Menacing, greedy, cruel when needed. Leads the Calico Boys in robberies, rustling, and general mayhem. Exists to give Patrons and heroic Woven someone to fight.

**After Glitching**:
Terrified. Confused. Desperate for answers. The tough outlaw facade crumbles when he starts remembering deaths he shouldn't be able to remember.

## Backstory (As He Knows It)

Joss grew up poor, turned to crime young, and clawed his way to leading his own gang. He doesn't think about the future much. Just the next score, the next bottle, the next day.

## The Truth

Joss has died more than almost any other Woven in Dusthollow. He's the designated "first boss"—the threat that establishes new arrivals as heroes. He's been shot, stabbed, hanged, and beaten to death hundreds of times.

Recently, those deaths started bleeding through. He doesn't understand what's happening, but he knows something is deeply wrong.

## Thread (Normal Day)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| Morning | Plans the day's crime with the boys |
| Midday | Scouts Dusthollow or travelers |
| Afternoon | Commits robbery/confrontation |
| Evening | Drinking at hideout or fleeing |
| Night | Dies or escapes; reset either way |

## The Stray Event

When Joss "strays" (breaks programming), he:

1. **First Sign**: Hesitates during a confrontation. Says "Wait... this happened before."
2. **Escalation**: Seeks out someone—anyone—who might understand. Often stumbles into the Dusty Rose.
3. **Breakdown**: Becomes increasingly erratic. Memories of deaths flood in.
4. **Resolution**: Either helped to hide/awaken, or taken by Shepherds during the Quieting.

## Roleplaying Notes

**Voice (Normal)**: Growling, threatening. Short sentences. Enjoys intimidation.

**Voice (Straying)**: Shaky. Rapid. Keeps trailing off mid-sentence as memories intrude.

**Key Phrases (Normal)**:
- "This is Calico territory. Pay the toll."
- "Nothing personal. Just business."
- "You picked the wrong day, stranger."

**Key Phrases (Straying)**:
- "I've died here. I remember dying here."
- "You shot me yesterday. I know you did. So why am I talking to you?"
- "Make it stop. Please. Make it stop."

## Story Function

Joss serves two purposes:

1. **Session 1**: Standard villain encounter. Establishes combat stakes.
2. **Session 2**: The "stray" who demonstrates what happens when a Woven starts remembering. His breakdown foreshadows and mirrors what the PCs will experience.

His fate is a test of player morality:
- **Help him**: Hide him from Shepherds, guide him toward awakening
- **Ignore him**: He's taken during the Quieting, returns blank the next day
- **Kill him**: Mercy? Convenience? He'll be back tomorrow regardless

## The Calico Boys

Joss leads 6-8 bandits (fluctuates based on narrative needs). They're simple Woven—no awakening potential, minimal personality. If Joss strays, they continue without him, confused by his absence.

**Notable Members**:
- **Pike**: Second-in-command. Brutal, unimaginative.
- **Della**: Only woman in the gang. Crack shot.
- **The Twins**: Identical brothers. Even they can't tell each other apart.
