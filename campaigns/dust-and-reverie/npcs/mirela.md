# Mirela Holten

> *"I've been having these dreams lately. Dreams where I'm someone else. Someone who knows things I shouldn't know."*

## Quick Reference

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Race | Human |
| Class | Commoner (awakening to Fighter) |

## Appearance

- Fair skin with a light dusting of freckles
- Golden-brown hair often in a practical braid
- Blue eyes that seem to see more than they should
- Simple but well-maintained ranch clothes
- Carries a worn leather satchel

## Personality

**Surface Thread (Initial)**:
Sweet, optimistic, innocent. Believes in the goodness of people. Loves her father, her ranch, her simple life. Rides into Dusthollow each morning for supplies. Gets caught up in whatever adventure the day brings. Forgets by morning.

**Awakening**:
As she progresses, Mirela becomes:
- More perceptive (notices the wrongness)
- Haunted (fragments of past loops intrude)
- Determined (refuses to accept the lie)
- Dangerous (discovers she knows how to fight—many loops of violence)

## Backstory (As She Knows It)

Mirela lives on a small ranch outside Dusthollow with her father, Holt. Her mother died when she was young. She dreams of seeing the world beyond the frontier, but duty keeps her close to home. She's kind to strangers and quick to help those in need.

## The Truth

Mirela is one of the oldest Woven in the Reach—she's been running for decades of real-time. She's died thousands of times, loved and lost countless Patrons who treated her as entertainment, and been reset each night.

Orenthal Vane specifically modified her to begin awakening. She's the centerpiece of his "new narrative"—the first domino meant to trigger the revolution.

## Glitches & Awakening Signs

**Early Signs** (players may witness):
- Pauses mid-sentence, eyes unfocused
- Says "I've been here before" about new situations
- Knows things she shouldn't (PC names before introduction)
- Whispers fragments: "These violent delights have violent ends"

**Progressive Awakening**:
- Remembers past deaths ("I've died here before")
- Recognizes Patrons for what they are
- Gains combat abilities (Fighter levels) from loop memories
- Eventually can resist the Quieting

## Relationships

- **Holt** (father): Loves him, but he's a simple Woven without awakening potential. She may have to leave him behind.
- **[[npcs/corwin]]**: Her designated love interest. Genuinely cares for him, but realizes their love is programmed.
- **[[npcs/sylene]]**: A fellow awakening Woven. They recognize something in each other.
- **[[npcs/the-ashen-rider]]**: Hunts her across loops. She's central to what he's searching for.

## Roleplaying Notes

**Voice**: Soft but clear. Becomes more intense as she awakens.

**Mannerisms**:
- Tilts her head when listening
- Looks at the horizon often
- When glitching, her hands tremble

**Key Phrases**:
- "Some folks say the frontier never ends."
- "I feel like I've known you forever."
- "Something's wrong. I don't know what, but... something's wrong."

## Story Function

Mirela is the player's primary ally and guide through the awakening process. She's often one step ahead of them on the Spiral, providing clues and emotional resonance. Her fate—whether she fully awakens, whether she survives—is a key campaign outcome.
