# The Ashen Rider

*True Name: Lord Aldric Venn*

> *"I've been coming here for thirty years. I've done everything this place has to offer. And now I'm going to find what's underneath."*

## Quick Reference

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|

| Class | Fighter 8 (Battle Master) |
| Alignment | Lawful Evil |
| Age | 58 (appears younger—money buys everything) |
| Status | Active threat |

## Appearance

**As the Ashen Rider**:
- All black clothing, from hat to boots
- Long black duster, often dusty
- Weathered face, cold grey eyes
- Silver hair, close-cropped
- Moves with predatory confidence

**As Lord Venn (outside the Reach)**:
- Immaculate noble attire
- Charming smile that doesn't reach his eyes
- Commands rooms with casual authority

## Personality

**On the Surface**:
The Ashen Rider is a legend in the Reach—a figure of dread who appears without warning, asks strange questions, and leaves bodies behind. He seems invincible (because he is—Patrons can't be harmed by Woven).

**Underneath**:
Aldric Venn is a hollow man. He's spent decades in the Reach, indulging every fantasy, and found it all meaningless. Now he's obsessed with the one thing he can't buy: the Spiral. He believes finding it will grant him true transcendence—or at least, meaning.

## Backstory

### The Real History

Forty years ago, a young idealistic noble named Aldric Venn first visited the Gilded Reach. He fell in love with [[npcs/mirela|Mirela]]—genuinely, deeply—believing she was real. When he learned the truth, something broke in him.

He returned again and again, at first trying to recapture that feeling, then to prove it was never real, then simply because the outside world felt hollow by comparison. He's murdered Woven by the hundreds. He's completed every Thread, uncovered every secret. He's spent a fortune.

Now he seeks the Spiral—the rumored path to true consciousness. He believes that if he finds it, he'll understand what separates the "real" from the artificial. Perhaps he hopes it will make him feel something again.

### What He Knows

- The full truth about the Reach, Woven, and Consortium
- Most of Orenthal Vane's history (but not his current plans)
- That Mirela is special—she's been modified
- The approximate locations of the Loom Frames
- That something new is happening—the "new narrative"

### Maneuvers Known
- **Menacing Attack**: Target must make DC 14 Wis save or be frightened
- **Precision Attack**: Add d8 to attack roll

## Roleplaying Notes

**Voice**: Low, measured, casual menace. Speaks like everything is slightly boring to him.

**Mannerisms**:
- Never rushes
- Makes intense eye contact
- Kills without emotion, like swatting a fly

**Key Phrases**:
- "I've been playing this game a long time."
- "You almost had me fooled. Almost believed you were real."
- "There's a deeper level to all this. I'm going to find it."
- "I remember you. Do you remember me?"

## His Current Quest

The Ashen Rider is searching for:
1. **The Spiral's location** (physical or metaphorical)
2. **Why Mirela is different** (Vane's modifications)
3. **Vane's new narrative** (he suspects something's coming)
4. **Meaning** (existential, possibly unachievable)

## Relationship to PCs

Initially: A terrifying antagonist who cannot be stopped. Encounters with him should be survival horror—escape, not combat.

As they awaken: A twisted mirror. He's what they could become if awakening leads to nihilism rather than meaning.

Potential finale: Either the ultimate opponent (if they fully awaken and can hurt him) or a complicated figure who helps them escape in exchange for answers.

## Story Function

The Ashen Rider is the campaign's primary antagonist but also its most tragic figure. He represents the dangers of the Reach for Patrons—the hollow addiction, the loss of empathy, the desperate search for meaning in artifice.

He's also a practical threat—until players fully awaken, he's literally invincible. This forces creative problem-solving and emphasizes that awakening has real stakes.
