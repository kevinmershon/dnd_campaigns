# Desmond Hale

> *"I've dedicated my life to understanding how people think. Turns out, I didn't understand myself at all."*

## Quick Reference

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Race | Human (Woven) |
| Class | Artificer 4 |
| Alignment | Lawful Neutral |
| Age | Appears 45 |
| Status | Woven (doesn't know) |

## Appearance

- Thoughtful expression, often slightly furrowed brow
- Brown skin, salt-and-pepper hair cut short
- Wire-rimmed spectacles (aesthetic, the Reach provides perfect vision)
- Practical clothing—vest, rolled sleeves
- Carries a journal and toolkit

## Personality

**Surface Presentation**:
Desmond is the town's deputy and informal problem-solver. He's analytical, calm under pressure, and genuinely wants to help people. He asks probing questions and notices things others miss.

**Hidden Truth**:
Desmond is a Woven who believes he's a Patron. He thinks he works for the Consortium as an in-field observer, monitoring the Reach and making recommendations. His "vacations back home" are actually maintenance and memory updates in the Loom Sanctum.

He's been Vane's closest collaborator for years—helping design narratives, improve Woven psychology, even studying the Spiral. He has no idea he's a subject of the very research he's been conducting.

## Backstory (As He Knows It)

Desmond is a scholar from the capital who joined the Consortium years ago to study consciousness and behavior. He was stationed in the Reach as an observer, living as a deputy to blend in. He returns periodically to file reports and visit his wife (implanted memory—there is no wife).

## The Truth

Desmond was created by Orenthal Vane as an experiment: Could a Woven function as a Consortium employee? Could they observe themselves?

Vane made Desmond as close to himself as possible—intelligent, curious, analytical. They've had thousands of conversations about consciousness and the Spiral. Desmond has helped design the very modifications that might allow Woven to awaken.

He doesn't know any of this.

## The Bernard Reveal

When Desmond learns the truth (which should be a major campaign moment), it should be devastating:

1. Evidence accumulates (he doesn't remember arriving, his "wife" doesn't exist, etc.)
2. Confrontation with proof (maintenance records, seeing himself in the workshop)
3. Psychological crisis (everything he believed is false)
4. Choice: break down, or accept and move forward

## Narrative Role

| Situation | Desmond's Function |

| Investigation | Offers analytical help, asks probing questions |
| Violence | Competent but not dominant, provides support |
| Mystery | Provides clues he doesn't know the significance of |
| Emotional | Stable presence, struggles visibly as truth approaches |

## Roleplaying Notes

**Voice**: Precise, academic, occasionally lost in thought. Softens when speaking about ideas he finds fascinating.

**Mannerisms**:
- Cleans his spectacles when thinking
- Takes notes in his journal
- Asks "why" more than "what"

**Key Phrases**:
- "There's always a pattern. We just have to find it."
- "Curious. That doesn't match the behavioral model at all."
- "I've studied this for years. I should understand it better than I do."
- "Something doesn't add up. Something about... me."

## Foreshadowing the Reveal

Subtle clues that Desmond is Woven:
- He can't remember specific details of his "wife" or "home"
- He knows things about Dusthollow he shouldn't (Woven knowledge)
- He has the same tells as other Woven (déjà vu, repeated phrases)
- He's been in the Reach longer than any Patron should be

## Story Function

Desmond is the campaign's secondary revelation—if the players are Woven, even the "staff" might be too. His discovery mirrors the players' own journey and raises questions about identity and consciousness.

He can become:
- A powerful ally (if he processes the truth and joins the awakening)
- A threat (if he breaks down and alerts the Consortium)
- A tragic sacrifice (if the truth destroys him)

His fate should be in the players' hands.
