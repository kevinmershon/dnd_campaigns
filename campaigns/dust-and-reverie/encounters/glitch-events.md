## Running Glitches Effectively

### Timing

**Early Sessions**: Subtle, easily dismissed. PCs should question whether they imagined it.

**Mid Sessions**: More frequent, harder to ignore. NPCs start to seem "off."

**Late Sessions**: Reality is clearly broken. The question isn't "is this real?" but "what IS real?"

### Player Responses

**If Players Dismiss It**: That's fine. The glitches accumulate. Eventually they can't be ignored.

**If Players Investigate**: Reward them with information:
- DC 12-14: Notice the pattern exists
- DC 15-17: Understand the pattern repeats
- DC 18+: Begin to perceive the nature of the loop

**If Players Confront NPCs**: Most NPCs rationalize or forget:
> "What? No, I just said good morning. Same as always. You feeling okay, friend?"

Some NPCs (especially [[characters/npcs/sylene]], [[characters/npcs/mirela]], [[characters/npcs/dusty-rose-staff#Pearl|Pearl]]) may react differently—with recognition, or fear.

### Stacking Glitches

The more PCs notice, the more they see. Once awakened to patterns, they can't unsee them.

## DM Notes

### Purpose

Glitches serve multiple functions:
1. **Atmosphere**: The world is *wrong*
2. **Foreshadowing**: Something deeper is happening
3. **Player Agency**: Reward observation and curiosity
4. **Pacing**: Control revelation speed

### Don't Overuse

A glitch every few minutes becomes noise. Space them out. Let tension build between moments of wrongness.

### Player Spiral Points

Track these privately or openly (your choice). Players knowing their points can create metagaming, but it can also create anticipation.

### When Glitches Stop Mattering

By Session 3, glitches become background noise—the PCs know reality is broken. Shift focus from "noticing wrongness" to "acting on knowledge."
