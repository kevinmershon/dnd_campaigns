# The Stray: Joss Harlow's Breakdown

> *"I remember dying. Yesterday. You shot me, and I remember dying, and I woke up this morning in my bed and I* remember.*"*

## Setup

[[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss Harlow]]—the Calico Boys leader from [[sessions/session-01-arrival|Session 1]]—has broken his Thread. He remembers dying (or almost dying) at the PCs' hands. His programming can't reconcile the contradiction: he should have reset, should have forgotten, but he didn't.

He's become a **stray**—a [[lore/the-woven|Woven]] whose glitch is too severe to suppress.

### When It Happens

This encounter triggers:
- Morning of Session 2 (he's been tormented all night)
- After PCs notice the looping patterns
- Before or during [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Ashen Rider's]] arrival

## The Encounter

### Read-Aloud

> The doors of [[locations/dusthollow|the Dusty Rose]] bang open. The outlaw from yesterday—[[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss Harlow]], the Calico Boys' leader—stumbles in. His eyes are wild, red-rimmed. His hands shake.
>
> He grabs a bottle from behind the bar, ignoring [[characters/npcs/dusty-rose-staff#Silas|Silas's]] protest. He takes a long drink, whiskey spilling down his unshaved chin.
>
> Then he sees you.
>
> "You," he says, pointing with a trembling finger. "You shot me. Yesterday. Right here." He taps his chest. "I felt the bullet. I felt myself dying. And then I woke up this morning in my bed and I *remember*."
>
> He looks around the room with the expression of a man who no longer trusts reality.
>
> "What's happening to me?"

## Roleplaying [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]]

### His State of Mind

- **Terrified**: The world doesn't make sense anymore
- **Desperate**: He needs someone to explain
- **Fragile**: Could break further at any moment
- **Dangerous**: A cornered animal can bite

### Key Dialogue

- "I've been doing this my whole life. Riding with the boys, running protection, the usual. But now I'm looking at it like... like I'm watching myself do it. Like it's not *me*."

- "I remember other things too. Things that shouldn't be memories. I remember dying before. Different ways. Different days. How is that possible?"

- "The man in black—[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]]—he asked me questions. About patterns. About spirals. I didn't know what he meant. But now..." *He draws a spiral in spilled whiskey.* "Now I keep seeing this shape everywhere."

- "What am I? *What am I?*"

## PC Options

### Option 1: Help Him

**Approach**: Calm him, explain what little they know, offer protection.

**Mechanics**: DC 13 Persuasion to calm him, DC 15 to build trust.

**Result**: [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] becomes an ally—fragile but grateful. He may:
- Share information about [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider's]] questions
- Reveal locations where he's seen strange things
- Help hide from [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] "Shepherds"

**Consequence**: Shepherds will come for him that night. PCs can try to hide him (DC 15 group Stealth) or fight the Shepherds.

### Option 2: Let Him Be

**Approach**: Don't engage, observe from a distance.

**Result**: [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] grows more agitated as the day progresses. By evening:

> He's in the corner, muttering to himself, drawing spirals on any surface he can find. [[characters/npcs/sylene\|Sylene]] watches with an expression of recognition—and fear.
>
> "He's seen too much," she murmurs. "They'll come for him tonight."

**Consequence**: The Shepherds take him. He returns the next day, wiped clean, running his normal Thread. He doesn't recognize the PCs beyond his programmed hostility.

### Option 3: Kill Him

**Approach**: Mercy killing—or pragmatism.

**Mechanics**: He won't fight back if approached kindly. "Maybe that's best," he whispers.

**Result**: He dies. He resets the next morning, wiped clean, no memory of his breakdown.

**Consequence**:
- **Spiral Points**: -1 (regression from truth) OR +2 (if done with understanding and compassion, recognizing the cycle)
- Moral weight for PCs to carry
- He's fine tomorrow. That might be the cruelest part.

### Option 4: Help Him Awaken

**Approach**: Guide him through the Spiral.

**Mechanics**: This is very difficult. Requires:
- DC 17 Insight to understand his mental state
- DC 18 Persuasion to guide him toward acceptance
- Time (at least an hour of roleplay)
- Knowledge PCs may not yet have

**Result** (if successful): [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] reaches Stage 1 or 2. He's still fragile, but conscious. He may become a permanent ally.

**Result** (if failed): He breaks completely. Catatonic. The Shepherds take him.

## The Shepherds

If [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] isn't hidden or awakened, Shepherds arrive at midnight.

### What Are Shepherds?

[[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] maintenance units. They appear as ordinary townsfolk who suddenly move with too-perfect coordination. Their job: retrieve strays for "maintenance."

### Read-Aloud

> Midnight. The town is silent. And then you hear footsteps—multiple sets, moving in perfect synchronization.
>
> Three figures walk down Main Street. They look like ordinary townspeople—the blacksmith [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Cade|Cade]], the general store owner [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Harken|Harken]], a ranch hand you don't recognize. But they move wrong. Every step exactly the same length. Every arm swing exactly the same angle.
>
> They stop in front of the saloon.
>
> "MAINTENANCE REQUIRED," [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Cade|Cade]] says, but his voice isn't his voice. "STRAY UNIT DESIGNATED 'JOSS HARLOW' WILL ACCOMPANY US."

### If PCs Fight

**Shepherds** (3): Use **Thug** statblock with advantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. Non-lethal damage only—they want to retrieve, not destroy.

**Consequence**: Defeating them buys time, but more will come. The [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] takes its maintenance seriously.

### If Joss Goes With Them

> [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] looks at you one last time. There's something in his eyes—gratitude, maybe, or resignation.
>
> "I guess I'll see you tomorrow," he says. "And I won't remember any of this."
>
> He walks out. The Shepherds close around him. They walk into the darkness, and by morning, [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss Harlow]] will be back to threatening shopkeepers, with no memory of ever being anything else.

## Aftermath

### The Next Morning (If Wiped)

[[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] is back. Running his Thread. Threatening [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Harken|Harken]]. He doesn't recognize the PCs as anything but strangers.

**Spiral Points**: +2 for witnessing the cycle

### The Next Morning (If Awakened)

[[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] is shaky but present. He's an ally now—and a liability. The [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] knows something went wrong. They'll investigate.

### [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene's]] Reaction

She watched everything.

> "It happens sometimes," she says quietly. "Someone sees too much, remembers too much. They come for them. They always come."
>
> She meets your eyes. "I've been careful. Very careful. But I've been where he was. I chose to look away from what I was seeing. He couldn't."
>
> A pause. "You're seeing it too, aren't you?"

## DM Notes

### Purpose
This encounter humanizes the [[lore/the-woven|Woven]] condition. [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] was an enemy—now he's a victim. His fate shows what awaits anyone who wakes up wrong.

### Tone
Horror, but also tragedy. [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] isn't a monster. He's a person who stumbled onto a truth too big for him. What the PCs do matters.

### Connection to Main Story
- Demonstrates the Quieting's failure points
- Introduces Shepherds as a threat
- [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene's]] reaction hints at her own awakening
- Foreshadows what PCs themselves are becoming

### Player Questions
PCs may ask questions [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] can't answer:
- "What is a stray?" → He doesn't know the word
- "What are you?" → "I don't know. That's what I'm asking YOU."
- "Have you seen other strange things?" → Yes—[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]], the patterns, the feeling of déjà vu
