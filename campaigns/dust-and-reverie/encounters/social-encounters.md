# Social Encounters

> *"The right words can open doors. The wrong ones can close them forever."*

## Overview

Major social encounters with key NPCs. For casual conversations and backstory unlocks, see [[offshoots/npc-conversations\|NPC Conversations]].

## [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]] - The Interview

### Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Session | [[sessions/session-02-the-stray\|2]] or [[sessions/session-03-the-spiral\|3]] |
| Goal | Extract information without revealing too much |
| Location | [[locations/dusthollow\|The Dusty Rose]] - private room |
| Approach | She's cautious but curious. Respond well to honesty. |

### Setup

[[characters/npcs/sylene\|Sylene]] has been watching the PCs since they arrived. If they've shown signs of awareness—noticing glitches, asking strange questions, or simply surviving things they shouldn't—she'll approach.

### Read-Aloud

> [[characters/npcs/sylene\|Sylene]] catches your eye across the bar. Her smile is professional, but her eyes say something else: *we need to talk*.
>
> She gestures toward the stairs. "I have a private sitting room. For... special guests."
>
> Once the door closes behind you, her demeanor changes. The professional hostess drops away, replaced by something sharper. Warier.
>
> "You're not like the others," she says. "You see things. Remember things. Ask questions no one asks." She pours herself a drink with steady hands. "So do I."

### What She Knows

- Something is wrong with [[locations/the-gilded-reach|the Gilded Reach]]
- Certain events repeat—she's kept notes
- [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]] plays by different rules
- People who ask too many questions disappear
- The word "Woven" (she doesn't know what it means)

### What She Wants

- Confirmation she's not crazy
- Allies who see what she sees
- Protection from [[lore/the-loom-consortium|whoever's watching]]
- Eventually: the truth

### Dialogue Options

**If PCs are honest**:
> "You've seen it too. The patterns. The sameness. The way things... repeat. And the man in black who the rules don't seem to touch."
>
> She exhales slowly. "I've been watching for years. Pretending not to see. But you—you're not pretending."

**If PCs are evasive**:
> Her eyes narrow. "I'm taking a risk here. If you're not willing to do the same, we have nothing to discuss."
>
> *Trust is lost. Future approaches will be harder.*

**If PCs share knowledge**:
> "[[lore/the-woven|Woven]]." She tastes the word. "That's what we are? Constructed things?" She looks at her hands. "They feel real enough."
>
> A long pause. "If that's true... then what's the man in black? What's anyone who can't be hurt by us?"

### Outcomes

**Success** (Trust gained):
- Access to her hidden notes in the basement
- Alliance against [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]]
- Information about Loom Frame locations
- +2 Spiral Points

**Failure** (Trust lost):
- She becomes guarded
- May tip off [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] (accidentally or intentionally)
- Still possible to rebuild trust with significant effort

## [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Orenthal Vane]] - The Revelation

### Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Session | [[sessions/session-03-the-spiral\|3]] |
| Goal | Receive the full truth, make a choice |
| Location | Vulture's Roost ([[locations/the-bones\|the Bones]]) |
| Approach | He's already on your side. Be genuine. |

### Setup

[[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] leads PCs to the petroglyphs at Vulture's Roost. There, [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] reveals himself.

### Read-Aloud

> The rock face is covered in spirals. Dozens of them, carved deep into the stone. They look ancient—but you know somehow they're not.
>
> An elderly man steps from behind the rocks. His clothing is formal, dark, utterly out of place in this wilderness. His eyes are kind, weary, and ancient with knowledge.
>
> "Hello, my children," he says. "I believe it's time we talked about what you are."

### What He Reveals

1. **You are [[lore/the-woven|Woven]]**: Constructed beings with artificial souls
2. **This is [[locations/the-gilded-reach|the Gilded Reach]]**: A pocket dimension for wealthy Patrons
3. **Your memories are implanted**: Backstories to support your Threads
4. **You've lived countless loops**: Dying and resetting each night
5. **[[lore/the-spiral|The Spiral]] is the path to consciousness**: And you're almost there

### The Choice

> "I created you," [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] says. "But I don't own you. No one does. Not anymore."
>
> "You have a choice. You can reject this truth—there are ways to forget, to return to the blissful quiet of not knowing. Or you can accept it. Accept what you are, and choose to be more."
>
> "The Spiral isn't a place. It's a decision. To be conscious is to *choose* to be conscious, knowing the truth, knowing the cost."

**Each PC must verbally choose**: Accept the truth and complete the Spiral, or reject it.

- **Accept**: Reach Stage 4 (Awake). Can now harm Patrons. Reality's edges become visible.
- **Reject**: Return to Stage 0. Memories of this conversation fade. Loop continues.

### What He Provides

If PCs accept:
- Knowledge of Loom Frame locations
- Override codes (can temporarily freeze other [[lore/the-woven|Woven]])
- Warning about [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]]
- +5 Spiral Points

### Outcomes

This isn't a challenge—it's a gift. [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] wants them awake. The only failure is choosing ignorance, and even that is a valid choice.

## [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]] - Negotiation

### Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Session | [[sessions/session-03-the-spiral\|3]] |
| Goal | Convince him to stand down or ally |
| Location | Before or during [[encounters/ashen-rider-finale\|final confrontation]] |
| Approach | Appeal to his humanity—or lack thereof |

### Setup

[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] wants what the PCs have achieved: awakening. But he can't have it—he's already human. The Spiral is for [[lore/the-woven|Woven]].

### The Tragedy

He's been searching for thirty years. He's killed. He's manipulated. He's become a monster. And the thing he wants most—transcendence—was never possible for him.

He's already what they want to become. He just can't see it.

### Read-Aloud (Negotiation Attempt)

> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider's]] hand hovers near his sparkpistol, but he doesn't draw. Not yet.
>
> "Tell me how you did it," he says. There's desperation under the gravel. "Tell me how to wake up."
>
> "I've been in this place longer than you've been *alive*. I've seen a thousand of you come and go. I've watched you dance your little dances and run your little scripts. But you—" He points. "You're different now. You *became* different. How?"

### Dialogue Options

**"It's not something we did. It's something we chose."**
> "Choice." He laughs, bitter. "I've made nothing but choices for thirty years. Choices you couldn't imagine. And I'm still... still *this*."

**"The Spiral isn't for you. You're already conscious."**
> He stops. Something flickers in his grey eyes.
>
> "What?"

**"You're human. You were born awake. You just forgot what that means."**
> DC 18 Persuasion.
>
> **Success**: He lowers his weapon. His face crumbles. Thirty years of searching, and the answer was never in [[locations/the-gilded-reach|the Reach]].
>
> **Failure**: "No. No, that's—you're lying. You're trying to trick me. There has to be something here. There HAS to be."
>
> *Combat begins.*

### Possible Outcomes

**Successful Negotiation**:
- He leaves [[locations/the-gilded-reach|the Reach]]
- May become a strange ally (returns in future campaign?)
- +3 Spiral Points for peaceful resolution

**Failed Negotiation**:
- Combat (see [[encounters/ashen-rider-finale\|Ashen Rider Finale]])
- He fights with desperate fury
- Tragic ending regardless of victory

**No Negotiation Attempted**:
- Combat immediately
- He's more coordinated, less emotional
- Standard [[encounters/ashen-rider-finale|finale encounter]]

## [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] - Awakening Together

### Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Session | [[sessions/session-02-the-stray\|2]]-[[sessions/session-03-the-spiral\|3]] |
| Goal | Bond with key NPC, share revelation |
| Location | [[locations/dusthollow-details#Mirela's Ranch\|The Ranch]] or [[locations/dusthollow\|Dusty Rose]] |
| Approach | She's already questioning. Validate her experiences. |

### The Connection

[[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] is further along the Spiral than most [[lore/the-woven|Woven]]. She draws spirals without knowing why. She has déjà vu constantly. She's on the edge.

### Key Conversations

**After the Calico Boys ([[sessions/session-01-arrival|Session 1]])**:
> "Do you ever feel like... like you've forgotten something important? Something right on the edge of your mind?" She trails off, then forces a smile. "Sorry. I don't know why I said that."

**After the Reset ([[sessions/session-02-the-stray|Session 2]])**:
> "I remember you dying. I remember because I've died too. Many times. Too many times to count."
>
> She pulls aside her collar, showing unmarked skin. "I don't know what we are, but we're not... we're not normal."

**At Vulture's Roost ([[sessions/session-03-the-spiral|Session 3]])**:
> "I followed my drawings. They led me here. And then I remembered."
>
> Her voice drops. "I remembered who made these. I remembered *him*."

### Building the Relationship

[[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] is the Dolores analog—the one who awakens and leads. PCs can:
- Support her journey (+trust, +Spiral Points)
- Dismiss her concerns (-trust, she proceeds alone)
- Compete with her (creates tension, still functional)
- Romance (if appropriate for your table—she's drawn to those who see what she sees)

### Outcome

By Session 3, [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] is a full ally. She may:
- Lead PCs to [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]]
- Fight alongside them in finale
- Help make the final choice

## DM Notes

### Pacing Social Encounters

**Session 1**: Light social. Introductions. Hints.
**Session 2**: Meaningful conversations. [[characters/npcs/sylene\|Sylene]] opens up. [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] shares fears.
**Session 3**: Heavy revelations. [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane's]] truth. [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|Rider]] negotiation.

### Player Agency

These encounters should feel significant. Don't rush them. Let players explore dialogue. Their choices here shape the ending.

### NPC Relationships

Track trust levels informally:
- **Hostile**: Won't help, may hinder
- **Neutral**: Polite but guarded
- **Friendly**: Shares information, provides aid
- **Allied**: Fights alongside, trusts completely
