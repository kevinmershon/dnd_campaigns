# Encounter Index

## Standard Encounters

### The Calico Boys (Session 1)

**Setup**: Outlaws threatening Harken's General Store
**Enemies**: 4 Bandits
**Difficulty**: Easy (for Level 1 party)
**Goal**: Establish PCs as capable heroes

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Damage |
|----------|----|----|--------|--------|
| Bandit (x4) | 12 | 11 | +3 | 1d10+1 (sparkpistol) or 1d6+1 (scimitar) |

**Tactics**: Spread out, take cover behind barrels and posts. Surrender if 2+ are killed.

**Complications** (roll d4 if desired):
1. Hostage situation (townsfolk in crossfire)
2. Fire starts (knocked lantern)
3. Additional bandit arrives mid-fight
4. None

---

### The Ambush (Session 2)

**Setup**: Ashen Rider orchestrates trap, wants to test/break PCs
**Enemies**: 6 Bandits + The Ashen Rider (observing)
**Difficulty**: Deadly (intentionally)
**Goal**: Force at least one PC death to trigger reset revelation

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Damage |
|----------|----|----|--------|--------|
| Bandit (x6) | 12 | 11 | +3 | 1d10+1 (sparkpistol) |
| The Ashen Rider | 18 | 75 | +7 | 1d10+5 (sparkpistol x2) |

**Tactics**: Bandits use cover, focus fire. Rider only intervenes if PCs are winning—his attacks are lethal and unavoidable (Patron immunity in reverse).

**The Death**: When a PC drops, let them make death saves normally. If they stabilize, Rider finishes them. This isn't cruelty—it's necessary for the plot.

---

### Final Confrontation (Session 3)

**Setup**: Awakened PCs face the Ashen Rider
**Enemies**: The Ashen Rider (full combat)
**Difficulty**: Hard
**Goal**: Climactic fight where PCs can finally hurt him

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Damage |
|----------|----|----|--------|--------|
| The Ashen Rider | 18 | 75 | +7 | 1d10+5 x3 per turn |

**Tactics**: Uses all Battle Master maneuvers. Mobile, tactical. Takes cover, repositions.

**Maneuvers**:
- **Menacing Attack**: DC 14 Wis or frightened
- **Precision Attack**: +1d8 to hit roll
- **Riposte**: Attack when missed in melee
- **Disarming Attack**: DC 14 Str or drop weapon

**Terrain**: Use dramatic location (burning town, cliff edge, ruins) with:
- Falling hazards
- Fire/environmental damage
- Cover positions
- Elevation differences

### The Stray (Non-Combat)

**Setup**: Joss Harlow (former bandit) breaks programming
**Type**: Roleplay/horror
**Goal**: Show consequences of awakening, foreshadow

**Key Beats**:
1. He remembers dying (by PC hands, maybe)
2. He's confused, terrified, desperate
3. He asks questions no one can answer ("What am I?")
4. If not helped, Shepherds take him that night

**Resolution Options**:
- Hide him (Sylene can help)
- Let him be taken (he returns, wiped, the next day)
- Kill him (mercy killing? he asked for it?)
- Help him awaken (possible but difficult)

## Revelation Mechanics

### When PCs Investigate the Truth

**Finding Evidence**:
- DC 14 Investigation: Notice loop patterns
- DC 16 Investigation: Find Sylene's notes
- DC 18 Investigation: Locate Loom Frame access

**Memory Recovery** (Stage 2+):
- DC 15 Wisdom check, once per long rest
- Success: Recall one specific memory from a past loop
- The memory should be useful but disturbing

**Perceiving the Boundaries** (Stage 3+):
- No check required
- The world's edges shimmer with geometric patterns
- Travel toward them becomes subjectively difficult

## Social Encounters

### Sylene Interview
**Goal**: Extract information without revealing too much
**Approach**: She's cautious but curious. Respond well to honesty.
**Success**: Access to her notes, alliance
**Failure**: She becomes guarded, may tip off the Rider

### Vane Meeting
**Goal**: Earn trust, receive the full truth
**Approach**: He's already on their side. Be genuine.
**Success**: Full revelation, resources, support
**Note**: This should feel like a reward, not a challenge

### Ashen Rider Negotiation
**Goal**: Convince him to stand down or ally
**Approach**: He's desperate. Appeal to his humanity—or lack thereof.
**Success**: He withdraws, possibly helps
**Failure**: Combat (see above)
**Note**: Very difficult. He's been hollow for decades.
