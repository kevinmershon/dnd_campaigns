## Glitch Quick Reference

See [[encounters/glitch-events\|Glitch Events]] for full tables.

### Minor Glitches (DC 12-14)
- NPC repeats exact phrase
- Reflection delayed
- Sign text changes
- Sound cuts out

### Major Glitches (DC 15-17)
- Time freezes briefly
- Same person in two places
- Memory of something not yet done
- Horizon shimmers with geometry

### Scripted Glitches (No roll)
- [[sessions/session-01-arrival\|Session 1]]: Piano freeze, combat flashback, clock tower
- [[sessions/session-02-the-stray\|Session 2]]: Stagecoach repeat, [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] breakdown, Patron immunity
- [[sessions/session-03-the-spiral\|Session 3]]: Quieting failure
