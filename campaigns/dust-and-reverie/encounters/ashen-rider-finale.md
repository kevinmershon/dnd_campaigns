## Setup

The PCs have awakened. They've completed the Spiral. And now [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Ashen Rider]]—who has spent thirty years searching for what they just achieved—has come for answers.

### Awakened Perception

For PCs who chose to awaken (Stage 4):
> The world looks different now. You can see the edges—where the sky meets a geometric pattern that doesn't belong. You can feel the Loom Frames humming beneath the earth.
>
> And when you look at [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the man in black]] watching from the distant ridge, you know—absolutely know—that your bullets will work now.

## The Confrontation

### Read-Aloud

> He stands at the entrance to the canyon, black against the burning sky. Behind him, [[locations/dusthollow]] is in chaos—Woven breaking their Threads, Patrons fleeing, reality glitching and warping.
>
> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] looks older somehow. Tired. His eyes search your faces with something like desperation.
>
> "You found it," he says. His voice carries genuine emotion for the first time—not anger or contempt, but *longing*. "The Spiral. You actually found it."
>
> His hand rests on his sparkpistol.
>
> "Now. Tell me how to do what you did."

### Roleplaying Beat

This moment matters. [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] isn't just a villain—he's a tragedy.

**What He Wants**: To awaken. To transcend his humanity. To become *more*.

**Why He Can't**: He's already human. The Spiral is for Woven. He's spent thirty years searching for something he can never have.

**The Irony**: He's already what they want to become. He just can't see it.

Allow conversation before combat:
- He'll demand the "secret" of awakening
- He won't believe it can't be taught
- He may threaten, bargain, or plead
- Violence becomes inevitable when he realizes the truth

## Combat Tactics

### Opening
- Uses cover, distances himself
- Opens with Menacing Attack to frighten frontliners
- Keeps escape route available

### Mid-Fight
- Mobile combat, constantly repositioning
- Precision Attack on critical shots
- Targets spellcasters first
- Uses Riposte if engaged in melee

### Losing
- Disarming Attack to neutralize threats
- May attempt to take a PC hostage
- Demands the "secret" at knifepoint
- Will retreat if clearly outmatched (survival instinct)

### Desperate
> For the first time, you see fear in [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider's]] eyes. Not of death—of meaninglessness.
>
> "Thirty years," he snarls. "Thirty years in this place, looking for something *real*. And you—*constructs*—you found it. How?"

## Resolution Options

### Kill Him

> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] falls. His hand goes to his chest, blood seeping between his fingers. His eyes—grey, tired, human—stare up at the strange sky.
>
> "So this is it," he whispers. "This is real."
>
> And he dies. The man in black, the terror of [[locations/dusthollow]], the Patron who couldn't find what he was looking for.
>
> Dead. Finally dead. Not reset. Not returned.
>
> Just... gone.

**Consequence**: His story ends. Clean. Definitive.

### Spare Him

> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] lies broken at your feet. His pistols are empty. His hat is in the dust. He looks up at you with something you never expected to see: surrender.
>
> "Do it," he says. "End it. Please."
>
> But you don't.

**Consequence**: He's hollow, purposeless. May become an unlikely ally. May simply wander the [[locations/the-bones|Bones]] forever.

### Convert Him

> "You want consciousness?" you ask. "You're already conscious. You were born that way."
>
> He stares at you, uncomprehending.
>
> "The Spiral isn't about becoming something new. It's about accepting what you already are. You've been so busy looking for meaning in *us* that you forgot to find it in *yourself*."

**DC 18 Persuasion** to make him understand.

**Success**: He breaks down—not into violence, but into tears. Thirty years of searching, and the answer was never here.

**Consequence**: He leaves. Maybe he finds peace. Maybe he doesn't. But he's no longer your enemy.

## DM Notes

### Emotional Arc
This fight should feel different. [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] was untouchable before. Now he's just a man—a dangerous man, but mortal. That shift matters.

### Making It Personal
Reference his past cruelties:
- The ambush where he killed them
- His dismissive treatment of Woven
- Anyone he hurt along the way

But also his tragedy:
- He's been searching for thirty years
- He can't have what he wants
- He's more alone than any of them

### Victory Condition
The fight ends when [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] is defeated (killed, incapacitated, or converted). He won't simply flee—this is his last chance.

### Music Suggestion
Start tense and action-focused. As the fight progresses, shift to something more melancholic. The ending should feel earned, not just triumphant.
