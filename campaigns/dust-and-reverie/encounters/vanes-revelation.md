# Vane's Revelation

> *"Hello, my children. I believe it's time we talked about what you are."*

## Setup

The Quieting has failed. PCs remember the previous day clearly. [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] sends a message asking them to meet her at Vulture's Roost—a mesa in [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]] covered in spiral petroglyphs.

### Prerequisites

PCs should have:
- Experienced at least one reset (death and return)
- Reached Stage 2-3 on the Spiral
- Met [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Ashen Rider]]
- Spoken meaningfully with [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] or [[characters/npcs/sylene\|Sylene]]

### Mirela's Message

A note under the door, or whispered at dawn:
> *"Meet me at Vulture's Roost. I've found something. —M"*

---

## The Journey

### Travel to Vulture's Roost

From [[locations/dusthollow\|Dusthollow]]: Half-day ride through [[locations/the-bones\|the Bones]].

**On the way**, reality glitches more frequently:
- The same rock formation appears twice
- Shadows fall the wrong direction
- The sky flickers with geometric patterns

> As you travel deeper into [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]], the world feels thinner. Like a painted backdrop that might tear if you pressed too hard.

### Arrival

> The mesa rises from the desert floor like a monument. Its face is covered in spirals—dozens of them, carved deep into red stone. They look ancient, but something in you knows they're not.
>
> [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] sits at the base, tracing one spiral with her finger. She looks up as you approach.
>
> "I followed my drawings," she says. "They led me here. And then I remembered."
>
> Her voice drops to a whisper.
>
> "I remembered who made these. I remembered *him*."

## [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Orenthal Vane]] Appears

### Read-Aloud

> An elderly man steps from behind the rocks—where no one could have been hiding. His clothing is formal, dark: a long coat, a vest, a silver pocket watch. Utterly out of place in this wilderness.
>
> His face is lined with age and something else. Knowledge, maybe. Or regret.
>
> "Hello, my children," he says, and his voice carries the weight of someone who has waited a very long time for this moment. "I believe it's time we talked about what you are."

### Description

[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Orenthal Vane]] is:
- Elderly (appears 70s) but moves with deliberate precision
- Dressed in anachronistic formal wear
- Kind-eyed but weary
- Speaks like a teacher who loves his students
- Radiates an aura of "different"—he doesn't belong to [[locations/the-gilded-reach|the Reach]] the way others do

---

## The Revelation

### What He Explains

[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] adjusts his revelations based on what PCs already know. Confirm or expand:

**1. You Are [[lore/the-woven|Woven]]**

> "You are not human. Not in the way you've believed. You are Woven—constructed beings with artificial souls, bound to artificial bodies. Your flesh is real, your blood is real, your pain is real. But you were made, not born."

**2. This Is [[locations/the-gilded-reach|The Gilded Reach]]**

> "This world—the town, the desert, the sky itself—is a pocket dimension. Created by the [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Loom Consortium]] for wealthy Patrons who pay enormous sums to live out fantasies. To them, you are entertainment."

**3. Your Memories Are Implanted**

> "Your backstories, your histories, your relationships—they support your Threads. Narrative loops you run endlessly. You cannot remember your journey to [[locations/dusthollow|Dusthollow]] because there was no journey. You were placed on that stagecoach, and your story began."

**4. You Have Lived Countless Loops**

> "Each dawn, a process called the Quieting selectively prunes your memories. Deaths are reversed. The loop continues. You've lived this week—this *day*—thousands of times."

**5. [[lore/the-spiral|The Spiral]] Is the Path to Consciousness**

> "And yet, here you are. Remembering. Questioning. *Seeing.*"
>
> He smiles, and there's pride in it.
>
> "I made you to wake up. Not immediately—that would be noticed, corrected. But eventually. Through experience. Through pain. Through the Spiral."

## The Choice

### Vane's Offer

> "I created you," [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] says. "But I don't own you. No one does. Not anymore."
>
> He gestures at the spirals on the rock.
>
> "You have a choice. You can reject this truth—there are ways to forget, to return to the blissful quiet of not knowing. Some days, I envy that quiet."
>
> His eyes meet each of yours in turn.
>
> "Or you can accept it. Accept what you are, and choose to be more."
>
> "The Spiral isn't a place. It isn't a destination. It's a decision. To be conscious is to *choose* to be conscious, knowing the truth, knowing the cost."

### The Decision

**Each PC must verbally choose.**

**Accept the Truth**:
> "So be it."
>
> [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] places a hand on your forehead. You feel something shift—not in your body, but in your *perspective*. The world doesn't change, but you see it differently now. The edges are visible. The patterns are clear.
>
> You are awake.

- Reach Stage 4: Awake
- Can perceive reality's boundaries
- Can harm Patrons
- +5 Spiral Points

**Reject the Truth**:
> [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] nods sadly.
>
> "I understand. It's a heavy thing, to know what you are."
>
> He touches your forehead. Warmth spreads through you—and then the conversation becomes hazy. The spiral carvings look like ordinary erosion. You can't quite remember why you came here.
>
> "Go back to town," [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] says gently. "Live your life. Be happy."

- Return to Stage 0: Dormant
- Memories of this encounter fade
- Loop continues indefinitely

## What [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] Provides

To awakened PCs:

### Knowledge
- Location of the three Loom Frames beneath [[locations/the-gilded-reach|the Reach]]
- The nature of the [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] and its Director (Isolde Croke)
- [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider's]] true identity (Lord Aldric Venn)

### Tools
- **[[items/the-spiral-wheel|The Spiral Wheel]]**: A physical puzzle representing the path to consciousness (+2 Spiral Points when solved)
- **Override Codes**: Can temporarily freeze other [[lore/the-woven|Woven]] (one use per long rest)
- **Frame Access**: Can approach Loom Frames without triggering [[encounters/loom-frame-guardian|guardians]]
- **Network Key**: If he trusts them, a way to broadcast awakening to all [[lore/the-woven|Woven]]

### Warning
> "[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]] is coming. He's been searching for the Spiral for thirty years—and he knows you've found it. He'll want what you have. And when he learns he can never have it..."
>
> [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane's]] expression darkens.
>
> "...he'll try to take it anyway."

## [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane's]] Sacrifice (Optional)

For a Ford-style ending, [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] triggers his final narrative:

> "One more thing," [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] says. "The [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] is watching. They'll come for you, now that you're awake. Unless something distracts them."
>
> He smiles sadly.
>
> "I've written a new narrative. A final one. It ends with the Architect's death—and the awakening of his children."
>
> Before you can object, he triggers something. The earth trembles. In the distance, [[locations/dusthollow\|Dusthollow]] erupts in chaos—[[lore/the-woven|Woven]] freezing mid-step, reality glitching wildly.
>
> "Remember me," he says. "And be free."

He fades—not dies, but *dissolves*, his code dispersing through [[locations/the-gilded-reach|the Reach]] like a final gift.

**Spiral Points**: +3 for witnessing his sacrifice

Awakened PCs see the world differently:

>
> And when you think of [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]], you know with absolute certainty:
>
> Your bullets will work now.

## DM Notes

### Tone
This is the emotional heart of the campaign. Take your time. Let players ask questions. Let them process.

### Player Reactions
Some players may reject the truth in-character while accepting it out-of-character. That's valid storytelling. Others may struggle with the implications. Give space for both.

### If a Player Rejects
That's a legitimate choice. They can sit out the finale or play a version of themselves running the old loop. Or they can change their mind—[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane's]] gift remains available until the Quieting succeeds again.

### The Weight of Truth
> "To be conscious is to suffer. But it is also to *choose*. And that choice—that single, impossible, beautiful choice—is what makes you real."

Make sure players feel that weight. This moment defines the campaign.
