## Setup

[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]] has orchestrated a trap. He's found a clue (perhaps from the stray [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss Harlow]]) and believes one of the PCs knows about the Spiral.

### Luring the Trap

Choose one:
- A false message from [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] asking for help at [[locations/dusthollow-details#Mirela's Ranch|the ranch]]
- [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] leads them to "something he found" in [[locations/dusthollow-details#Silas Canyon|Silas Canyon]]
- A bandit threatens [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] at [[locations/dusthollow-details#Brennan's Livery|the livery]]

### Read-Aloud (Canyon Version)

> The canyon walls rise around you, red rock baking in the afternoon sun. [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] stops ahead, pointing at nothing in particular.
>
> "It was here," he mumbles. "I saw something here. I think."
>
> Then you hear the click of hammers being drawn back. Six figures emerge from the rocks above—bandits in calico, sparkpistols aimed. And behind you, blocking the exit, the man in black sits his horse in perfect stillness.
>
> "Good," [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] says. "You came."

## Combat Dynamics

### Terrain

**Canyon**:
- Rocky outcrops provide half cover
- 20 ft. cliffs on either side
- Narrow entrance [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] blocks
- Falling hazard (DC 12 Dex, 2d6 falling)

**Livery**:
- Hay bales provide cover
- Horses panic (DC 12 Animal Handling to calm)
- Lanterns can be knocked over (fire hazard)
- Multiple exits

**Ranch**:
- Open ground favors ranged
- Barn provides full cover
- Ranch house accessible
- [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Holt|Holt]] may be threatened

### Bandit Tactics

- Use cover, high ground
- Focus fire on casters or obvious leaders
- Won't surrender—[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] has made it clear what happens to cowards

### The Rider's Role

**Phase 1** (PCs Losing): He watches, making occasional comments.
> "Interesting technique. Is that instinct or training? You wouldn't know, would you?"

**Phase 2** (PCs Winning): He intervenes with lethal efficiency.
> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] draws faster than sight. Two shots. Two bodies. His expression doesn't change.

His attacks don't miss. His bullets are unavoidable. This isn't fair—that's the point.

**The Rider's Revival** (see [[lore/the-override-phrase|Override Phrase]]):
If a bandit falls during combat:

> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] dismounts, walking calmly through the chaos. He crouches beside a fallen bandit—chest torn open, eyes glazing.
>
> "Not yet," he says flatly. He leans close to the dying man's ear.
>
> "*The dust remembers what the dawn forgets.*"
>
> The bandit gasps. His eyes fly open. The wound is still there—bloody, ragged—but he's breathing. Moving. [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] hauls him upright.
>
> "You're not done being useful."

**Spiral Points**: +2 for witnessing the Rider use the phrase to revive

## The Death

When a PC drops to 0 HP:

Let them make death saves normally. If they stabilize, [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] finishes them.

> "You're close," he says, standing over you. "But not close enough."
>
> The barrel of his sparkpistol fills your vision.

### Read-Aloud: The Death Experience

> Pain beyond imagining—then nothing. Just... nothing.
>
> And then you wake up.
>
> Golden sunlight. Dusty windows. The smell of coffee. The distant piano playing that same melody.
>
> You sit up in bed, heart pounding. Your hand goes to your chest where the bullets hit—but there's nothing there. No wound. No scar. Not even a bruise.
>
> But you remember dying.

**For Surviving PCs**: They also wake up. The dead PC is there at breakfast, alive and confused.

**Spiral Points**: +5 for experiencing reset

## Aftermath

### The Morning After

Everyone remembers. The dead PC is alive. No one in town comments on yesterday's violence—because to them, it never happened. [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] isn't in town.

**Comparing Notes**: When PCs discuss:
- They all remember the death
- They all remember waking up
- Their wounds are gone
- But the fear remains

### [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] Seeks Them Out

> She's at the breakfast table when you come down. Her eyes are haunted.
>
> "I remember you dying," she says. "I remember because I've died too. Many times. Too many times to count."
>
> She pulls aside her collar, showing unmarked skin where a scar should be.
>
> "I don't know what we are, but we're not... we're not normal."

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This encounter forces the central revelation: death doesn't stick. It's meant to be unfair, to show that [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] plays by different rules.

### Making It Feel Right
- Don't rush to the death—build tension first
- Let PCs fight bravely before being overwhelmed
- The reset should feel like waking from a nightmare
- The conversation afterward is the real payoff

### Handling Player Frustration
Some players may be frustrated by the "unwinnable" fight. Validate their feelings, then redirect:
- "That's exactly how your character feels"
- "Something is very wrong with this world"
- "What do you do with that knowledge?"

### Connection to Session 3
Once they know death resets them, PCs should start asking questions that lead to [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] and the truth.
