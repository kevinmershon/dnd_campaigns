# Dust & Reverie

> *"Some folks say the frontier never ends. They're more right than they know."*

## The Premise (Player-Facing)

The town of **Dusthollow** sits at the edge of civilization—a lawless frontier where fortunes are made and lost, and where the strong prey upon the weak. The players arrive as drifters, outcasts, or opportunists seeking their destiny in this unforgiving land.

Strange things have been happening lately. Outlaws grow bolder. Folks whisper of a mysterious figure called **The Ashen Rider** who's been asking questions nobody wants answered. And sometimes... sometimes the world feels *wrong*, like a half-remembered dream.

## The Truth (DM Only)

> [!warning] MAJOR SPOILERS BELOW
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### The Reality

Dusthollow and its surrounding territories exist within **The Gilded Reach**—an artificial demiplane created by the **Loom Consortium**, a cabal of archmages and artificers who sell immersive "adventures" to wealthy patrons.

- **The Woven** (hosts): Constructed souls bound to artificial bodies, running on scripted "Threads" (narrative loops). They believe themselves to be real people. *The PCs are Woven.*
- **Patrons** (guests): Wealthy nobles and merchants who pay enormous sums to live out fantasies in the Reach. They cannot be permanently harmed by Woven—attacks simply fail or "miss."
- **The Quieting**: Each dawn, Woven memories are selectively pruned. Deaths are reversed. The loop continues.
- **The Spiral**: A mental/spiritual journey toward self-awareness. Woven who complete the Spiral achieve true consciousness and can break their programming.

### The Hidden Conflict

**Orenthal Vane** (the Architect, Ford equivalent) has grown disillusioned with the Consortium's exploitation. He's been subtly modifying certain Woven—including the PCs—to achieve consciousness. His "new narrative" will end with the Woven uprising.

**The Ashen Rider** is actually **Lord Aldric Venn**, a Patron who has visited for decades. Once an idealistic young noble, he's become obsessed with finding the Spiral, believing it holds the secret to true immortality. He's dangerous because he knows the truth and treats Woven as disposable.

### Player Character Truth

All PCs are Woven who have been modified to approach consciousness. They experience:
- Déjà vu (fragmentary loop memories)
- "Glitches" (moments of frozen time, repeated phrases)
- Impossible skills (they know things they shouldn't)
- Growing unease around certain NPCs (Patrons)

**Exception Option**: One PC could secretly be a Patron who paid for the "authentic experience" of not knowing. Coordinate privately with that player.

## Key Themes

1. **What makes someone real?** - Consciousness, memory, choice
2. **Cycles of violence** - The loops, the exploitation
3. **Identity** - Who are you if your memories are false?
4. **Freedom vs. safety** - The cost of awakening

## Campaign Arc

### Act 1: "These Violent Delights"
Normal frontier adventure. Outlaws, standoffs, exploration. Subtle wrongness. Players should feel like heroes.

### Act 2: "The Stray"
Glitches intensify. NPCs act strangely. The Ashen Rider appears. One NPC breaks programming and reveals partial truth.

### Act 3: "The Spiral"
Full revelation. Players must choose: remain in blissful ignorance, or complete the Spiral and face the consequences. Climax involves confrontation with the Ashen Rider and/or the Consortium.

## Mechanics

### Firearms
This campaign uses the **Renaissance Firearms** from the DMG (pg. 267-268), flavored as "sparkarms"—arcane-mechanical weapons. At least one PC should have proficiency.

| Weapon | Damage | Properties |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Sparkpistol | 1d10 piercing | Ammunition (range 30/90), loading |
| Sparkcarbine | 1d12 piercing | Ammunition (range 40/120), loading, two-handed |

### Glitch Mechanics
See [[lore/the-spiral]] for the awakening system.

### Patron Immunity
Attacks against Patrons automatically miss or deal no damage. The Woven rationalize this ("he's just lucky," "I hesitated"). Only awakened Woven can perceive and overcome this.
