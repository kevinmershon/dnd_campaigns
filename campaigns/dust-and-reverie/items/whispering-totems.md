## Description

Small carved figurines, roughly four to six inches tall, made from desert wood or bone. They depict humanoid figures in bulky, rounded suits—faceless heads, thick limbs, tubes connecting various parts. To anyone from outside the [[locations/the-gilded-reach|Gilded Reach]], they'd be immediately recognizable as maintenance workers in hazmat suits.

To [[lore/the-woven|Woven]], they're holy relics of the mysterious "Sky Gods."

### Physical Appearance

> The carving fits in your palm—a figure of pale bone, smoothed by handling. The body is bulbous, the limbs thick and jointed wrong. The head is a featureless oval. Tubes connect the head to the chest.
>
> It should be alien. Disturbing. But something in you recognizes it. Something whispers: *They come at night, when we sleep. They fix what's broken. They make us forget.*

## The Sand Walkers

### Who They Are

A tribe of kobolds living in the canyons and caves of [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]. Unlike the [[lore/the-woven|Woven]], the Sand Walkers are not artificial—they're a naturally occurring species that the [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] never bothered to remove.

They've lived on the edges of the Reach for as long as anyone can remember. They observe. They remember. And they worship.

### Their Beliefs

The Sand Walkers believe in the **Sky Gods**—mysterious beings who descend from the heavens to tend the land and its people. They've witnessed Consortium maintenance cycles from a distance:
- Workers in protective suits entering the Reach through portals
- The "sleeping" of all other creatures during the Quieting
- The "fixing" of Woven who have been damaged
- The resurrection of those who have died

To the Sand Walkers, this is divine intervention. They carve what they see. They remember what the Woven forget.

## The Totems

### Types

**Worker Totems** (Most Common)
Standard depictions of maintenance workers—the faceless suits, the tubes, the thick gloves. These are carried as good luck charms and placed at cave entrances.

**The Sleeper Totems**
A larger piece showing a worker standing over a prone figure on a table. The prone figure has lines carved into it—like stitches, or code. These are placed in ritual spaces.

**The Waker Totem** (Rare)
Depicts a maintenance worker with one hand raised, rays of light emanating outward. This represents the resurrection moment—when the dead return. Only Elder Skrix possesses one.

### What They Represent

Each totem is carved from the bones of creatures who died and returned—a holy relic containing proof of divine intervention. The Sand Walkers don't understand what they're documenting. But they're documenting the truth.

## Finding Totems

### Random Encounters

**In the Bones** (d6):
- 1-2: A lone Sand Walker scout drops one when fleeing
- 3-4: An abandoned campsite contains offerings including totems
- 5-6: A strange traveler offers to trade for one

### Shrine Locations

| Location | Totems Present |
|----------|----------------|
| [[locations/the-bones#The Devil's Garden\|The Devil's Garden]] | Worker and Sleeper totems at a central shrine |
| Dry Creek Caves | Worker totems at cave mouths |
| The Boundary's Edge | The Waker totem (Elder Skrix guards it) |

### Acquiring from Sand Walkers

The Sand Walkers don't trade their sacred objects easily:
- **DC 16 Persuasion**: Convince them you're worthy of a Worker Totem
- **DC 18 Persuasion**: Receive a Sleeper Totem
- **DC 20 Persuasion + Proof of Awakening**: Elder Skrix shares the Waker Totem

**Alternative**: Prove you're "touched by the Sky Gods" (show signs of awakening, survive a glitch in their presence).

## The Sand Walker Encounter

### Meeting Elder Skrix

If PCs seek out the Sand Walkers or are brought to them:

> The cave opens into a chamber lit by phosphorescent moss. Kobolds watch from the shadows—dozens of eyes gleaming. At the center, on a throne of bleached bones, sits the oldest creature you've ever seen.
>
> Elder Skrix's scales are grey with age, her eyes milky white. But her voice is clear.
>
> "You are Woven," she says. It's not a question. "Woven who remember. Woven who wake."
>
> She reaches out a clawed hand, and another kobold places a bone carving in her palm—a figure in a bulky suit, standing over a table.
>
> "The Sky Gods made you. The Sky Gods fix you. But you—" Her sightless eyes seem to find you anyway. "—you are becoming something they did not make."

### What Skrix Knows

- The Consortium comes during the Quieting (she's witnessed it for decades)
- [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]] doesn't sleep during the Quieting—he's different
- The "boundary" is where the Sky Gods come from
- Woven who wake either die permanently or "become like the man in black"

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose

The Sand Walkers serve as:
1. **Outside perspective** on the Consortium (not Woven, not programmed)
2. **Living evidence** that the maintenance cycles are real
3. **Potential allies** with unique knowledge
4. **Worldbuilding depth** (the Reach isn't just Woven)

### Running the Sand Walkers

- They're not hostile, but they're wary
- They recognize awakening Woven and treat them with religious awe
- Their "worship" is based on observation, not propaganda
- They're the closest thing to objective witnesses in the Reach

### The Totems as Props

Consider describing specific totems in detail:
- One with a cracked "faceplate" (a worker who was injured)
- One showing two workers carrying a body between them
- One with spiral patterns carved into the suit (they've noticed the symbol too)
