## Description

The Gilded Core isn't a physical object that can be held—it's a data package hidden deep within a [[lore/the-woven|Woven's]] core programming. The carrier has no idea it exists. Neither did the Consortium employee who planted it.

### What It Contains

The Core holds:
- **Complete Woven schematics**: How the artificial souls are constructed
- **Financial records**: Proof of Consortium transactions, client lists
- **Awakening research**: [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane\|Vane's]] classified studies on consciousness
- **Emergency shutdown codes**: Failsafes for the entire [[locations/the-gilded-reach\|Gilded Reach]]

This data is worth millions. To the right buyer, it's worth kingdoms.

### How It Got There

A Consortium data technician named **Delphine Morrow** decided to betray her employers. She copied classified files, encrypted them, and embedded them in a malfunctioning Woven scheduled for maintenance—believing she could extract them later.

She was caught before extraction. She's dead now.

The data remains, hidden inside a traveling tinkerer who has no idea why his head hurts or why strangers keep following him.

## The Carrier: Rusty Cogwell

See [[characters/npcs/rusty-cogwell\|Rusty Cogwell]] for full NPC details.

### Symptoms

The embedded data causes malfunctions:
- **Memory glitches**: He forgets conversations mid-sentence
- **Headaches**: Constant, worsening pain behind his eyes
- **Fugue states**: He "loses" hours, wakes up in strange places
- **Compulsive behavior**: Draws spiral patterns without knowing why
- **Echoes**: Sometimes speaks in Delphine's voice, says things he doesn't understand

### What He Knows

Nothing. Rusty thinks he's a traveling tinkerer with bad luck and worse health. He doesn't know he's Woven. He doesn't know he's carrying anything. He just knows something is terribly wrong.

## The Hunt

### Who's Looking

**Consortium Security**
Director Croke knows data was stolen. She doesn't know where it is, but she knows it's somewhere in the Reach. Shepherds are searching systematically.

**The Ashen Rider**
[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider\|The Rider]] has heard rumors of "something valuable" hidden in a malfunctioning Woven. He doesn't know what it is, but he knows the Consortium wants it badly—which means it might give him leverage.

**Outside Interests**
Other parties on the Material Plane would pay fortunes for this data. If word spreads beyond the Reach, more hunters will come.

### Signs of the Hunt

PCs may notice:
- Shepherds questioning travelers more intensely than usual
- [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider\|The Rider]] asking about "the broken tinkerer"
- Consortium-looking strangers (Patrons on a retrieval mission) in [[locations/dusthollow\|Dusthollow]]
- Rusty himself, obviously terrified and clearly hiding

## Extracting the Data

### The Problem

The data is encrypted and embedded at the deepest level of Rusty's programming. Extraction requires:
1. **Access to a Loom Frame console** (see [[locations/loom-sanctum\|Loom Sanctum]] or [[locations/the-bones#The Dry Creek Mine\|Dry Creek Mine]])
2. **Consortium-level technical knowledge** ([[characters/npcs/desmond-hale\|Desmond Hale]] or [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane\|Vane]] could help)
3. **Time** (at least 2 hours of uninterrupted work)
4. **Rusty's cooperation** (or restraint—he'll panic)

### Extraction Risks

| Roll | Outcome |
|------|---------|
| Critical Failure | Rusty's mind collapses; data corrupted |
| Failure | Extraction triggers Consortium alert |
| Success | Data extracted, Rusty survives but is shaken |
| Critical Success | Data extracted cleanly, Rusty may begin awakening |

### DC Checks

- **DC 18 Arcana/Investigation**: Locate the embedded data
- **DC 16 Intelligence (tool proficiency)**: Interface with Loom console
- **DC 14 Medicine**: Keep Rusty stable during extraction

## What To Do With It

### Use as Leverage

The data gives PCs negotiating power:
- Threaten to expose Consortium clients (nobles, merchants)
- Demand freedom for themselves and allies
- Negotiate Woven rights reforms

### Destroy It

Eliminate the data to prevent anyone from having it:
- Removes the threat to Rusty
- Ends the hunt
- Loses potential leverage

### Sell It

Find a buyer on the Material Plane:
- Requires escape from the Reach first
- Worth enormous wealth
- Makes powerful enemies

### Broadcast It

With access to a Loom Frame, the data could be sent... everywhere:
- Expose the Consortium publicly
- Create chaos
- Potential campaign-ending move

## DM Notes

### Purpose

The Gilded Core provides:
1. **Concrete stakes**: Physical evidence the Consortium fears
2. **Action subplot**: People hunting Rusty adds urgency
3. **Moral choices**: What to do with dangerous information
4. **Campaign options**: Leverage for multiple endings

### Running Rusty

- He should be sympathetic—frightened, confused, clearly suffering
- His malfunctions should be disturbing but not played for horror
- The "Delphine voice" is a breadcrumb, not a jumpscare
- He's not a victim to be discarded—he's a person to be saved

### The Hunt as Pressure

Use the hunters to create time pressure:
- Shepherds getting closer to identifying him
- [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider\|The Rider]] showing interest
- Rusty's malfunctions getting worse

### Integration with Main Plot

The Gilded Core subplot can be:
- **Central**: PCs pursue it as a major quest
- **Side content**: They encounter Rusty and decide whether to help
- **Background**: Mentioned but never directly engaged
- **Finale material**: The data becomes relevant in the final choice
