# Mirela's Sketchbook

> *"I draw what I see in my dreams. I don't know what any of it means. But I can't stop."*

| Attribute | Value |

## Description

A worn leather journal, roughly six inches by eight, with a brass clasp. The pages are filled with charcoal sketches, handwritten notes, and pressed flowers from the frontier. At first glance, it's a rancher's daughter's diary—sentimental, ordinary.

Look closer, and the drawings become disturbing.

### Physical Appearance

> The leather is soft from handling, the edges worn smooth. Inside, pages are filled with the careful hand of someone who draws to understand, not to impress.
>
> Flowers. Sunsets. Her father's face. A horse she loves.
>
> And then: spirals. Dozens of spirals, some covering entire pages. Geometric patterns that shouldn't mean anything but make your eyes hurt. Figures in strange suits—bulbous helmets, tubes and wires, shapes that don't belong in any frontier.
>
> And in the margins, written over and over: *"Where do you come from? Where do you go? Where do you come from, when you don't know?"*

## Contents

### The Ordinary Pages

- Sketches of [[locations/dusthollow\|Dusthollow]] and [[locations/dusthollow-details#Mirela's Ranch|the ranch]]
- Pressed wildflowers with notes about where she found them
- Lists of supplies needed, chores to do
- A half-written letter to "Mother" (she died years ago—in the Thread)

### The Strange Pages

#### Spiral Drawings
Page after page of [[lore/the-spiral\|spiral]] symbols. Some are simple. Others are elaborate, geometric, almost architectural. She doesn't know why she draws them.

#### The Masked Figures
Crude but recognizable drawings of figures in hazmat-style suits—Consortium maintenance workers she's glimpsed during "maintenance" cycles. She calls them "the faceless ones" and "the ones who fix things."

> A figure with a smooth oval for a face. Tubes running from the head. Thick gloves. Behind it, something that might be a table, and on the table, something that might be her.

#### The Machine
A sketch of a massive cylinder covered in geometric patterns—a [[locations/loom-sanctum|Loom Frame]]. She's never consciously seen one. The drawing is labeled: "The Hum Beneath the Ground."

#### The Map That Shouldn't Exist
A rough map of the [[locations/the-gilded-reach|Gilded Reach]] showing its actual shape—a bounded hexagonal area with the "Boundary" clearly marked. Woven shouldn't know this. The map is labeled: "The Edge of Everything."

#### Repeated Phrases
Written in margins, sometimes overlapping:
- "These violent delights have violent ends"
- "The maze is the mind"
- "I've been here before"
- "Round and round until we stop"

## Significance

The sketchbook is evidence of [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela's]] accelerating awakening. She's been Drawing spiral imagery and Consortium imagery for months without understanding why. The sketches are memories bleeding through—fragments of maintenance cycles, glimpses of the Loom Frames, echoes of [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane's]] programming.

### For Players

Seeing the sketchbook provides:
- **+2 Spiral Points** (first viewing)
- Evidence that something is wrong with reality
- Connection to Mirela's experience
- Clues about Loom Frame locations

## Acquiring the Sketchbook

### Mirela Shows It

She'll share the sketchbook if:
- PCs have proven trustworthy (helped in [[encounters/calico-boys-showdown\|the Calico Boys fight]])
- They've shown awareness of the wrongness (asked the right questions)
- She's reached Stage 2+ on the Spiral (actively questioning)

**DC 14 Persuasion** to see it early.
**DC 16 Persuasion** to borrow it.

### Mirela Offers It

After the reset in [[sessions/session-02-the-stray|Session 2]], Mirela seeks out the PCs:

> "I remember dying," she says. "I remember, and I need to know I'm not insane."
>
> She pulls out the sketchbook, hands trembling.
>
> "I've been drawing things I've never seen. Places I've never been. Tell me—tell me if any of this means something to you."

## DM Notes

### Purpose

Mirela's sketchbook serves as:
1. **Visual evidence** of awakening (show, don't tell)
2. **Foreshadowing** of the Loom Frames and Consortium
3. **Emotional connection** to Mirela's journey
4. **Clue source** for exploration

### Showing the Drawings

Consider describing specific pages when players examine it:
- The spiral that fills an entire page, getting tighter toward the center
- The faceless figure standing over a prone body (maintenance)
- The map with "HERE BE NOTHING" written at the edges
- A self-portrait where Mirela's eyes are spirals

### If Players Keep It

Mirela is reluctant to give it up—it's her only proof she's not insane. If PCs keep it:
- She feels vulnerable without it
- She may ask for it back at critical moments
- The [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] is interested in it (evidence of awakening)
