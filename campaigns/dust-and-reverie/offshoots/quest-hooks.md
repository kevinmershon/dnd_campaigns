# Quest Hooks & Narrative Loops

> *"Every story in Dusthollow has been told a thousand times. The question is whether anyone will remember it differently this time."*

## Overview

These are the "loops"—narrative storylines that [[lore/the-woven|Woven]] run and [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Patrons]] can engage with. PCs can stumble into these naturally, be hired for them, or discover them through exploration.

Each quest has:
- **Surface Story**: What it appears to be
- **Hidden Truth**: What's really happening
- **Glitch Opportunities**: Ways it can reveal the nature of reality
- **Resolution Options**: How it can end

## Combat & Adventure Hooks

### The Bounty Hunt

**Quest Giver**: [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin Dray]]

**Surface Story**:
> Corwin approaches at the saloon, respectful but direct.
>
> "I could use some help. There's a bounty on a man named Pike—one of [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss Harlow's]] boys. He killed a rancher out near the Bones. Two hundred gold for his head."
>
> He pauses. "I'd go alone, but I promised Mirela I wouldn't take unnecessary risks. Riding with a group isn't a risk."

**The Job**:
- Track Pike to the [[locations/the-bones#The Calico Hideout|Calico Hideout]]
- Take him dead or alive
- Return for reward

**Hidden Truth**:
Pike didn't kill anyone—the "murder" is a scripted narrative hook that resets each loop. The rancher is fine the next morning. But the bounty feels real, the violence is real, and PCs may notice that Corwin seems... practiced at this. Too practiced.

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- The "dead rancher" is seen alive the next day
- Corwin knows the route to the hideout without being told
- Pike says something like "Not you again" before combat

**Resolution**:
- Kill Pike → 200 gp, Corwin's respect
- Capture Pike → 200 gp, option to question him (he's confused about "how many times" he's been caught)
- Refuse → Corwin goes alone, returns injured

**Spiral Points**: +1 if glitch noticed

### The Gold Escort

**Quest Giver**: [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Harken|Harken]]

**Surface Story**:
> Harken pulls you aside, nervous.
>
> "I've got a shipment coming in from the [[locations/the-bones#The Dry Creek Mine|Dry Creek Mine]]. Silver, mostly. Problem is, word's gotten out, and the [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow#The Calico Boys|Calico Boys]] will be waiting."
>
> He wrings his hands. "Fifty gold each to ride escort. More if you run into trouble."

**The Job**:
- Meet the mine wagon at dawn
- Escort through [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]
- Deliver safely to Harken's store

**Hidden Truth**:
The "silver" shipment is cover for Consortium supply runs—technical equipment, replacement parts, chemicals for the Loom Frames. The Calico Boys are programmed to attack; they always attack.

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- The cargo crates have strange symbols (Consortium markings)
- One crate hums faintly (Loom Frame components)
- If a crate breaks open: geometric crystals that don't look natural

**Ambush Encounter**:

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Damage |
|----------|----|----|--------|--------|
| Calico Boy (x4) | 12 | 11 | +3 | 1d10+1 |
| Pike | 13 | 16 | +4 | 1d10+2 |

**Resolution**:
- Success → 50 gp each, Harken's gratitude
- Investigate cargo → DC 16 Investigation reveals technical equipment; DC 18 Arcana identifies it as "reality maintenance" components
- Steal cargo → Consortium attention, potential [[items/the-spiral-wheel|Spiral Wheel]] or [[items/whispering-totems|totem]] among contents

**Spiral Points**: +1 (glitch), +2 (investigating cargo)

### Kobold Hunting

**Quest Giver**: [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Old Brennan|Old Brennan]] or [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Holt|Holt]]

**Surface Story**:
> "Damn lizards been stealing my chickens again. Five gold per tail. Ten if you find where they're nesting."

**The Job**:
- Track [[items/whispering-totems#The Sand Walkers|Sand Walker]] scouts into [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]
- Recover stolen livestock
- Optional: Find their caves

**Hidden Truth**:
The "kobolds" are the Sand Walkers—the only non-Woven inhabitants of the Reach. They've been observing the Consortium for generations. They're not thieves; they're scavengers trying to survive on the edges of a world that wasn't made for them.

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- The kobolds have [[items/whispering-totems|carvings]] of humanoid figures in strange suits
- They recognize the PCs ("The Sleepers Who Walk")
- Elder Skrix offers information in exchange for peace

**Resolution Options**:
- Extermination → 50 gp, removes a source of truth
- Negotiation → Access to Sand Walker knowledge, [[items/whispering-totems|totems]]
- Alliance → Ongoing help from non-Woven observers

**Spiral Points**: +1 (seeing totems), +2 (meeting Elder Skrix)

## Mystery & Investigation Hooks

### Pearl's Missing Sister

**Quest Giver**: [[characters/npcs/dusty-rose-staff#Pearl|Pearl]]

**Surface Story**:
> Pearl approaches when no one's watching.
>
> "I need to find someone. My sister, Opal. She was working at a saloon in Copperton, two days' ride east. Then the letters stopped."
>
> Her voice drops. "I have forty gold saved. I'll give you all of it. I just need to know what happened to her."

**The Job**:
- Travel east toward "Copperton"
- Find Opal or learn her fate

**Hidden Truth**:
There is no Copperton. There is no Opal. The [[locations/the-gilded-reach|Gilded Reach]] ends at [[locations/the-bones#The Boundary|the Boundary]]—there is no "two days east."

Pearl's backstory includes a sister she's never actually had. The letters were manufactured memories. If PCs travel far enough, they'll hit the edge of reality.

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- Maps don't show Copperton
- No one remembers hearing of it
- Travel east becomes progressively harder (instinctive aversion)
- The Boundary shimmers with geometric patterns

**Resolution Options**:
- Reach the Boundary → Major revelation (+3 Spiral Points)
- Investigate locally → Find Copperton doesn't exist (library, old timers); Pearl breaks down
- Lie to Pearl → She's satisfied but suspicious
- Tell Pearl the truth → Devastating; possible ally if she accepts it

**Spiral Points**: +2 (discovering the lie), +3 (seeing the Boundary)

### The Widow's Vigil

**Quest Giver**: [[characters/npcs/saloon-regulars#Widow Parsons|Widow Parsons]]

**Surface Story**:
> "Please," she says, clutching your arm with surprising strength. "My husband, Tobias. He went to the [[locations/the-bones#The Dry Creek Mine|mine]] three days ago. He should have been back by now."
>
> Her eyes fill with tears. "Everyone else has given up. But I know he's out there. Please find him."

**The Job**:
- Search the Dry Creek Mine
- Find Tobias Parsons
- Bring him home or bring closure

**Hidden Truth**:
Tobias Parsons doesn't exist—not anymore. He was a background Woven removed during a Thread update. The mine's records show no one by that name. No one remembers him. But Widow Parsons's programming was never adjusted; she'll wait forever for someone who was erased from reality.

**Investigation**:
- **Town records** (DC 14 Investigation): No marriage certificate for Parsons
- **Old Jem** (DC 12 Persuasion): "Parsons? Don't recall the name. Then again, I don't recall much."
- **Mine records** (DC 16 Investigation): No employee named Tobias
- **[[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]]** (if trusted): "There are ghosts in this town. People who should exist but don't. People waiting for things that will never come."

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- The wedding ring she shows is blank inside (no inscription)
- Photographs of "Tobias" are blurred or missing faces
- Her home has spaces where furniture should be—his chair, his belongings—but empty

**Resolution Options**:
- Search the mine → Find nothing, but potentially find Loom Frame
- Tell the truth → Widow Parsons either breaks down or refuses to believe
- Fabricate closure → "He died peacefully" gives her peace, but it's a lie
- Help her remember → DC 18 Insight; she briefly recalls he was "taken" during a "smoothing"

**Spiral Points**: +2 (investigating), +3 (understanding what happened to Tobias)

### Doc Mercer's Impossible Cases

**Quest Giver**: PCs notice, or [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Doc Mercer|Doc Mercer]] admits under pressure

**Surface Story**:
> Something's strange about Doc Mercer's practice. Gunshot wounds that heal overnight. Patients who were dead on his table walking around the next morning. He doesn't advertise it—he doesn't even talk about it. But his records don't lie.

**The Investigation**:
- Examine Mercer's patient logs (DC 14 Investigation to access, DC 16 to notice patterns)
- Interview "survivors" (they don't remember dying)
- Confront Mercer (he's terrified someone finally noticed)

**Hidden Truth**:
Mercer has unknowingly documented thousands of loop resets. His records show patients dying and recovering—not medically, but through the Quieting. He's written it off as "frontier toughness," but deep down, he knows it's impossible.

**What the Logs Show**:
- Same injuries recurring on the same patients
- "Deceased" patients returning alive the next day
- Notes that trail off: "Patient expired at 3:47 PM. Note: Patient seen at breakfast this morning. Record unclear."

**Doc Mercer's Response**:
> "I've been practicing medicine for—" He stops. "How long have I been here? I can't... I can't remember a time before Dusthollow."
>
> He looks at his hands. "People die in my office. And then they're fine. I tell myself it's a miracle. But miracles don't happen every day. Every single day."

**Resolution Options**:
- Take the records → Physical evidence of the loops (+3 Spiral Points)
- Help Mercer understand → Potential ally (low awakening potential, but willing)
- Destroy the records → Mercer thanks you, returns to denial

**Spiral Points**: +3 (understanding the records)

### Father Aldous's Demons

**Quest Giver**: [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Father Aldous|Father Aldous]] (indirectly)

**Surface Story**:
> The preacher's been drinking more than usual. Muttering about "spiral paths" and "the center that waits." His sermons have taken a strange turn—less scripture, more... prophecy.
>
> Some say he's lost his faith. Some say he's found something else.

**The Investigation**:
- Attend his sermons (strange spiral imagery, not-quite-scripture)
- Speak with him sober (rare, early morning)
- Examine his chapel (spiral symbols carved under the altar)

**Hidden Truth**:
[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] embedded clues in Father Aldous's programming—spiral imagery hidden in religious language. Aldous doesn't understand what he's saying, but he's a walking breadcrumb trail to [[lore/the-spiral|the Spiral]].

**His "Scripture"**:
- "Round and round the path leads, but the center waits for those who seek."
- "In the beginning was the Thread, and the Thread was with the Maker, and the Thread was lies."
- "Blessed are those who remember, for they shall see the truth."

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- He quotes "scripture" that doesn't exist in any holy book
- The chapel's weathervane is a spiral (see [[lore/the-spiral|Spiral locations]])
- When drunk, he draws spirals in the dirt and weeps

**Resolution Options**:
- Decode his words → DC 16 Religion/Insight reveals spiral clues
- Help him understand → Terrifying for him; may break down or find peace
- Follow the clues → Lead to [[locations/the-bones#Vulture's Roost|Vulture's Roost]] and the petroglyphs

**Spiral Points**: +1 (attending sermon), +2 (decoding clues)

## Social & Personal Hooks

### The Card Sharp's Gambit

**Quest Giver**: [[characters/npcs/saloon-regulars#Clara Vance|Clara Vance]]

**Surface Story**:
> The widow corners you after a game.
>
> "I've watched you play. You're not bad. I have a proposition."
>
> "There's a high-stakes game tonight. Private room. [[characters/npcs/saloon-regulars#Lucky Mercer|Lucky Mercer's]] invited, and so is a Patron—wealthy traveler, deep pockets, thinks he can't lose."
>
> She smiles coldly. "I need a partner. Someone to distract the mark while I work. Sixty-forty split. Interested?"

**The Job**:
- Participate in the private poker game
- Keep the Patron distracted
- Help Clara clean him out

**Hidden Truth**:
The "Patron" is real—an actual Consortium client playing the gambling narrative. He views Clara and the other players as entertainment. Cheating him is dangerous; winning openly is a blow to his ego.

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- The Patron treats Woven with casual cruelty (they're not real to him)
- Clara seems confused when she wins—"I've played this hand before"
- If violence erupts, the Patron cannot be harmed (immunity)

**Resolution Options**:
- Help Clara win → 200 gp split, Patron humiliated (may seek revenge)
- Expose Clara → Patron grateful, Clara hostile
- Attack the Patron → Discover immunity (+3 Spiral Points)
- Walk away → Clara handles it alone (50/50 success)

**Spiral Points**: +1 (glitch), +3 (discovering Patron immunity)

### The Traitor in the Calico Boys

**Quest Giver**: Della (secret meeting)

**Surface Story**:
> A woman in dusty trail clothes finds you outside town. She's nervous, keeps checking over her shoulder.
>
> "I'm Della. I ride with [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Harlow]]. But I want out."
>
> She spits. "The things we do—they're getting worse. And something's wrong with Joss. He's been talking about dying. About remembering dying. It's got the boys spooked."
>
> "I've got money hidden. Two hundred gold. Get me out of the Bones alive, and it's yours."

**The Job**:
- Secretly meet Della at the [[locations/the-bones#The Calico Hideout|hideout]]
- Extract her without alerting the gang
- Get her to safety (Dusthollow or beyond)

**Hidden Truth**:
Della's desire to escape is genuine—but she can't actually leave. The [[locations/the-gilded-reach|Gilded Reach]] has boundaries. "Beyond" doesn't exist. If she travels far enough, she'll hit the edge and loop back.

**Complications**:
- Pike suspects betrayal
- [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] is glitching (confused, sympathetic, dangerous)
- The Twins are simple but loyal—they'll fight

**Resolution Options**:
- Extract Della → 200 gp; she thanks you, but where does she go?
- Failed extraction → Combat with Calico Boys
- Talk to Joss → He might let her go (he's too confused to care)
- Explain the truth → Della breaks down when she realizes there's no escape

**Spiral Points**: +1 (Joss's glitching), +2 (explaining the truth to Della)

### Following Mirela

**Quest Giver**: None—PCs choose to follow [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela's]] loop

**Surface Story**:
> She's beautiful, kind, and there's something different about her. Something in her eyes that suggests she sees more than she says. Each morning she rides into town. Each afternoon she gets caught up in some adventure. Each evening she returns home or stays for the dance.
>
> And each morning, she doesn't remember.

**The Experience**:
Following Mirela means watching her run her Thread—and watching her slowly break free of it. This is the campaign's main narrative, but it can also be a "quest" players choose to pursue.

**The Arc**:
1. **Day 1**: Standard loop. She's friendly, distracted, hints at something wrong.
2. **Day 2**: Déjà vu moments. She draws spirals. Looks at PCs with recognition.
3. **Day 3**: Remembers fragments. Seeks out PCs. Shares her [[items/mirelas-sketchbook|sketchbook]].
4. **Day 4+**: Progressive awakening. Becomes an ally.

**Romantic Option**:
If a PC pursues romance with Mirela:
- It's programmed—she's designed to respond to romantic interest
- As she awakens, she questions whether her feelings are real
- Ultimate resolution: Are they choosing to love, or following a script?

**Glitch Opportunities**:
- She knows things she shouldn't
- Her drawings match things that happen later
- She remembers previous days before she should

**Resolution**:
Mirela's awakening leads to [[encounters/vanes-revelation|Vane's Revelation]] and the campaign's climax.

**Spiral Points**: Accumulate throughout; Mirela is a primary source

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Using Quest Hooks

- **Session 1**: Introduce 1-2 hooks naturally (Bounty Hunt, Gold Escort, Card Sharp)
- **Session 2**: Escalate with mystery hooks (Pearl's Sister, Doc's Cases)
- **Session 3**: Hooks become secondary to main plot, but can provide evidence/allies

### Glitch Integration

Each quest is designed with "cracks"—moments where the artificial nature of the world shows through. Use these to:
- Award Spiral Points
- Foreshadow the main revelation
- Reward observant players

### NPC Reuse

These quests reuse existing NPCs to create interconnected stories:
- **Corwin** appears in Bounty Hunt and as Mirela's protector
- **Joss** connects Session 1 combat, the Traitor quest, and his stray breakdown
- **Pearl** and **Widow Parsons** have parallel "searching for someone who doesn't exist" arcs
- **Father Aldous** and **Old Jem** both contain hidden Spiral clues

### Resolution Flexibility

No quest has a single "correct" ending. All resolutions should:
- Feel meaningful
- Have consequences
- Potentially advance awakening
