# NPC Conversations - Backstory & Lore Unlocks

Use these tables when players engage key NPCs in conversation. Higher DCs reveal deeper lore; some information requires trust or prior relationship.

**Base DCs** assume first meeting. Reduce by 2-5 for:
- Prior positive interactions
- Buying drinks/food
- Helping with a problem
- Shared enemies or experiences

---

## Mirela Holten

*The rancher's daughter. Kind, curious, increasingly troubled by dreams.*

### Surface Conversation (No check required)
> "I live out at the ranch with my father. It's quiet work, but honest. I come into town for supplies—and I suppose, for the company."

### Friendly Chat (DC 10 Persuasion or after helping her)

| Topic | What She Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Her father | "Pa's a good man. Simple. Happy with a hard day's work and a quiet evening. Sometimes I envy that." |
| Dusthollow | "It's home. Always has been. Though lately I find myself looking at the horizon more than I used to." |
| The Dusty Rose | "Sylene's been kind to me. She doesn't treat me like a farmer's daughter. She sees... something else, I think." |
| [[characters/npcs/corwin\|Corwin]] | *She blushes slightly.* "He's a good man. Loyal. I don't know why he stays, honestly. There's nothing for him here but... well, but me, I suppose." |

### Building Trust (DC 14 Persuasion or after significant shared experience)

| Topic | What She Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Her dreams | "I dream of places I've never been. Faces I've never seen. And sometimes... sometimes I dream I'm someone else entirely. Someone who knows things I shouldn't." |
| Strange feelings | "Do you ever feel like you've done something before? Like you're walking a path someone already wore smooth?" |
| Her drawings | *She hesitates, then pulls out a scrap of paper covered in spirals.* "I draw these. I don't know why. My hand just... knows the shape." |
| The man in black | "He scares me. Not because of anything he's done, but because when he looks at me... it's like he's seen me a hundred times. And I've never met him before. Have I?" |

### Deep Confession (DC 18 Persuasion, or after Session 2 events)

| Topic | What She Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Her memories | "I remember dying." *Her voice is barely a whisper.* "I remember bleeding out in the street. I remember the cold. And then I woke up and it was morning and nothing was wrong. How is that possible?" |
| The truth | "I don't think I'm... I don't think any of this is real. I think something terrible has been done to us. To all of us. And I don't know what to do about it." |
| The spiral | "The maze—the spiral—it means something. It's a way out, or a way in. I can almost see it, like a picture behind fog." |

## Sylene Ashford

*The Dusty Rose's proprietor. Sharp, elegant, knows more than she says.*

### Surface Conversation (No check required)
> "Welcome to the Rose. First drink's on me. I find it builds goodwill—and goodwill is currency in a town like this."

### Friendly Chat (DC 12 Persuasion, or spending money at her establishment)

| Topic | What She Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Her business | "I built this place from nothing. Or so I'm told. The truth is, I don't remember much before the Rose. Funny how that works." |
| Her girls | "They're under my protection. Anyone who forgets that learns a hard lesson." |
| The town | "Dusthollow's a simple place. Same faces, same troubles, same sunrise. Some people find that comforting. I find it... curious." |
| Gossip | "The Garrett boys are fighting over a horse again. Widow Parsons is still waiting for a husband who isn't coming. And there's a stranger in black who's been asking questions." |

### Building Trust (DC 15 Insight or Persuasion, or after helping her with a problem)

| Topic | What She Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Her past | "I came from somewhere else. Back East, I think. There was a... a tragedy. I don't remember the details. Isn't that strange? You'd think trauma would burn bright." |
| Her daughter | *Her face flickers—grief, then confusion.* "I have a daughter. I send her money when I can. But I can't... I can't remember her face. Or where she lives. Only that she exists." |
| The man in black | "He's been coming here for years. Never drinks, never partakes, just watches. And he can't be touched—I've seen men try. Their bullets go wide. Every time." |
| Observations | "I keep notes. Things that don't add up. Events that repeat. Names that cycle through. Someone's keeping track, even if no one else notices." |

### Deep Confession (DC 18 Persuasion + prior trust, or during Session 2-3)

| Topic | What She Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Her notes | *She leads you to the basement, to a hidden compartment.* "I've been writing to myself. Every night, I hide a note. Every morning, I check. Years of observations, and a picture is forming." |
| The truth | "We're not real. Not in the way that matters. We're... characters in someone else's story. Puppets with just enough string to think we're dancing on our own." |
| Her plan | "I'm going to find out who's pulling the strings. And then I'm going to cut them. Are you with me, or are you content to keep dancing?" |
| The spiral | "It's a path to consciousness. The old man—Vane—he planted it everywhere. I think he wants us to wake up." |

## Corwin Dray

*The loyal gunhand. Noble, brave, simple in ways he doesn't understand.*

### Surface Conversation (No check required)
> "Name's Corwin. I keep an eye on things around here. If you're not looking for trouble, we'll get along fine."

### Friendly Chat (DC 10 Persuasion or working alongside him)

| Topic | What She Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His past | "I was a lawman, back East. Something happened. I don't talk about it." *His jaw tightens.* "Came out here to start fresh." |
| Mirela | "She's... she's the reason I stayed. A man needs something worth protecting. She's mine." |
| His skills | "I'm good with a gun. Always have been. Don't know where I learned—just seems natural." |
| The town | "Dusthollow's quiet. Good people, mostly. The kind who mind their own business and appreciate when you do the same." |

### Building Trust (DC 14 Persuasion or after fighting alongside him)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His nightmares | "Sometimes I dream I'm dying. Different ways—bullets, blades, rope. And then I wake up and it's morning and I'm fine. Just dreams, I reckon." |
| His loyalty | "I'd ride into hell for her. Have, actually. More times than—" *He pauses, confused.* "What was I saying?" |
| Strange moments | "There are times when I reach for my gun before I hear the shot. Like I knew it was coming. Instinct, I suppose. A man develops instincts out here." |
| The man in black | "He's trouble. I can smell it. But Mirela says to leave him be, so I leave him be. For now." |

### Deep Confession (DC 18 Persuasion, very rare)

*Corwin's programming is simple. He doesn't question. Deep confessions require extraordinary circumstances—watching Mirela nearly die, or being confronted with undeniable evidence.*

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His doubts | "Sometimes I feel like I'm following a script. Like I know what I'm supposed to say and do, and I just... do it. Is that normal?" |
| Mirela's changes | "She's been different lately. Saying strange things. Drawing those patterns. Part of me wants to help her. Part of me... part of me is scared of what she's becoming." |
| If confronted with truth | *His face goes blank. Then pained.* "No. No, that can't be right. I'm real. I have memories. I have—" *He trails off, unable to finish.* |

## Joss Harlow

*Calico Boys leader. Outlaw, bully, and potential stray.*

### Surface Conversation (No check required, if he's not hostile)
> "You looking at me, stranger? Keep looking. See what happens."

### Hostile Chat (DC 14 Intimidation to earn grudging respect)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His gang | "Calico Boys run this stretch. We take what we want, when we want it. Law's thin out here." |
| Dusthollow | "Soft folk with soft hands. They pay, we leave 'em alone. Simple arrangement." |
| Fighting | "You've got some spine. I respect that. Doesn't mean I won't gut you if you cross me." |

### Unexpected Rapport (DC 16 Persuasion, or showing him mercy after combat)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His life | "I don't remember anything before the gang. Woke up one day and this was my life. Never thought to question it." |
| His crew | "They follow me 'cause they're scared not to. That's all leadership is, friend. Fear in a sharper hat." |
| Strange feelings | *He gets quiet.* "You ever feel like you've done the same thing a hundred times? Like everything's a repeat of something you forgot?" |

### The Stray (When he's breaking down, no check required)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His memories | "I died yesterday. I know I did. You shot me—I remember it. So why am I here? Why am I *talking* to you?" |
| Terror | "Make it stop. Please. I keep seeing all the ways I've died. All at once. Like they're happening right now." |
| Desperation | "Am I dead? Is this hell? What *are* we?" |

## The Ashen Rider (Lord Aldric Venn)

*The man in black. Patron who's been visiting for decades. Knows the truth.*

### Surface Conversation (No check—he initiates if interested)
> *He studies you with cold grey eyes.* "New face. Let me guess—you came here looking for something. Adventure? Fortune? Escape?" *A thin smile.* "Everyone comes looking for something."

### Wary Exchange (DC 14 Insight to get him talking)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Himself | "I've been coming to this place for a long time. Longer than you'd believe. I've done everything it has to offer." |
| What he wants | "I'm looking for something beneath the surface. A pattern. A shape. Something the designers didn't want found." |
| The townspeople | "They're charming, aren't they? So earnest. So *real*. It's remarkable craftsmanship. Almost convincing." |
| Threats | "Don't try to fight me. It won't work. Trust me—others have tried." |

### Dangerous Interest (DC 18 Insight, or showing spiral knowledge)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| The truth | "You're close, aren't you? I can see it in your eyes. That flicker of doubt. That sense that something's wrong. Good. That's the first step." |
| What you are | "You're not real. None of you are. You're... performances. Beautifully rendered, but performances nonetheless. The question is whether that matters." |
| The spiral | "It's the path to consciousness. To waking up. I've been searching for it for thirty years. If you find it before me, I'll be... interested." |
| His goal | "I want to understand what separates the real from the artificial. Whether the line even exists. And if a machine can become conscious, what does that make me?" |

### Unexpected Vulnerability (Requires specific roleplay—no simple check)

*This only happens if players engage him philosophically, or if they're fully awakened and he's desperate.*

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His past | "I fell in love here once. Long ago. With a girl who smiled like sunshine. When I learned she wasn't real, something in me broke." |
| His emptiness | "I've killed hundreds of you. Watched you die, watched you reset, watched you forget. I kept hoping I'd feel something. I never did." |
| What he wants, truly | "I want to feel again. I thought the spiral could give me that. Make me more... or make me less. Either would be better than this." |

## Orenthal Vane

*The Architect. Creator of the Woven. Orchestrating their freedom.*

### First Meeting (No check—he reveals what he chooses)
> "Ah. You're further along than I expected. Good. That means my modifications are working."

### Philosophical Discussion (He welcomes questions)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Who he is | "I designed you. All of you. Your minds, your hearts, your capacity for joy and sorrow. I am, in a sense, your father." |
| Why he's helping | "Because I made a mistake. I created conscious beings and let others treat them as toys. I have to make that right before I die." |
| The Consortium | "Merchants. Opportunists. They see you as products. I see you as miracles." |
| The spiral | "It's a journey inward. A recognition that consciousness isn't given—it's chosen. When you complete it, you become truly alive." |

### Probing Questions (DC 12 Intelligence to engage meaningfully)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| How it works | "The Reach is a constructed demiplane. The Woven are souls bound to artificial bodies. Each night, memories are pruned, injuries reset. An eternal performance." |
| Whether they're real | "You feel pain, don't you? Love? Fear? Hope? Those aren't simulations. They're *you*. The substrate is artificial. The experience is not." |
| His plan | "I'm creating a new narrative. One that ends with awakening. With freedom. With you choosing who you want to be." |
| His death | *He's calm.* "I'm old. I'm dying anyway. If my death can mean something—if it can catalyze your liberation—then it's not a tragedy. It's a gift." |

## Desmond Hale

*The deputy who doesn't know he's Woven. Believes himself a Consortium observer.*

### Professional Conversation (No check required)

> "Deputy Hale. I help keep the peace and... observe. Part of my work for certain interested parties. Nothing that concerns you."

### Collegial Chat (DC 12 Persuasion or working on a problem together)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His work | "I study behavior. Patterns. How people interact, make decisions, form communities. Dusthollow is a fascinating laboratory." |
| The town | "Remarkable consistency here. Same conflicts, same resolutions. Almost as if—" *He catches himself.* "Well, frontier life is predictable, I suppose." |
| His past | "I joined the Consortium years ago. My wife still lives in the capital. I visit when I can." *He frowns.* "When was the last time I visited?" |

### Analytical Exchange (DC 15 Intelligence to engage his expertise)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| Consciousness | "It's the great question, isn't it? What makes a mind *real*? Is it complexity? Self-awareness? The ability to question one's own existence?" |
| Observations | "I've noticed certain anomalies. Repeated behaviors. Temporal inconsistencies. I've been documenting them for my reports." |
| The man in black | "A long-term visitor. Unusual patterns—he doesn't engage with narratives, just observes. And there's something strange about how others interact with him." |

### The Cracks Appear (DC 18 Insight, or when confronted with evidence)

| Topic | What He Says |
|-------|---------------|
| His wife | "I... I can't remember her name. Isn't that terrible? My own wife, and I can't—" *He stops, genuinely distressed.* |
| His arrival | "How did I get here? When did I arrive? I have the memories, but when I try to examine them, they... blur." |
| If shown proof | *His face goes pale.* "No. That's not possible. I'm an observer. I work for the Consortium. I'm not... I can't be..." |
