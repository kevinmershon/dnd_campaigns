# Travel & Connections

How locations in the Gilded Reach connect, and what journeys between them feel like.

---

## Map Overview

```
                    THE BONES
                   (Badlands)
                       |
                       | 2 hours
                       |
    [Edge - blocked]---DUSTHOLLOW---[Edge - shimmer]
                       |
                       | 20 min
                       |
                  MIRELA'S RANCH
                       |
                       | [Edge - mountains]
```

The Reach is approximately 50 miles in diameter, but travel distances don't always add up. The world loops subtly—ride too far in any direction and you'll find yourself circling back.

## Dusthollow ↔ Mirela's Ranch

**Distance**: 20 minutes on horseback, 45 minutes on foot
**Terrain**: Packed dirt road through open grassland

### The Journey

> The road to the Holten ranch winds through knee-high grass that whispers in the constant wind. Fence posts mark property lines. In the distance, cattle graze under the watchful eye of the mountains.
>
> It's peaceful. Almost too peaceful. The kind of quiet that feels like it's waiting for something.

### Landmarks

- **The Split Oak**: A lightning-struck tree at the halfway point. Locals use it for directions.
- **Holt's Fences**: Well-maintained wire fencing starts about ten minutes from the ranch.
- **The Creek**: A small stream crosses the road. Shallow enough to wade or ride through.

### Glitch Opportunity (DC 15 Perception)
The Split Oak looks different each time you pass it. Same tree, but the branches seem to have moved. No one else notices.

### What Might Happen

| d6 | Travel Encounter |
|----|------------------|
| 1 | A lone rider heading into town—nods but doesn't stop |
| 2 | Cattle blocking the road—takes 10 minutes to pass |
| 3 | The Garrett brothers riding past, still arguing |
| 4 | Nothing—suspiciously, completely nothing |
| 5 | A vulture circling overhead—following you? |
| 6 | You could swear you just passed that same rock |

## Dusthollow ↔ The Bones

**Distance**: 2 hours on horseback
**Terrain**: Grassland transitioning to arid badlands

### The Journey

> The green fades as you ride north. Grass gives way to scrub, then bare earth the color of dried blood. Rock formations rise from the ground like broken teeth. The air gets drier, hotter. The horizon shimmers.
>
> This is outlaw country. Everyone knows it. The respectable folk of Dusthollow don't come out here unless they have to.

### Landmarks

- **The Last Fence**: Where the ranching territory officially ends. Beyond this, no law.
- **Sentinel Rock**: A tall spire visible for miles. Navigation point.
- **The Dry Riverbed**: An ancient river, now bone-dry. Easy travel through the wash.
- **The Warning Cairn**: A pile of stones. No one remembers who built it or why.

### Terrain Hazards

| Hazard | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Loose scree | DC 12 Dex save or horse stumbles (no damage, lose 10 min) |
| Narrow passage | Single file only. Ambush territory. |
| No water | After 4 hours without water, Constitution saves begin |
| Rattlesnakes | DC 12 Perception to notice before stepping on one |

### What Might Happen

| d6 | Travel Encounter |
|----|------------------|
| 1 | Calico Boys scout watching from a ridge |
| 2 | Abandoned campsite—still warm |
| 3 | Vultures circling something dead |
| 4 | A prospector's mule, wandering alone—owner unknown |
| 5 | Strange petroglyphs on a rock face—spirals |
| 6 | You reach Sentinel Rock faster than expected. Or slower. |

## Dusthollow ↔ Silas Canyon

**Distance**: 2.5 hours on horseback (through The Bones)
**Terrain**: Badlands, then box canyon

### The Journey

> Past the Bones proper, a narrow defile opens in the rock face. The walls close in, red and orange stone streaked with mineral deposits. Sound echoes strangely. Every hoofbeat comes back wrong.
>
> The canyon is beautiful and deeply unsettling. Like a place that wasn't meant to be found.

### Landmarks

- **The Narrows**: Barely wide-enough passage. Classic ambush point.
- **Petroglyph Wall**: Ancient spirals carved into the rock. They seem to move in peripheral vision.
- **The Hidden Overhang**: Calico Boys' fallback camp. Hard to spot (DC 16 Perception).
- **The Dead End**: Where the canyon terminates at a sheer cliff. Or so it appears.

### The Dead End (Hidden)

> The canyon ends at a wall of red rock, two hundred feet high. Impassable.
>
> But something about it bothers you. The wall looks... flat. Too flat. Too perfect. And when you stare at it too long, your eyes water and you feel compelled to look away.

- DC 14 Wisdom (Insight): Strong urge to turn around. Something is wrong with this place.
- Stage 3+ Characters: See geometric patterns shimmering in the rock. This is the edge of the Reach.

## Dusthollow ↔ Other Edges

### East: The Open Prairie

**Distance**: 4 hours... that never ends

> You ride east across open grassland. The sun moves in the sky. Hours pass. And somehow, you find yourself approaching Dusthollow from the west.

Players who try to leave this way end up back where they started. They don't notice unless they make a DC 16 Wisdom save. Even then, they simply feel "turned around."

### South: The Mountain Pass

**Distance**: 3 hours to the "avalanche"

> A road winds into the mountains, promising passage to... somewhere. But two hours in, the way is blocked by a massive rockfall. Impassable. Must have happened recently.

The avalanche is always "recent." Locals accept this without question. There's no way through—the Reach ends here.

- DC 15 Investigation: The rocks are too perfectly placed. Almost arranged.
- Stage 3+ Characters: The "avalanche" shimmers faintly. It's not real. But neither is there anything beyond it.

### West: The River Road

**Distance**: 3 hours to the "bridge out"

> A road follows a lazy river toward distant woods. Pleasant travel until you reach the bridge—or what's left of it. Collapsed. Water too deep to ford.

Same as the mountains. The bridge is always recently collapsed. No one thinks to question it.

## Travel Atmosphere by Time

### Morning Travel
> The world is fresh, the air cool. Dew on the grass. Long shadows stretching west. A sense of possibility, of new beginnings. The same new beginning as every other morning.

### Afternoon Travel
> Hot. Dusty. The sun beats down without mercy. Everything shimmers in the heat—and not just from temperature. Mirages flicker at the edges of vision.

### Evening Travel
> Golden light paints the landscape. Shadows grow long. There's a melancholy beauty to it—the day ending, another cycle completing. The sunset is spectacular. It's always spectacular.

### Night Travel
> Not recommended. The trails are treacherous, the predators active. And the sky... there's something wrong with the sky. No stars. Just endless black. Most travelers don't notice. Awakening ones can't stop noticing.

## Connection to the Loom Sanctum

There is no physical path to the Loom Sanctum from within the Reach. It exists outside the demiplane.

### Access Points
- **Loom Frames** (hidden maintenance portals): One beneath the Bones, one under the chapel, one in the collapsed caves south.
- **The Quieting**: During reset, badly damaged Woven are pulled to the Sanctum automatically.
- **Vane's Override**: The Architect can transport individuals directly.
- **Full Awakening**: Stage 4 Woven can perceive and potentially access the Loom Frames.

### What It Looks Like (If Accessed)
> One moment you're in the Reach. The next, you're standing in a corridor of white stone and glowing conduits. The air smells wrong—sterile, mechanical. Through glass panels, you see the Reach below, like looking at a painting from behind the canvas.

## DM Notes: Travel as Atmosphere

Travel scenes serve three purposes:

1. **Pacing**: Give breathing room between intense scenes
2. **Atmosphere**: Build the western frontier feel
3. **Foreshadowing**: Plant glitch seeds for later payoff

Don't make every journey eventful. Sometimes the quiet monotony reinforces the loop—the sameness of existence that the Woven don't notice but players might.
