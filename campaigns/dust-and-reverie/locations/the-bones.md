# The Bones

> *"They call it the Bones because that's all you'll leave behind if you're not careful."*

## Overview

A sprawling badlands north of [[dusthollow]]—a maze of canyons, mesas, and sun-scorched rock formations. The terrain is hostile, the water scarce, and the inhabitants hostile. This is where outlaws hide, where ancient secrets lie buried, and where the edge of the world grows thin.

## Atmosphere

- **Sounds**: Wind through canyons, rattlesnakes, distant coyotes, eerie silence
- **Smells**: Dry dust, sage, sun-baked rock
- **Sights**: Red and orange rock formations, skeletal remains of animals, abandoned mines
- **Feel**: Exposed, watched, timeless

## Key Locations

### The Calico Hideout
A network of caves where the **Calico Boys** gang makes their base. Led by **Joss Harlow**, they're a standard outlaw gang—robbing, rustling, occasionally killing. They're Woven on a villain Thread, but Joss has been glitching lately...

### Vulture's Roost
A high mesa offering views across the Bones. The Ashen Rider has been seen here, watching. Ancient petroglyphs on the rocks depict the Spiral—placed by Orenthal Vane as a clue.

### The Dry Creek Mine
An "abandoned" silver mine that goes deep. At the lowest level, concealed behind a cave-in, is a **Loom Frame**—one of the machines that maintains the Reach. Heavy security constructs guard it.

### The Devil's Garden
A forest of hoodoo rock formations that create a natural maze. Woven say it's haunted—actually, reality is thin here, and voices from other Threads (past narrative versions) bleed through.

### The Boundary
The northern edge of the Reach. Woven feel an instinctive aversion to approaching. The world becomes geometrically wrong—repeating patterns, impossible distances. Awakened characters can perceive a shimmering wall.

## Hidden Details

### The Mine's Secret
The Dry Creek Mine contains:
- **Level 1**: Standard mine tunnels, old equipment, some silver ore
- **Level 2**: Older tunnels, strange smooth walls (Consortium construction)
- **Level 3**: The Loom Frame chamber, guarded by 2 Shield Guardians disguised as "cave golems"

Destroying or damaging the Loom Frame causes a localized reality collapse—everything within 100 feet experiences violent glitches, and Consortium security is alerted.

### Petroglyph Locations
Ancient-looking (actually placed by Vane) spiral symbols appear at:
- Vulture's Roost
- The entrance to Devil's Garden
- The deepest point of the mine
- These glow faintly for awakening characters

## Travel

- Dusthollow to the Bones edge: 4 hours on horseback
- Navigating to the Hideout: DC 14 Survival check, or get lost (1d4 hours wasted)
- Finding the mine: Requires a guide, clue, or DC 18 Investigation
