# Dusthollow - Detailed Locations

Supplementary details for locations within and around [[dusthollow]].

## Brennan's Livery

> *"Horses don't judge. That's why I like 'em better than people."*

### Description
A large stable and corral at the east end of town. Smells of hay, leather, and horse. The building is weathered but well-maintained—Old Brennan takes pride in his animals.

### Atmosphere
- Hay dust floating in sunbeams
- Soft nickering of horses
- Creak of saddle leather
- Flies buzzing lazily

### Notable Features
- **Main Stable**: 12 stalls, usually 8-10 occupied
- **Tack Room**: Saddles, harnesses, tools
- **Hayloft**: Good hiding spot, overlooks the street
- **Corral**: Breaking pen for new horses
- **Brennan's Quarters**: Small room in the back

### Brennan
Old, half-deaf, observant despite appearances. Knows everyone's comings and goings because he sees who rides in and out. Simple Woven, no awakening potential, but useful for information if players are patient.

### Uses in Play
- **Cover during fights**: Hay bales, stalls, hayloft
- **Information gathering**: Brennan notices strangers
- **Ambush location**: Session 2's trap could happen here
- **Horse acquisition**: Standard rates apply

## Silas Canyon

> *"They say outlaws hide in the canyon. They say worse things hide deeper."*

### Description
A winding box canyon in [[the-bones]], about two hours' ride north of Dusthollow. Red rock walls, sparse vegetation, and an unsettling echo quality. The Calico Boys use it as an escape route; older legends speak of stranger things.

### Atmosphere
- Walls that turn sound strange
- Shadows that don't match the sun's angle
- Occasional petroglyphs (spirals among them)
- Feeling of being watched

### Notable Features
- **The Narrows**: Tight passage where ambushes happen
- **Hidden Overhang**: Calico Boys' fallback camp
- **The Dead End**: Canyon terminates at a sheer cliff—or does it? (Boundary shimmer visible to Stage 3+ characters)
- **Petroglyph Wall**: Ancient-looking spirals carved in stone

### Uses in Play
- **Ambush location**: The Rider could trap PCs here
- **Chase scene**: Fleeing bandits head for the canyon
- **Discovery**: Spirals hint at the truth
- **Boundary encounter**: Stage 3+ PCs can see the world's edge here

### Hidden Detail
The "dead end" is actually the edge of the Reach. Unawakened Woven feel compelled to turn back ("nothing worth seeing"). Awakened characters see geometric patterns in the cliff face—the demiplane's boundary.

## Mirela's Ranch

> *"It's not much, but it's home. Has been as long as I can remember."*

### Description
A small homestead twenty minutes' ride from Dusthollow proper. Modest house, a barn, chicken coop, and a few acres of grazing land. Everything is well-worn but cared for.

### Atmosphere
- Peaceful in the morning light
- Windmill creaking
- Distant lowing of cattle
- The smell of wildflowers and dust

### Layout
- **Main House**: Two rooms. Kitchen/living area and a small bedroom.
- **Porch**: Where Mirela often sits, drawing spirals she doesn't understand
- **Barn**: Storage, a few horses, hay
- **The Well**: Deep. Echoes strangely. (Loop voices audible to Stage 2+ characters)
- **Holt's Grave**: Behind the house. He's not actually buried there—he's alive. But the grave exists, a fragment from a different narrative thread.

### Holt (Mirela's "Father")
Simple farmer Woven. Kind, hardworking, protective of Mirela. Minimal dialogue, minimal personality. He exists to give Mirela something to care about. No awakening potential.

**Hidden Detail**: Holt has "died" in previous threads. Some versions of the narrative have Mirela avenging his death. The grave is a remnant from one such version that was never fully cleaned up.

### Uses in Play
- **Session 1**: Where Mirela lives; potential visit after the Calico fight
- **Session 2**: Ambush location option; or where Mirela hides after glitching
- **Session 3**: Could serve as a rallying point before the finale
- **Emotional stakes**: What happens to Holt if Mirela awakens? Can she leave him behind?

## The Dusthollow Jail

> *"Funny thing about this jail—never seems to hold anyone long."*

### Description
Attached to the sheriff's office. Two cells with iron bars, a desk for the deputy, and a weapons locker. Currently unmanned—the sheriff position is vacant after the last one "left town" (actually pulled for maintenance after a glitch).

### Atmosphere
- Dust motes in barred window light
- Creaking floorboards
- An untuned clock that keeps stopping at 3:47

### Notable Features
- **Two Cells**: Solid construction. Could hold Joss or other prisoners.
- **Weapons Locker**: 2 sparkcarbines, 4 sparkpistols, ammunition
- **Desk**: Deputy Desmond Hale sometimes works here
- **Wanted Posters**: The same faces keep appearing. Players may notice familiar names.

### Hidden Detail
The clock stopped at 3:47 during a major reality glitch six "months" ago. No one has been able to fix it. Most Woven have stopped noticing.

### Uses in Play
- **Hold prisoners**: Joss, bandits, or even PCs
- **Weapons acquisition**: Locker can be accessed
- **Desmond connection**: He uses this space
- **Clue location**: Wanted posters repeat; the clock is wrong

## The Water Tower

> *"Best view in town, if you can make the climb."*

### Description
Tallest structure in Dusthollow. A wooden tank atop a skeletal frame, providing water pressure to the town. The ladder is rickety but functional.

### Atmosphere
- Wind whistles through the frame
- The tank creaks and settles
- Unobstructed view in all directions
- At night, the stars seem... wrong

### Uses in Play
- **Sniper position**: Overlooks main street
- **Observation point**: See the whole town, plan movements
- **Revelation location**: From here, Stage 3+ characters can see the Reach's boundaries at the horizon—a faint shimmer where the world ends
- **Dramatic confrontation**: Final standoff location

### Hidden Detail
At night, observant characters (DC 16 Perception) notice there are no stars. The sky is simply black. This is one of the earliest clues that the world isn't real.
