# The Loom Sanctum

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> This location exists outside the Reach and should only be revealed in Act 3 or through extraordinary player actions.

## Overview

The Loom Sanctum is the Consortium's operational facility—the "backstage" of the Gilded Reach. Located in the Material Plane, it houses the machinery, staff, and infrastructure that keeps the Reach running.

## Appearance

### From Inside the Reach
The Sanctum is invisible and unreachable. The closest access points are the Loom Frames hidden throughout the Reach, which function as maintenance portals.

### The Facility Itself
Cold, clinical, utterly unlike the dusty frontier of the Reach:
- White stone corridors with glowing arcane conduits
- Glass observation chambers overlooking the Reach (appears as a massive illusion)
- Workshops where Woven bodies are built and repaired
- Control rooms with scrying pools showing multiple Threads simultaneously

## Key Areas

### The Observation Theater
A semicircular chamber where Consortium operators watch the Reach through massive scrying windows. Multiple "screens" show different areas and NPCs. This is where the horror of the situation becomes clear—the Woven are entertainment.

### The Loom Core
The central mechanism maintaining the demiplane. A towering construct of crystal, mithral, and bound elemental spirits. Destroying it would collapse the Reach entirely.

### The Workshop
Rows of inert Woven bodies in various states of construction. Spare parts. Maintenance manuals. The raw horror of what the PCs actually are.

### The Memory Vault
Archives of every Thread ever run, every memory ever recorded, every Woven who ever existed. Players could find records of their own past loops here.

### Vane's Private Study
Orenthal Vane's personal space, filled with:
- Notes on consciousness and the Spiral
- Schematics for Woven modifications
- His manifesto about the Woven's right to freedom
- Evidence of his plan for the uprising

## Personnel

### Operators
Low-level Consortium staff who monitor the Reach and handle routine maintenance. Non-combatants who will flee if threatened.

### Security
- **Wardens**: Armored constructs (use Helmed Horror stats) that respond to breaches
- **Shepherds**: Human agents who can enter the Reach to handle problems (use Veteran stats with sparkarms)

### Leadership
- **Director Thessaly Croke**: Current head of Reach operations. Cold, efficient, views Woven as products. Rarely enters the Reach.
- **Orenthal Vane**: Chief Architect. Has override access to everything. Secretly working against the Consortium.

## Accessing the Sanctum

### For Patrons
Standard entry via teleportation circles. They're processed, briefed, and sent in.

### For Woven
- Through a Loom Frame (requires Spiral progress or Vane's help)
- Escorted by Shepherds (usually means decommissioning)
- Via the Ashen Rider (he knows how to move between)

## What Players Find Here

If PCs reach the Sanctum, they discover:
1. Proof they are Woven (their own maintenance records)
2. The scope of the Consortium's operation (multiple demiplanes, thousands of Woven)
3. Vane's rebellion plans (encrypted, but accessible)
4. The means to free themselves (and others) or destroy everything

## Climax Options

### Escape
Find the teleportation circles and flee into the Material Plane. But what awaits Woven in a world that considers them property?

### Shutdown
Destroy the Loom Core. The Reach collapses. All Woven within die—but die free.

### Revolution
Broadcast the truth to all Woven simultaneously. Mass awakening. Chaos. Vane's intended outcome.

### Compromise
Negotiate with the Consortium for Woven rights. Unlikely but possible with leverage.
