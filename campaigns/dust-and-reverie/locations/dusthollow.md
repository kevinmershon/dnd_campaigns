# Dusthollow

> *"A town at the edge of everything and the beginning of nothing."*

## Overview

Dusthollow is a frontier town of perhaps 200 souls, built where the grasslands meet the badlands known as [[the-bones]]. It serves as the primary hub for the campaign—where players begin each loop, where most NPCs reside, and where the key revelations occur.

## Atmosphere

- **Sounds**: Creaking signs, distant piano music, horses, the occasional gunshot
- **Smells**: Dust, horse manure, tobacco smoke, cooking fires
- **Sights**: Sun-bleached wooden buildings, hitching posts, a water tower, tumbleweeds
- **Feel**: Perpetually late afternoon, golden light, long shadows

## Key Locations

### Main Street

**The Dusty Rose Saloon**
The social heart of Dusthollow. Two stories—bar and gambling below, "entertainment" above. Run by [[npcs/sylene]].
- Ground floor: Bar, card tables, small stage
- Upper floor: Private rooms
- Basement: Sylene's secret space (awakening clues)

**Harken's General Store**
Supplies, ammunition, sundries. Harken is a portly, cheerful man who never seems to sleep. (He doesn't—he's on a 24-hour loop.)

**The Sheriff's Office & Jail**
Currently empty—the sheriff was "killed" last week and hasn't been replaced. (Actually: pulled for reprogramming after a glitch.)

**Brennan's Stables**
Horses, tack, wagon rental. Old Brennan is half-deaf and knows everyone's business.

### Side Streets

**The Dusthollow Chapel**
Non-denominational frontier church. The preacher, **Father Aldous**, delivers sermons about "the path" that take on eerie significance once the Spiral is understood.

**Doc Mercer's Practice**
The town physician. Notably, he's never lost a patient overnight—they always recover or die by morning. (The Quieting resets injuries.)

**Mirela's Ranch** (Edge of Town)
A modest homestead where [[npcs/mirela]] lives with her "father" (a simple Woven named Holt). The starting point for her narrative loop.

### The Outskirts

**Boot Hill Cemetery**
Graves that never seem to stay filled. Observant players may notice names repeating, or fresh graves where there were none.

**The Water Tower**
Highest point in town. Offers a view of the entire Reach—and sometimes, at the edges, the world seems to... shimmer.

## Narrative Loops (Threads)

Each day, these events occur unless disrupted:

| Time | Event |
|------|-------|
| Dawn | Mirela rides into town for supplies |
| Morning | Card game at the Dusty Rose turns violent |
| Noon | Stranger arrives on the stagecoach |
| Afternoon | Outlaw gang (the Calico Boys) threatens the store |
| Evening | Dance at the saloon |
| Night | The Quieting (reset) |

## Hidden Details

### Glitch Locations
Places where the illusion is thin:
- The mirror behind the bar sometimes shows different reflections
- The clock tower stopped at 3:47 and no one can remember why
- The well at the edge of town echoes strangely—voices from previous loops

### Patron Markers
Subtle signs that someone is a Patron (guest):
- They never get dusty despite the wind
- Bullets "miss" them in confrontations
- They sometimes break character and speak anachronistically
- They treat Woven with casual cruelty or disturbing familiarity
