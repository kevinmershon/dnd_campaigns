# The Gilded Reach

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> This document contains campaign secrets.

## The Truth

The Gilded Reach is not a real place. It is a **constructed demiplane**—a pocket dimension approximately 50 miles in diameter, created and maintained by the [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Loom Consortium]].

## Physical Properties

### Boundaries
The Reach has defined edges that cannot be crossed:
- **The Shimmer**: At the boundaries, reality becomes hazy. Woven who approach feel compelled to turn back ("nothing interesting that way").
- **Impossible Geography**: Paths loop back on themselves. A three-day ride east somehow returns you to Dusthollow.
- **Awakened Perception**: Those who've progressed on the Spiral can see the boundaries as walls of softly glowing geometric patterns.

### The Sky
The sun rises and sets on a perfect schedule. There are no stars—the night sky is simply black. Awakening Woven may notice this with a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check.

### Weather
Weather follows a predictable schedule:
- Clear skies with occasional dust devils (default)
- Thunderstorm every 7th day
- Never snows, never truly cold

## The Substrate

Beneath the illusion of reality, the Reach is built on **Loom Frames**—massive magical constructs that project and maintain the demiplane. These are hidden in:
- The mine beneath [[the-bones]]
- The basement of the chapel in [[dusthollow]]
- The "collapsed" cave system to the south

Damaging a Loom Frame causes localized reality glitches—flickering textures, frozen NPCs, sound distortion.

## Entering and Exiting

### For Patrons
- Entry: Through the **Loom Sanctum**, a facility in the Material Plane. Patrons are physically transported via teleportation circles.
- Exit: Speaking a command phrase ("I wish to wake") or being escorted out by Consortium staff.

### For Woven
- Woven cannot leave. They don't know there's anywhere to go.
- Awakened Woven might find the Loom Frames and attempt to exit, but doing so triggers Consortium security.

## Time Dilation

One day in the Reach equals roughly one hour in the Material Plane. A two-week "vacation" for a Patron is about a day of real time.

## Maintenance

The Reach requires constant upkeep:
- **The Quieting**: Nightly reset of Woven memories and injuries
- **Thread Updates**: Narrative adjustments pushed by Consortium story designers
- **Retrieval**: Badly damaged Woven are pulled for repair/replacement

## What Players Experience

Initially: A convincing frontier world. Harsh but fair.

As they awaken:
- The edges feel wrong
- The sky seems too perfect
- The same events keep almost happening
- They remember things they shouldn't

## GM Notes

Use the Reach's artificial nature to create mounting dread:
1. **Subtle**: Repeated NPC phrases, déjà vu moments
2. **Noticeable**: Same stranger arrives on multiple days, impossible geography
3. **Undeniable**: Frozen moments, reality glitches, seeing the boundaries
