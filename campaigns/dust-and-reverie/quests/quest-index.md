# Quest Index

> *"Every story in Dusthollow has been told a thousand times. The question is whether anyone will remember it differently this time."*

## Overview

These are the "loops"—narrative storylines that [[lore/the-woven|Woven]] run and Patrons can engage with. PCs can stumble into these naturally, be hired for them, or discover them through exploration.

Each quest has:
- **Surface Story**: What it appears to be
- **Hidden Truth**: What's really happening (DM only)
- **Glitch Opportunities**: Ways it can reveal the nature of reality
- **Related Encounters**: Combat or social encounters the quest leads to

## Quest Availability by Session

### Session 1
Introduce 1-2 hooks naturally:
- [[quests/the-bounty-hunt|The Bounty Hunt]] (Corwin approaches after the showdown)
- [[quests/the-gold-escort|The Gold Escort]] (Harken mentions it as thanks)
- [[quests/the-card-sharps-gambit|The Card Sharp's Gambit]] (evening at the saloon)

### Session 2
Escalate with mystery hooks:
- [[quests/pearls-missing-sister|Pearl's Missing Sister]] (Pearl approaches privately)
- [[quests/the-widows-vigil|The Widow's Vigil]] (Widow Parsons pleads for help)
- [[quests/doc-mercers-cases|Doc Mercer's Cases]] (PCs notice the patterns)
- [[quests/kobold-hunting|Kobold Hunting]] (if exploring the Bones)

### Session 3
Hooks become secondary to main plot, but can provide:
- Evidence for the truth
- Allies for the finale
- Alternative paths to revelation

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Using Quests

Quests serve multiple purposes:
1. **Sandbox content** between main plot beats
2. **Spiral Point sources** through glitch opportunities
3. **NPC development** by reusing established characters
4. **Worldbuilding** that reinforces the loop nature of the Reach

### Quest vs Encounter

- **Quests** are narrative threads players choose to pursue
- **Encounters** are specific scenes with mechanics (combat, social, etc.)
- Quests often *lead to* encounters but aren't the same thing

### Parallel Stories

Several quests mirror each other thematically:
- Pearl and Widow Parsons both search for people who don't exist
- The Bounty Hunt and Gold Escort both involve the Calico Boys
- Father Aldous and Mirela both contain hidden Spiral clues
