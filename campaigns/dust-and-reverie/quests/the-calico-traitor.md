## The Hook

A woman in dusty trail clothes finds you outside town. She's nervous, keeps checking over her shoulder:

> "I'm Della. I ride with [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Harlow]]. But I want out."
>
> She spits. "The things we do—they're getting worse. And something's wrong with Joss. He's been talking about dying. About remembering dying. It's got the boys spooked."
>
> "I've got money hidden. Two hundred gold. Get me out of the Bones alive, and it's yours."

---

## The Job

### Objectives
1. Secretly meet Della at the [[locations/the-bones#The Calico Hideout|hideout]]
2. Extract her without alerting the gang
3. Get her to safety (Dusthollow or beyond)

### Approach Options
- **Stealth**: Sneak in at night (DC 14 Stealth per PC)
- **Distraction**: Create a diversion (DC 16 Deception)
- **Direct**: Fight through the gang (see Combat below)
- **Negotiation**: Talk to Joss directly (see Joss's State below)

---

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

Della's desire to escape is genuine—but she can't actually leave. The [[locations/the-gilded-reach|Gilded Reach]] has boundaries. "Beyond" doesn't exist. If she travels far enough, she'll hit the edge and loop back.

Her Thread is to be a member of the Calico Boys. She can leave the gang, but she can't leave the Reach.

---

## Complications

### Pike's Suspicion
Pike suspects betrayal. He's watching Della closely.
- DC 15 Stealth to avoid his patrols
- DC 14 Deception if confronted

### Joss's State
[[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss]] is glitching badly. He's confused, sometimes sympathetic, sometimes dangerous.

> He stares at Della with hollow eyes. "You want to leave? Go ahead. What does it matter? We'll all be back here tomorrow anyway."
>
> He laughs—bitter, broken. "I've died three times this week. I remember it. Every time."

DC 14 Persuasion: Joss lets Della go without a fight.

### The Twins
The Twins (Colt and Cole) are simple but loyal. They don't understand what's happening to Joss, but they'll fight to protect the gang.

## The Escape Attempt

If PCs successfully extract Della and travel toward the edge:

> You ride for hours. The landscape should be changing—new territory, new horizons. But something feels wrong.
>
> Della pulls up short. "Wait. I know that rock formation." She points. "We passed it two hours ago."
>
> You've been traveling in a straight line. You haven't turned. But you're back where you started.
>
> Della's voice cracks. "There's no 'beyond,' is there? There's just... this."

## Resolution Options

### Extract Della
- 200 gp reward
- She's free of the gang but trapped in the Reach
- "Where do I go now?"

### Failed Extraction
- Combat with Calico Boys
- Della is recaptured or killed
- She resets the next day

### Talk to Joss
- DC 14 Persuasion: He lets her go
- He's too broken to care
- May provide information about his deaths

### Explain the Truth
- Della breaks down when she realizes there's no escape
- The dream of "beyond" was all she had
- May become an ally or enemy depending on how it's handled

>
> She looks at you. "What do you do when you find out the cage goes on forever?"

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This quest demonstrates that escape is impossible—not because of guards or walls, but because the world itself prevents leaving. The Reach is a closed system.

### Della's Arc
Della genuinely wants freedom. She's not a plant or a trap. Her tragedy is that freedom doesn't exist in the Reach. She can change gangs, change towns, but she can't leave.

### Joss as Ally
If PCs talk to Joss, they may find a broken man willing to help. He's too far gone to fight, but he's also too far gone to care about gang loyalty. He might become an unlikely ally in later sessions.

### The Loop-Back
The "loop back" effect—traveling in a straight line and ending up where you started—is a key feature of the Boundary. Woven can't leave; they can only think they're leaving.
