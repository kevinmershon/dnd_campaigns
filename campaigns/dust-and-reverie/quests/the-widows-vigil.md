## The Hook

[[characters/npcs/saloon-regulars#Widow Parsons|Widow Parsons]] clutches your arm with surprising strength:

> "Please. My husband, Tobias. He went to the [[locations/the-bones#The Dry Creek Mine|mine]] three days ago. He should have been back by now."
>
> Her eyes fill with tears. "Everyone else has given up. But I know he's out there. Please find him."

---

## The Job

### Objectives
1. Search the Dry Creek Mine
2. Find Tobias Parsons
3. Bring him home or bring closure

### Investigation Options

**Town Records** (DC 14 Investigation):
- No marriage certificate for anyone named Parsons
- No property deed in Tobias's name

**Old Jem** (DC 12 Persuasion):
> "Parsons? Don't recall the name. Then again, I don't recall much."

**Mine Records** (DC 16 Investigation):
- No employee named Tobias Parsons
- No work history, no accident report, nothing

**[[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]]** (if trusted):
> "There are ghosts in this town. People who should exist but don't. People waiting for things that will never come."

---

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

Tobias Parsons doesn't exist—not anymore. He was a background [[lore/the-woven|Woven]] removed during a Thread update. The mine's records show no one by that name. No one remembers him.

But Widow Parsons's programming was never adjusted. She'll wait forever for someone who was erased from reality.

## The Widow's Home

If PCs visit her house:

> The cottage is small but well-kept. Flowers in the window. Fresh bread cooling on the counter.
>
> But something is wrong.
>
> There's a chair by the fire—a man's chair, sized for someone larger than the widow. It's positioned as if someone should be sitting there. But there's no cushion. No pipe on the side table. No boots by the door.
>
> The space is perfectly preserved for someone who isn't there.

**DC 14 Perception**: The wedding photos on the mantle show only the widow. No groom. Just... empty space beside her, as if someone was erased.

---

## The Mine Search

If PCs search the Dry Creek Mine:

> The mine is operational—workers nod as you pass, picks ring against stone. But no one knows Tobias Parsons.
>
> "Check the foreman's records," one miner suggests. "He's got everyone who ever worked here."
>
> The foreman checks his ledger, running a finger down endless names. His brow furrows.
>
> "No Parsons. Never has been. You sure you got the right mine?"

---

## Helping Her Remember

If PCs attempt DC 18 Insight to help her remember:

> Something flickers in her eyes. A moment of clarity cutting through the fog.
>
> "He was... taken. During the smoothing." Her voice is distant, not her own. "They said he was too complex. Too many connections. They removed him to simplify the Thread."
>
> She blinks. "What was I saying?"
>
> The memory is gone. But for a moment, she knew.

## Resolution Options

### Search the Mine
- Find nothing about Tobias
- May discover [[locations/the-bones#Loom Frame Access|Loom Frame]] components
- Widow is grateful for the effort

### Tell the Truth
- Widow Parsons breaks down
- She may refuse to believe, becoming hostile
- Or she may find strange peace in understanding

### Fabricate Closure
- "He died peacefully in a cave-in. He didn't suffer."
- She weeps but finds closure
- It's a lie, but a kind one

### Help Her Remember
- DC 18 Insight triggers a flash of true memory
- She briefly understands, then forgets
- The fragment is enough for +3 Spiral Points

## Widow Parsons's Breakdown

If confronted with the truth:

> "You're lying." Her voice is steel. "We were married for fifteen years. We built that house together. We—"
>
> She stops. Tries to remember his face. His voice. The color of his eyes.
>
> Nothing. Just feelings without content. Love without an object.
>
> "What did they do to him?" she whispers. "What did they do to me?"

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This quest demonstrates that the [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] can edit people out of existence. Tobias was removed, but the Widow wasn't updated—a maintenance error that left her broken.

### Parallels with Pearl
Pearl searches for someone who never existed; Widow Parsons searches for someone who was erased. Both quests reveal the artificial nature of memory and identity in the Reach.

### Thread Updates
"Smoothings" are periodic narrative updates where the Consortium removes unnecessary complexity. Tobias was cut because his Thread didn't add enough value. The Widow was supposed to forget him. She didn't.

### The Wedding Ring
The blank ring is a powerful symbol. It should have his name inscribed. It has nothing. The Consortium erased the object, not just the person.
