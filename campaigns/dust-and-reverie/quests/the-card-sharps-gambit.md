## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

The "Patron" is real—an actual [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] client playing the gambling narrative. He views Clara and the other players as entertainment. Cheating him is dangerous; winning openly is a blow to his ego.

This is one of the few opportunities to interact directly with a Patron and observe their unusual nature.

## The Patron's Immunity

If violence erupts and someone attacks the Patron:

> Your blade passes through him—no, not through. Around. The air bends, reality shivers, and somehow your weapon is simply... elsewhere.
>
> The Patron laughs. "That's not in the script, friend."
>
> He adjusts his cuffs. "I think I'll fold for tonight. But I'll be back tomorrow. I always am."
>
> He walks out. No one tries to stop him. No one can.

This is absolute immunity—Patrons cannot be harmed by Woven. Even fully awakened characters cannot directly injure a Patron within the Reach.

## Resolution Options

### Help Clara Win
- 200 gp split (120 to Clara, 80 to PC)

- He may seek revenge in future encounters

### Expose Clara
- Patron grateful (50 gp reward)

### Attack the Patron
- Discover immunity (+3 Spiral Points)
- Patron leaves, amused
- No monetary reward
- Massive revelation about the nature of Patrons

### Walk Away
- Clara handles it alone (50% chance of success)
- If she fails, she's arrested
- If she succeeds, she remembers you refused

## Clara's Déjà Vu

During the game, Clara pauses:

> Her hand freezes mid-deal. Eyes distant.
>
> "I've played this hand before." She shakes her head. "Sorry. Feeling strange tonight."
>
> She deals. The exact same cards she was about to deal before she stopped.

She's running her loop. The game plays out the same way every time. Only PC interference changes the outcome.

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This quest introduces Patron immunity in a relatively safe context. The Patron is obnoxious but not dangerous—discovering he can't be harmed is shocking but not lethal.

### Clara's Loop
Clara has run this con hundreds of times. Sometimes she wins, sometimes she's caught. She doesn't remember, but her hands know every card. Her déjà vu is real—she's experiencing bleed-through from previous loops.

### Patron Behavior
The Patron treats the evening as entertainment—a vacation activity. He's aware he's in a simulation (or theme park, as he thinks of it) and acts accordingly. He's dismissive, cruel in small ways, and utterly unafraid.

### If PCs Attack
The immunity reveal is one of the most powerful demonstrations of the simulation's nature. Patrons exist outside the rules. This should feel wrong, impossible, terrifying.
