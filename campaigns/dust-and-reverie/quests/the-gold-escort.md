## The Hook

[[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Harken|Harken]] pulls PCs aside, nervous:

> "I've got a shipment coming in from the [[locations/the-bones#The Dry Creek Mine|Dry Creek Mine]]. Silver, mostly. Problem is, word's gotten out, and the [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow#The Calico Boys|Calico Boys]] will be waiting."
>
> He wrings his hands. "Fifty gold each to ride escort. More if you run into trouble."

---

## The Job

### Objectives
1. Meet the mine wagon at dawn
2. Escort through [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]
3. Deliver safely to [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Harken|Harken's]] store

### Travel
- 4-hour ride each way
- Wagon moves slowly (15 ft. speed)
- Ambush is inevitable—the Calico Boys always attack

### The Ambush

The Calico Boys attack at a narrow canyon pass.

**Enemies**:
| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Damage |
|----------|----|----|--------|--------|
| Calico Boy (x4) | 12 | 11 | +3 | 1d10+1 |
| Pike | 13 | 16 | +4 | 1d10+2 |

**Terrain**:
- Rocky outcrops (half cover)
- 20 ft. cliffs on either side
- Wagon provides full cover but can't move during combat

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

The "silver" shipment is cover for [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] supply runs—technical equipment, replacement parts, chemicals for the Loom Frames. The Calico Boys are programmed to attack; they *always* attack. This loop has run hundreds of times.

### The Real Cargo

The crates contain:
- Geometric crystals (Loom Frame components)
- Strange metallic tools with no obvious purpose
- Sealed containers that hum faintly
- Documentation in symbols no one recognizes

## Investigating the Cargo

If PCs examine the cargo:

| DC | Discovery |
|----|-----------|
| 12 | The crates are unusually heavy for their size |
| 14 | Strange symbols on the wood—not any known language |
| 16 | Technical equipment inside, not silver ore |
| 18 | Arcana: "Reality maintenance" components—whatever that means |

**If a crate breaks**:
> Inside, instead of silver ore, you find crystalline structures—perfect geometric shapes that catch the light wrong. They're not gems. They're not metal. They're something else entirely.
>
> The wagon driver looks nervous. "Don't... don't touch those. Just get them to [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Harken|Harken]]. He'll know what to do."

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This quest introduces the idea that something larger is operating behind the scenes. The "silver shipment" cover story falls apart under scrutiny.

### Harken's Role
[[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Harken|Harken]] doesn't know what he's transporting. His Thread includes receiving these shipments and storing them in his basement. [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] workers retrieve them during the Quieting.

### Connecting to Larger Plot
The cargo is genuine Loom Frame components. If PCs keep any:
- They glow faintly for awakening characters
- Can be used to locate Loom Frames (point toward nearest)
- [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] recognizes them immediately
