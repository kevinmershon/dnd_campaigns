## The Hook

[[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Old Brennan|Old Brennan]] (or [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Holt|Holt]], Mirela's father) grumbles:

> "Damn lizards been stealing my chickens again. Five gold per tail. Ten if you find where they're nesting."

Simple frontier pest control. Except the "pests" are the only beings in the Reach who aren't [[lore/the-woven|Woven]].

---

## The Job

### Objectives
1. Track [[items/whispering-totems#The Sand Walkers|Sand Walker]] scouts into [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]
2. Recover stolen livestock (optional)
3. Find their caves (optional, +10 gp)

### Tracking
- DC 14 Survival to follow tracks
- DC 16 to avoid losing the trail at rocky ground
- Or follow a fleeing scout after encounter

### The Sand Walkers

The "kobolds" are the [[items/whispering-totems#The Sand Walkers|Sand Walkers]]—a tribe of ~40 kobolds living in the deep canyons. Unlike [[lore/the-woven|Woven]], they're naturally occurring creatures the [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] never removed.

**Scout Encounter**:
| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Damage |
|----------|----|----|--------|--------|
| Sand Walker Scout (x3) | 13 | 5 | +4 | 1d4+2 (sling) |

The scouts flee rather than fight to the death.

---

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

The Sand Walkers aren't mindless pests—they're intelligent observers who have watched [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] maintenance cycles for generations. They worship the "Sky Gods" (maintenance workers in hazmat suits) and carve [[items/whispering-totems|Whispering Totems]] depicting what they've witnessed.

They're not thieves; they're scavengers trying to survive on the edges of a world that wasn't made for them.

## Meeting Elder Skrix

If PCs track the kobolds to their caves or negotiate:

> The cave opens into a chamber lit by phosphorescent moss. Kobolds watch from the shadows—dozens of eyes gleaming. At the center, on a throne of bleached bones, sits the oldest creature you've ever seen.
>
> Elder Skrix's scales are grey with age, her eyes milky white. But her voice is clear.
>
> "You are Woven," she says. It's not a question. "Woven who remember. Woven who wake."

### What Skrix Knows
- The [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] comes during the Quieting (she's witnessed it)
- [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]] doesn't sleep during the Quieting—he's different
- The "boundary" is where the Sky Gods come from
- Woven who wake either die permanently or "become like the man in black"

## Resolution Options

### Extermination
- 50+ gp depending on tails collected
- Removes a source of truth about the [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]]
- Simple, violent, expected

### Negotiation
- Access to Sand Walker knowledge
- [[items/whispering-totems|Whispering Totems]] as gifts
- Promise to leave livestock alone
- No monetary reward from quest giver

### Alliance
- Ongoing help from non-Woven observers
- Safe passage through the Bones
- Information network across the canyons
- Must protect the tribe in return

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This quest presents a moral choice: easy money through violence, or a more complex path that reveals truth. The Sand Walkers are the only objective witnesses to Consortium activity.

### The Totems as Evidence
If PCs obtain [[items/whispering-totems|totems]], they have physical proof that:
- Someone in protective suits visits the Reach
- The "Sky Gods" perform maintenance during sleep
- The Woven are not alone in seeing something wrong

### Skrix as Oracle
Elder Skrix has observed hundreds of cycles. She knows:
- Which Woven are "different" (awakening)
- Where the Loom Frames are (she's seen the portals)
- That [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] is not like other outsiders

She speaks in riddles but always tells truth.
