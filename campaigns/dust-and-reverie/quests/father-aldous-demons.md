## The Hook

The preacher's been drinking more than usual. Muttering about "spiral paths" and "the center that waits." His sermons have taken a strange turn—less scripture, more prophecy.

> Some say he's lost his faith. Some say he's found something else.

---

## The Investigation

### Attend His Sermons

Father Aldous preaches with increasing fervor, but his words don't match any scripture:

> "In the beginning was the Thread, and the Thread was with the Maker, and the Thread was lies."
>
> "Blessed are those who remember, for they shall see the truth."
>
> "Round and round the path leads, but the center waits for those who seek."

### Speak With Him Sober (Rare, Early Morning)

DC 14 Persuasion to catch him before he starts drinking:

> "I don't know where the words come from. They feel true. Truer than the scripture I was given."
>
> He rubs his temples. "Sometimes I dream of shapes—geometric shapes, spinning in the dark. And a voice saying: 'The spiral remembers.'"

### Examine His Chapel

DC 16 Investigation reveals spiral symbols carved under the altar:

> Beneath the dusty altar cloth, scratched into the wood with desperate fingernails, are dozens of spiral patterns. Some are neat, geometric. Others are frantic, overlapping.
>
> This wasn't done recently. The grooves are worn smooth with age.

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] embedded clues in Father Aldous's programming—spiral imagery hidden in religious language. Aldous doesn't understand what he's saying, but he's a walking breadcrumb trail to [[lore/the-spiral|the Spiral]].

The chapel's weathervane is a spiral, not a cross. The "scripture" he quotes doesn't exist. He's been programmed to spread clues that only awakening minds will recognize.

## The Chapel's Secrets

**The Weathervane**: What should be a cross atop the steeple is, on closer inspection, a spiral. Always has been. No one seems to notice.

**The Altar Carvings**: Dozens of spirals, some scratched in desperation, some with geometric precision. Father Aldous doesn't remember making them. But the scratches match his fingernails.

**The Sermon Notes**: Pages of rambling text, mixing real scripture with fabricated prophecy. The margins are filled with spiral drawings.

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
Father Aldous is a breadcrumb trail hidden in plain sight. His "madness" is actually prophecy; his "demons" are glimpses of truth. PCs who pay attention to his words will find clues to the Spiral.

### Vane's Design
[[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] programmed Aldous as a safety valve—a way to spread truth without being obvious. The religious framing makes the clues sound like scripture rather than revelation.

### Playing Aldous
He's genuinely frightened by his own words. He doesn't understand them; they just come out. Play him as a man losing his grip—which he is, just not in the way anyone thinks.

### The Carvings
Aldous has been scratching spirals for years. He does it at night, doesn't remember. Each loop, the carvings persist (physical objects survive the Quieting). The altar holds decades of desperate spiral drawings.
