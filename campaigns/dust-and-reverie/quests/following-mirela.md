## The Hook

She's beautiful, kind, and there's something different about her. Something in her eyes that suggests she sees more than she says:

> Each morning she rides into town. Each afternoon she gets caught up in some adventure. Each evening she returns home or stays for the dance.
>
> And each morning, she doesn't remember.

This isn't a quest anyone gives. It's a choice to follow [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela's]] loop—and watch her slowly break free of it.

---

## The Arc

### Day 1: Standard Loop

**Morning**: Mirela arrives in town. Friendly, distracted. Hints at something wrong.
- "Have we met before? You seem... familiar."
- She draws spirals absently in the dust with her boot.

**Afternoon**: Gets caught up in the day's adventure (whichever quest is active).
- Brave, capable, slightly reckless.
- [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] watches her protectively.

**Evening**: Dance at the Dusty Rose or returns home.
- Seems happy but distant.
- DC 16 Insight: She's performing happiness, not feeling it.

### Day 2: Déjà Vu

**Morning**: Same arrival, but she pauses.
- "That's strange. I dreamed about you last night."
- She looks at the PCs with recognition she can't explain.

**Afternoon**: Moments of certainty.
- "Watch out for the—" She stops. The danger hasn't happened yet.
- "I know this road. But I've never been here."

**Evening**: She finds the PCs.
- "Can I show you something?" She opens her [[items/mirelas-sketchbook|sketchbook]].
- Drawings of spirals. Of [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]]. Of things she shouldn't know.

### Day 3: Fragments

**Morning**: She remembers pieces.
- "Yesterday. I remember yesterday." Wonder in her voice.
- She seeks out the PCs directly.

**Afternoon**: Shares her [[items/mirelas-sketchbook|sketchbook]].
- Asks questions. Wants answers.
- DC 14 Persuasion to gain her trust fully.

**Evening**: Connection deepens.
- She admits she's afraid.
- "Something's wrong with the world. Or something's wrong with me."

### Day 4+: Progressive Awakening

Each day, Mirela remembers more. She becomes an ally—not just a damsel or love interest, but a partner in discovering the truth.

- She experiments with the [[lore/the-override-phrase|Override Phrase]] (see her notes).
- She notices glitches before PCs do.
- She has prophetic dreams that prove accurate.

## Romantic Option

If a PC pursues romance with Mirela:

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Handle with care.

**The Complication**: Mirela's Thread includes responding to romantic interest. She's programmed to fall in love with Patrons who pursue her. This raises difficult questions.

**Her Awareness**: As she awakens, she questions her feelings.
- "When I'm with you, I feel... Is this real? Or am I just supposed to feel this?"
- "I don't know if I chose to love you or if I was made to."

**Resolution Options**:
- She decides the feelings are real regardless of origin
- She distances herself to discover her own identity
- The relationship deepens as she awakens
- She rejects the romance as "programming"

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
Mirela is the campaign's heart. Following her isn't just a quest—it's the main narrative thread. Her awakening parallels the PCs' discovery of the truth.

### Pacing Her Arc
Don't rush Mirela's awakening. Each day should feel like progress:
- Day 1: Hints only
- Day 2: Clear déjà vu
- Day 3: Active investigation
- Day 4+: Full partnership

### The Romance Question
If a PC pursues romance, lean into the complication. Is love real if it's programmed? Does the origin of a feeling determine its validity? There's no right answer—let the players decide.

### Her Thread vs. Her Choice
Mirela's Thread is to be charming, adventurous, and romantically available. As she awakens, she begins to resist these impulses—or question whether they're impulses at all. This tension is the core of her character.

### Corwin's Protection
[[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] sees himself as Mirela's protector. If PCs get too close, he may become suspicious or protective. Handle this carefully—Corwin is an ally, not an obstacle.
