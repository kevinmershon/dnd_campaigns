## The Hook

[[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] approaches the PCs at the saloon after the [[encounters/calico-boys-showdown|Calico Boys Showdown]]:

> "I could use some help. There's a bounty on a man named Pike—one of [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss Harlow's]] boys. He killed a rancher out near [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]. Two hundred gold for his head."
>
> He pauses. "I'd go alone, but I promised [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]] I wouldn't take unnecessary risks. Riding with a group isn't a risk."

## The Job

### Objectives
1. Track Pike to the [[locations/the-bones#The Calico Hideout|Calico Hideout]]
2. Capture or kill him
3. Return to [[locations/dusthollow|Dusthollow]] for the reward

### Travel
- Half-day ride into [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]
- [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] knows the general area
- DC 14 Survival to find the hideout, or follow tracks

### The Hideout
A network of caves where the remaining Calico Boys are regrouping after [[encounters/calico-boys-showdown|the showdown]].

**Enemies Present**:

|----------|----|----|--------|--------|
| Pike | 13 | 16 | +4 | 1d10+2 |
| Calico Boy (x3) | 12 | 11 | +3 | 1d10+1 |

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

Pike didn't kill anyone—the "murder" is a scripted narrative hook that resets each loop. The rancher is fine the next morning. But the bounty feels real, the violence is real, and [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] seems... practiced at this. Too practiced.

This is a loop [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] has run countless times. He doesn't remember consciously, but his muscle memory knows every step.

## Resolution Options

### Kill Pike
- 200 gp reward
- [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin's]] respect
- Pike resets the next day (if PCs return to the area)

### Capture Pike
- 200 gp reward
- Opportunity to question him
- Pike is confused about "how many times" he's been caught

### Refuse the Quest
- [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] goes alone
- Returns injured but successful
- Less opportunity for glitch observation

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This is a straightforward "Western bounty" quest that introduces [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] as a capable ally. The glitch opportunities are subtle—easy to miss on a first pass.

### Corwin's Loop
[[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] has run this bounty dozens of times. He doesn't know it, but:
- His hand movements during the ride are identical each time
- He "predicts" terrain features before seeing them
- He knows Pike's fighting style intimately

### If PCs Return Later
After completing this quest, if PCs return to the hideout area in a later session:
- Pike is back (reset)
- The same bandits are there
- [[characters/npcs/corwin|Corwin]] might offer the same quest again

This can be a powerful "wait, what?" moment.
