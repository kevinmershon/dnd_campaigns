## The Hook

[[characters/npcs/dusty-rose-staff#Pearl|Pearl]] approaches when no one's watching:

> "I need to find someone. My sister, Opal. She was working at a saloon in Copperton, two days' ride east. Then the letters stopped."
>
> Her voice drops. "I have forty gold saved. I'll give you all of it. I just need to know what happened to her."

---

## The Job

### Objectives
1. Travel east toward Copperton
2. Find Opal or learn her fate
3. Return to Pearl with answers

### Investigation Options

**In Town**:
- DC 14 Investigation at town records: No mention of Copperton
- DC 12 Persuasion with [[characters/npcs/saloon-regulars#Old Jem|Old Jem]]: "Never heard of the place."
- DC 16 Investigation at library/general store: Maps show nothing east but the Bones

**Traveling East**:
- Each hour of travel, DC increases by 2 (instinctive aversion)
- DC 15 Wisdom save to continue after 4 hours
- Failure: Compelling urge to turn back, can't explain why

---

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

There is no Copperton. There is no Opal. The [[locations/the-gilded-reach|Gilded Reach]] ends at [[locations/the-bones#The Boundary|the Boundary]]—there is no "two days east."

Pearl's backstory includes a sister she's never actually had. The letters were manufactured memories. If PCs travel far enough, they'll hit the edge of reality.

---

## The Boundary

If PCs push through the aversion and travel far enough east:

> The landscape becomes... uncertain. Colors seem muted. The sun hangs at the same angle no matter how long you walk.
>
> And then you see it.
>
> The air ahead shimmers with geometric patterns—triangles and hexagons tessellating in impossible ways. It's not a wall. It's not a mirage. It's... nothing. The world simply ends.
>
> Behind the shimmer, you glimpse movement. Figures in strange suits. Machinery that hums with purpose.
>
> And beyond that—darkness. Infinite darkness, punctuated by distant stars that look like code.

## Resolution Options

### Reach the Boundary
- Major revelation (+3 Spiral Points)
- PCs see the Consortium maintenance area
- Changes everything they understand about the world

### Investigate Locally
- Find Copperton doesn't exist
- Pearl breaks down when confronted
- DC 16 Persuasion to comfort her: "But the letters felt so real..."

### Lie to Pearl
- "Opal moved on, started a new life"
- Pearl is satisfied but suspicious
- She may investigate herself later

### Tell Pearl the Truth
- Devastating for her
- DC 18 Insight: She briefly remembers "being given" the memories
- If she accepts: potential ally; if not: hostile and afraid

## Pearl's Breakdown

If confronted with the truth:

> Pearl's hands shake as she looks at the letters—letters she's read a hundred times.
>
> "But I remember her. I remember braiding her hair. I remember the day she left." Her voice cracks. "How can I remember something that never happened?"
>
> She looks at you with hollow eyes. "What am I?"

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This quest can be the most significant revelation in the campaign. The Boundary is concrete proof that the world is artificial. Handle this carefully—it changes everything.

### Pearl's Thread
Pearl was given the "missing sister" backstory to create emotional depth. The [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] does this routinely—manufactured tragedy makes Woven more compelling to Patrons.

### If PCs Avoid the Boundary
Even if they only investigate locally, finding that Copperton doesn't exist is a powerful clue. The Boundary is optional—impactful if reached, but not required.

### Connecting to Larger Plot
The Boundary is one of the most important locations in the campaign. If PCs see it, they can never un-see it. [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] knows about it; [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|the Rider]] guards it.
