## The Hook

PCs may notice this themselves, or [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Doc Mercer|Doc Mercer]] admits it under pressure:

> Something's strange about Doc Mercer's practice. Gunshot wounds that heal overnight. Patients who were dead on his table walking around the next morning. He doesn't advertise it—he doesn't even talk about it. But his records don't lie.

---

## The Investigation

### Accessing the Records (DC 14 Investigation or DC 12 Persuasion)

Mercer is protective of his files. He knows something is wrong but doesn't want to face it.

### What the Logs Show (DC 16 Investigation to notice patterns)

| Entry | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| "Jake Morrison, GSW to chest, 3:47 PM - expired" | Note: "Patient seen at breakfast this morning. Record unclear." |
| "Clara Dunn, thrown from horse, severe trauma" | "Deceased 2:15 PM" — Clara is serving drinks that evening |
| "Pike, multiple lacerations, blood loss" | "Did not survive. Third time this month." |

### Interview Survivors

The "survivors" don't remember dying:
> "Doc says I came in with a scratch. Don't remember much else. Felt tired that night, woke up fine."

---

## Hidden Truth

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Spoiler content below.

[[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Doc Mercer|Doc Mercer]] has unknowingly documented thousands of loop resets. His records show patients dying and recovering—not medically, but through the Quieting.

He's written it off as "frontier toughness." But deep down, he knows it's impossible.

## Confronting Doc Mercer

When pressed about his records:

> "I've been practicing medicine for—" He stops. Frowns. "How long have I been here? I can't... I can't remember a time before Dusthollow."
>
> He looks at his hands—hands that have stitched a thousand wounds.
>
> "People die in my office. And then they're fine. I tell myself it's a miracle. But miracles don't happen every day. Every single day."
>
> His voice drops to a whisper. "I've written Pike's death certificate three times this month. Three times. And he's out there right now, robbing coaches."

---

## The Records as Evidence

Doc Mercer's patient logs are physical proof of the loops:

> Stacks of leather-bound journals, going back... how far? The pages are filled with careful handwriting. Dates. Names. Injuries. Outcomes.
>
> And inconsistencies. So many inconsistencies.
>
> "Patient expired at 3:47 PM. Note: Patient seen at breakfast this morning. Record unclear."
>
> "Same injury, same patient, third occurrence this week."
>
> "I'm certain this is the same gunshot wound I treated yesterday. But yesterday's patient died."

---

## Resolution Options

### Take the Records
- Physical evidence of the loops (+3 Spiral Points)
- Mercer is relieved to have them gone
- Can be used to convince others of the truth

### Help Mercer Understand
- Potential ally (low awakening potential, but willing)
- He becomes a quiet observer, noting anomalies
- Provides medical aid and safe haven

### Destroy the Records
- Mercer thanks you, returns to denial
- He's happier not knowing
- Evidence is lost

### Expose the Records Publicly
- Town panic
- [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] attention
- Possible Shepherd intervention

## Doc Mercer's Awakening

If helped to understand:

> He sits heavily in his chair, staring at years of careful records—records that prove the impossible.
>
> "I'm not a doctor," he says finally. "I'm... I'm maintenance. I patch them up so they can die again."
>
> He looks up at you. "What does that make me?"

He won't fully awaken—he lacks the capacity—but he can be an ally. He'll watch for anomalies. Record them carefully. And when the time comes, he'll help.

## DM Notes

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Do not share with players under any circumstances.

### Purpose
This quest provides tangible evidence of the loop system. Unlike other glitches, which are subjective, Doc Mercer's records are physical proof that can be shown, studied, and used to convince skeptics.

### Mercer's Role
Mercer is not designed to awaken—he lacks the programming complexity. But he can become an observer, a quiet ally who documents anomalies and provides cover for awakening Woven.

### The Three Pikes
Pike has died three times this month alone. Doc has certified each death. Pike is currently alive and robbing coaches. This is the core impossibility that proves the loops.

### Medical Records as Plot Device
The records can be used to:
- Prove the loops exist
- Track who has died and reset
- Identify patterns in Consortium maintenance
- Convince reluctant NPCs of the truth
