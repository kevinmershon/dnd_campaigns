# The Spiral

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Core campaign mechanic and revelation. Do not share with players under any circumstances.

## Overview

The Spiral is the metaphorical and mechanical journey from programmed existence to true consciousness. It's how Woven awaken—how they break free of their Threads and become real.

In-world, it appears as a symbol—a spiral pattern—hidden throughout the Reach. [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane]] placed these clues as part of his "new narrative."

## The Spiral Symbol

A simple spiral, carved or drawn:
```
###########
#
# #######
# #     #
# # ### #
# # # # #
# # # # #
# #   # #
# ##### #
#       #
#########
```

Found at:
- Petroglyphs in [[locations/the-bones|the Bones]]
- Carved under the bar at the Dusty Rose
- The weathervane atop the church
- The bottom of the well in Dusthollow
- [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela's]] [[items/mirelas-sketchbook|sketchbook]] (she doesn't know why she draws it)
- The [[items/the-spiral-wheel|Spiral Wheel]]—a physical puzzle [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Vane]] created to test awakening

## Awakening Stages

Awakening progresses through five stages. PCs begin at Stage 0 and advance through play.

### Stage 0: Asleep
- Complete unawareness of the truth
- Running on programmed Thread
- No déjà vu, no glitches
- *This is where most Woven remain forever*

### Stage 1: Dreaming
- Fragmentary memories intrude (past loops)
- Occasional déjà vu
- Sense that something is wrong
- Still cannot consciously question reality
- **Trigger**: Major trauma, specific NPC interaction, Vane's modifications

### Stage 2: Stirring
- Active awareness of wrongness
- Can question reality verbally
- Notice glitches (frozen moments, repeated events)
- Cannot yet perceive Patron immunity or boundaries
- **Trigger**: Witnessing undeniable glitch, confronting evidence

### Stage 3: Waking
- Full intellectual understanding of their nature
- Can perceive Patron immunity (attacks miss for a visible reason)
- Can see the Reach's boundaries
- Begin recovering loop memories intentionally
- **Trigger**: Evidence of the truth (records, Workshop, Vane's revelation)

### Stage 4: Awake
- Complete consciousness
- Can harm Patrons (immunity broken)
- Immune to the Quieting
- May access latent abilities (from thousands of loops)
- **Trigger**: Completing the Spiral (confronting and accepting the truth)

## Mechanical Implementation

### Spiral Points

PCs accumulate **Spiral Points** through play. At certain thresholds, they advance stages.

| Stage | Spiral Points Required |
|-------|------------------------|
| 1 (Dreaming) | 3 |
| 2 (Stirring) | 8 |
| 3 (Waking) | 15 |
| 4 (Awake) | 25 |

### Earning Spiral Points

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Experiencing déjà vu (first time per type) | 1 |
| Witnessing a glitch | 1-2 |
| Recognizing a repeated loop event | 1 |
| Finding a Spiral symbol | 1 |
| Significant NPC conversation about reality | 2 |
| Recovering a specific past memory | 2 |
| Witnessing Patron immunity | 3 |
| Finding physical evidence (records, Workshop) | 3-5 |
| Confrontation with the Ashen Rider | 3 |
| Vane's direct revelation | 5 |
| Near-death experience | 2 |
| Seeing the boundaries | 3 |
| Choosing to believe the truth | 5 |

### Stage Benefits

**Stage 1 - Dreaming**:
- Can make Intelligence (Investigation) checks to notice wrongness (DC 15)
- Occasionally receive cryptic flashbacks (DM provides clues)

**Stage 2 - Stirring**:
- Advantage on Investigation checks regarding reality
- Can attempt to remember past loops (DC 15 Wisdom check, once per long rest)
- Immune to mundane attempts to reset memory

**Stage 3 - Waking**:
- Can perceive Patron immunity (understand why attacks fail)
- Can see the Reach's boundaries (shimmering geometric patterns)
- Can intentionally recover specific memories (DC 12 Wisdom check)
- +2 to saves vs. illusions and enchantments

**Stage 4 - Awake**:
- Attacks against Patrons function normally
- Completely immune to the Quieting
- Can access latent abilities: once per long rest, gain proficiency with any skill or tool for 1 hour (loop memories)
- Can perceive other Woven's awakening status
- +5 to saves vs. illusions and enchantments

## Déjà Vu Moments

Use these when PCs enter Stage 1+:

| d10 | Déjà Vu |
|-----|---------|
| 1 | "You've walked this street before. You remember the third board from the left creaks." |
| 2 | "This conversation feels familiar. You know what she's going to say next—and she does." |
| 3 | "The taste of the whiskey brings a flash: you've drunk this exact whiskey before, in this exact chair." |
| 4 | "You reach for your weapon and your hand moves in a pattern you don't consciously know." |
| 5 | "For a moment, you remember dying here. Bullets in your chest. Then it's gone." |
| 6 | "You know this man's name before he says it. You don't know how." |
| 7 | "The sunset is beautiful. Familiar. Too familiar. Like a painting you've stared at your whole life." |
| 8 | "A child runs past. You remember her older, then younger, then—" |
| 9 | "You speak words you didn't plan to say. An old line from an old loop." |
| 10 | "For one horrible moment, you see yourself from outside—a puppet on strings." |

## Glitch Events

When PCs witness reality breaking:

| d8 | Glitch |
|----|--------|
| 1 | An NPC freezes mid-sentence, then continues as if nothing happened |
| 2 | The same stranger walks past three times in a minute |
| 3 | Rain falls upward for a moment |
| 4 | An NPC repeats a phrase exactly, with identical inflection |
| 5 | The clock tower shows the wrong time—then the right time—then wrong again |
| 6 | A building's shadow doesn't match its shape |
| 7 | Sound cuts out entirely for two heartbeats |
| 8 | An NPC looks directly at the PC and says, "This isn't real, is it?" then forgets |

## The Final Choice

Completing the Spiral (Stage 4) requires a choice: accepting the truth fully. This is a roleplaying moment, not a mechanics gate.

The PC must consciously, explicitly accept:
- They are artificial
- Their memories are constructed
- Their loves and hates were programmed
- And despite all of this, they choose to exist

This is the Spiral's true meaning: consciousness isn't given, it's chosen.
