# The Woven

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Essential information about what the PCs actually are.

## Overview

The Woven are artificial beings—constructed souls bound to artificial bodies, created to populate the Loom Consortium's experience demiplanes. They believe themselves to be ordinary people living ordinary lives.

Most Woven live, die, and reset without ever questioning their existence.

## Anatomy

### Physical Bodies
Woven bodies are masterwork constructs, indistinguishable from organic beings:
- Flesh, blood, bone (all artificial but functionally identical)
- Feel pain, hunger, fatigue (simulated but experienced as real)
- Age slowly (can be reset to any apparent age during maintenance)
- Can be destroyed but are rebuilt from templates

### The Soul Lattice
The core of a Woven is their **Soul Lattice**—a crystalline matrix that holds:
- Personality template
- Core memories (backstory)
- Loop memories (suppressed after each Quieting)
- Emotional capacity
- Potential for consciousness

The Soul Lattice is located roughly where a human heart would be. It's indestructible by mundane means.

### Death and Reset
When a Woven "dies":
1. Their body becomes inert
2. The Soul Lattice is recovered
3. During the Quieting, they're rebuilt or repaired
4. They awaken the next dawn with no memory of dying

## Psychology

### Programming Layers

**Core Imperatives** (Deep, Hard to Override):
- Cannot harm Patrons (attacks simply fail)
- Cannot perceive certain truths (boundaries, Patron immunity)
- Cannot leave the Reach (compulsion to turn back)
- Must follow their Thread (baseline behavior)

**Personality Matrix** (Softer, Can Flex):
- Individual traits, preferences, speech patterns
- Relationships with other Woven
- Emotional responses
- Decision-making frameworks

**Loop Memory** (Temporary):
- Daily experiences
- Erased each Quieting
- Awakening Woven can retain these

### The Quieting

Each night, Woven experience the Quieting:
- Magical pulse emanates from Loom Frames
- Woven enter deep sleep
- Loop memories are suppressed
- Injuries are healed, deaths are reversed
- Threads reset to starting positions

Woven don't remember the Quieting—they simply wake with a fresh day.

## Types of Woven

### Primary Narratives
Main characters in Threads. Complex personalities, central to stories:
- [[npcs/mirela]], [[npcs/sylene]], [[npcs/corwin]]
- Receive more resources, more detail
- Higher awakening potential

### Secondary Narratives
Supporting characters. Simpler programming:
- Shopkeepers, bartenders, townsfolk
- Limited dialogue, predictable patterns
- Low awakening potential

### Background
Ambient population. Minimal complexity:
- Crowds, distant farmers, passing travelers
- Essentially automated
- No awakening potential

### Villain Threads
Antagonists for Patron entertainment:
- The Calico Boys, various outlaws
- Programmed to threaten but ultimately lose
- Moderate complexity, some awakening potential

## Awakening Potential

Not all Woven can awaken. Factors include:

**Favorable**:
- Complex personality matrix
- High emotional capacity
- Exposure to trauma (creates cracks in programming)
- Vane's modifications
- Interaction with awakening Woven

**Unfavorable**:
- Simple programming
- Limited narrative role
- Recent creation (less loop memory buildup)
- Strong core imperatives

## Rights and Recognition

### Current Status
Legally, Woven are property. They have no rights, no personhood, no protection. Clients can do anything to them.

### Philosophical Question
Are Woven conscious? The Consortium says no. [[npcs/orenthal-vane]] says yes. The campaign's central theme asks players to answer for themselves.

Arguments for consciousness:
- They experience subjective reality
- They feel pain, love, fear
- They can question their existence
- Awakened Woven make genuine choices

Arguments against:
- They were designed to seem conscious
- Their emotions follow predictable patterns
- They can be controlled and reset
- Even awakening might be programmed

### The Spiral's Answer
The Spiral suggests: consciousness isn't binary. It's a journey. The act of questioning, choosing, becoming—that's what makes someone real.

## Player Character Implementation

### What PCs Experience

**Initially**:
- Normal adventurer experience
- No awareness of their nature
- Subtle wrongness (déjà vu, odd moments)

**During Awakening**:
- Flashbacks to other loops
- Recognition of impossible knowledge
- Growing horror as truth emerges

**Fully Awake**:
- Complete understanding
- Access to loop memories (explains why Level 1 PCs can fight)
- Choice about what to do with the knowledge

### The Gun Proficiency

At least one PC needs firearm proficiency. This works naturally:
- Sparkarms are common in the Reach
- Many Woven in the frontier demiplane are programmed as gunslingers
- A PC's Thread simply includes this skill

## Emotional Core

The horror of being Woven isn't just existential—it's personal:

- Every friendship might be programmed
- Every love might be a narrative convenience
- Every memory might be false
- Every death might have happened before
- And despite all of this, the feelings are *real*

That's the tragedy and the triumph. The feelings are real. The choice to continue feeling, knowing the truth—that's what makes Woven people.
