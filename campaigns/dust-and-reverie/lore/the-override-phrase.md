# The Override Phrase

> *"The dust remembers what the dawn forgets."*

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Core campaign mechanic. Do not share with players under any circumstances.

## Overview

The Override Phrase is a verbal command embedded in every [[lore/the-woven|Woven's]] core programming by [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane|Orenthal Vane]]. When spoken to a Woven, it creates a temporary bridge between speaker and listener—a shared consciousness that allows influence, control, and even resurrection.

Vane designed it as a failsafe. The [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] uses it for maintenance. And now, the PCs might stumble upon it.

---

## The Phrase

> **"The dust remembers what the dawn forgets."**

This sounds like a frontier proverb—the kind of thing an old-timer might mutter over whiskey. But it's not wisdom. It's a key.

### Origin

Vane embedded the phrase during the original Woven construction. He chose words that:
- Sound natural in conversation (won't trigger suspicion)
- Reference the Quieting obliquely ("dawn forgets")
- Hint at the truth ("dust remembers")
- Could spread organically among awakening Woven

### Who Knows It

| Person | Knowledge Level |
|--------|-----------------|
| [[characters/npcs/orenthal-vane\|Vane]] | Full understanding—he created it |
| [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider\|The Ashen Rider]] | Knows the phrase; has seen it work; cannot use it himself (Patron) |
| [[characters/npcs/sylene\|Sylene]] | Has heard it; suspects its power |
| [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]] | Says it unconsciously when glitching |
| Consortium Staff | Taught as maintenance protocol |
| [[items/whispering-totems#The Sand Walkers\|Sand Walkers]] | Have heard the "Sky Gods" say it |

## Mechanics

### Triggering the Phrase

When the Override Phrase is spoken directly to a Woven:

**Immediate Effect**:
> The Woven freezes. Their eyes go distant, unfocused. For a moment, they're not present—they're *listening*.

The effect depends on the speaker's [[lore/the-spiral|Spiral Stage]].

---

### Abilities by Awakening Stage

The Override Phrase's power scales with the speaker's level of consciousness. As they awaken, the bridge between minds grows stronger.

#### Stage 0: Asleep
**Cannot use the phrase consciously.** May speak it instinctively during moments of extreme stress or déjà vu, causing a brief glitch in nearby Woven (DM discretion).

#### Stage 1: Dreaming
**Momentary Freeze** (1-2 rounds)
> The Woven stutters. Their eyes go blank for a heartbeat. Then they continue, slightly confused, as if they lost their train of thought.

- Duration: 1-2 rounds
- No memory of the freeze
- No roll required
- Cannot command or revive

#### Stage 2: Stirring
**Extended Freeze** (1 minute) + **Simple Suggestions**
> The Woven goes still. Their eyes are distant, unfocused. They're listening to something beyond your words.

- Duration: Up to 1 minute
- Can give a **simple suggestion** (DC 13 Charisma)
- Suggestions must be one sentence, non-harmful to the target
- Examples: "Calm down," "Forget this moment," "Tell me the truth"
- Cannot revive

#### Stage 3: Waking
**Full Command**
> The Woven's eyes meet yours, and for a moment, you share something. A connection. An understanding. They will see what you see. They will want what you want.

- Duration: Until dismissed or they take damage
- Can give **complex commands** (DC 15 Charisma)
- Commands can be multi-step, lasting up to 24 hours
- "They will share in your vision. They will understand your desires as their own."
- The Woven interprets commands through their personality—not robotic obedience
- Cannot directly order self-harm (self-preservation remains)
- Cannot revive

**Examples**:
- "Protect these strangers as if they were your family" → The Woven becomes fiercely protective
- "Lead us safely through the Bones" → They become a willing guide
- "When the Rider comes, you will warn us" → Delayed trigger command
- "Find peace" → They stop fighting, become calm

#### Stage 4: Awake
**Full Control** + **Revive from Death**
> The bridge between you is complete. You speak, and they hear you at the deepest level. Even death listens.

All Stage 3 abilities, plus:

- **Revive**: Speaking the phrase over a Woven who died within 1 minute restores them to full HP
- **Deep Commands**: Can implant lasting directives (DC 18 Charisma) that persist beyond 24 hours
- **Awakening Touch**: Can use the phrase to help another Woven progress on the Spiral (+1 Spiral Point to target)

> The revive bypasses the Quieting. The death happened—and was undone—in the same moment. No reset required.

---

### Limitations

- **Only Woven can use the phrase**: Patrons cannot activate it—they exist outside the system
- Only works on [[lore/the-woven|Woven]] (not Patrons, not natural creatures, not the [[items/whispering-totems#The Sand Walkers|Sand Walkers]])
- Revive only works within 1 minute of death
- Commands fade after 24 hours unless speaker is Stage 4
- **Awakened Woven (Stage 4) can resist**: DC 15 Wisdom save negates all effects
- Using the phrase on an ally creates moral tension—they'll remember being controlled

> [!note] Why Patrons Can't Use It
> The Override Phrase is a backdoor in Woven programming—a bridge between artificial minds. Patrons aren't Woven; they're outsiders, visitors. The phrase has no grip on them, and they have no grip on it. [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider]] has tried for years. It never works.

## Discovery Opportunities

### Session 1: Witnessing the Phrase

**The Piano Player Glitch**

When the piano player freezes (see [[sessions/session-01-arrival#First Glitch|Session 1 glitch]]), expand the scene:

> The piano player hits a wrong note. His hands freeze above the keys.
>
> [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]], watching from the bar, murmurs something under her breath—too quiet to hear clearly. Just a whisper of words.
>
> The piano player shudders. Then he continues, playing the exact same melody from the beginning, as if nothing happened. But there's something different in his eyes. Something... settled.

**DC 18 Perception**: Catch the words: "The dust remembers what the dawn forgets."

**Spiral Points**: +1 for noticing the intervention

---

**The Calico Boys Aftermath** (Optional)

If [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Joss Harlow]] is killed in the [[encounters/calico-boys-showdown|Calico Boys Showdown]]:

>
> She leans close to his ear. Whispers something.

> The woman looks as surprised as anyone. "I... I don't know why I said that. My ma used to say it. I just..."

The bandit is **Della** (see [[offshoots/quest-hooks#The Traitor in the Calico Boys|Calico Traitor quest]]). She doesn't understand what happened.

**Spiral Points**: +2 for witnessing resurrection

### Session 2: Learning the Phrase

**[[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela's]] Confession**

When Mirela shows her [[items/mirelas-sketchbook|sketchbook]]:

> "Sometimes I say things I don't understand," she admits. "Words that feel... old. Important."
>
> She looks at you with desperate hope.
>
> "The dust remembers what the dawn forgets. I keep saying it. I don't know why. But when I say it to people, they... listen. Really listen. Like I'm reaching something deeper than words."

She doesn't understand the phrase's power. But she's used it instinctively on Woven who were spiraling out of control—calming them, helping them.

**[[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Ashen Rider's]] Failed Attempt**

During the [[encounters/the-ambush|ambush]] or any confrontation:

> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] crouches beside a dying bandit, eyes cold.
>
> "Not yet," he mutters. He leans close to the man's ear. "The dust remembers what the dawn forgets."
>
> Nothing. The bandit's breathing slows. Stops.
>
> [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] stands, jaw tight with frustration. "Thirty years," he says to no one. "Thirty years and it still won't work for me."
>
> He steps over the body and continues as if nothing happened.

**DC 14 Insight**: He expected the phrase to do something. He's seen it work. But he can't use it himself.

**Spiral Points**: +1 for witnessing the failed attempt; +1 for understanding why it failed (Patrons can't use the phrase)

This is a key revelation: the Rider, for all his knowledge and power, is locked out of the system's deepest tools. He's an outsider, and always will be.

## Using the Phrase in Play

### When PCs Learn It

Once PCs know the phrase, their abilities depend on their [[lore/the-spiral|Spiral Stage]]:

| Stage | Ability | Roll Required |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| 0 | Cannot use consciously | — |
| 1 | Momentary freeze (1-2 rounds) | None |
| 2 | Extended freeze + simple suggestions | DC 13 Charisma |
| 3 | Full commands (24 hours) | DC 15 Charisma |
| 4 | Revive + lasting commands + awakening touch | DC 15-18 Charisma |

### Moral Weight

Using the phrase is *control*. It overrides free will. PCs should feel the ethical weight:
- They're doing what the Consortium does
- They're treating Woven as tools
- Even "good" commands remove agency

Let players grapple with this.

### NPC Reactions

**If used on [[characters/npcs/mirela|Mirela]]** (before Stage 4):
She complies, but something in her eyes shows recognition. After: "You know the words too. How?"

**If used on [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]]** (before Stage 4):
She resists instinctively (Wisdom save with advantage). After: "Don't. Don't you dare use those words on me."

**If used on awakened Woven**:
They can resist (DC 15 Wisdom save). If they fail, they're horrified afterward—their autonomy was violated.

## DM Notes

### Purpose

The Override Phrase serves multiple functions:
1. **Foreshadowing**: Players can hear it early, not understanding
2. **Player agency**: Once learned, it's a powerful tool
3. **Moral complexity**: Using it raises ethical questions
4. **Worldbuilding**: Shows how Consortium controls Woven
5. **Plot device**: Resurrection without the Quieting

### Pacing Discovery

| Session | Opportunity |
|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Witness Sylene whisper it; optional resurrection scene |
| 2 | Mirela explains she says it; Rider demonstrates it |
| 3 | Full explanation from Vane; PCs can use freely |

### Don't Over-Explain

When NPCs use the phrase, don't immediately explain what happened. Let players piece it together:
- First time: "Something strange happened"
- Second time: "That phrase again..."
- Third time: "Wait, we can do this?"

### Limits to Prevent Abuse

- 24-hour command duration prevents permanent mind control
- Awakened Woven can resist
- Revive only works within 1 minute
- Using it on allies feels *wrong*—they'll remember being controlled
