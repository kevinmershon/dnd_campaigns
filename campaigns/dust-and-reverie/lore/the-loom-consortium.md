# The Loom Consortium

> [!warning] DM ONLY
> Information about the campaign's hidden antagonists.

## Overview

The Loom Consortium is a partnership of archmages, artificers, and wealthy investors who create and operate immersive "experience demiplanes"—pocket dimensions where clients can live out fantasies without consequences.

The Gilded Reach is one of several Consortium properties.

## History

### Founding (200 years ago)
The Consortium began as an academic project: Could artificial consciousness be created? Three archmages pooled resources to build the first Woven—primitive constructs with basic personalities.

### Commercialization (150 years ago)
Realizing the commercial potential, the founders partnered with merchant princes to create the first experience demiplane. It was a success. Money poured in.

### Expansion (50 years ago)
[[npcs/orenthal-vane]] revolutionized Woven design, creating constructs with full emotional depth. The Gilded Reach was his masterwork—a frontier fantasy of unprecedented realism.

### Present Day
The Consortium operates at least six demiplanes:
- The Gilded Reach (frontier/western)
- The Crimson Court (political intrigue)
- The Endless War (military campaign)
- The Sunken Realms (nautical/pirate)
- The Fae Woods (fairy tale/romance)
- The Nightmare Carnival (horror)

## Structure

### The Board
Seven founding families control the Consortium. They're primarily concerned with profit and rarely involve themselves in operations.

### Operations Directors
Each demiplane has a Director who handles day-to-day management:
- **Director Thessaly Croke**: Runs the Gilded Reach. Cold, efficient, treats Woven as inventory.

### Architects
Senior artificers who design Woven and Threads. [[npcs/orenthal-vane]] is Chief Architect across all properties.

### Staff
- **Operators**: Monitor demiplanes, handle maintenance
- **Shepherds**: Security personnel who can enter demiplanes
- **Designers**: Create and update narrative Threads
- **Technicians**: Maintain Loom Frames and infrastructure

## Economics

### Pricing (For Context)
A one-week stay in the Gilded Reach costs roughly:
- **Standard Package**: 50,000 gold (modest nobility can afford annually)
- **Premium Package**: 200,000 gold (includes custom narrative)
- **Unlimited Access**: 1,000,000 gold (includes Board membership perks)

### Client Base
- Bored nobility seeking thrills
- Merchants wanting consequence-free violence
- Scholars studying "artificial consciousness" (ethically dubious)
- Those with dark appetites they can't satisfy elsewhere

## Consortium Ethics (Or Lack Thereof)

The official position: Woven are products, not people. They simulate consciousness but aren't truly conscious. Regulations exist primarily to protect clients and property, not Woven.

Unofficial reality:
- Many staff members suspect Woven are conscious
- Some treat Woven with kindness, others with casual cruelty
- A few whistleblowers have tried to expose the truth (they've been silenced)
- [[npcs/orenthal-vane]] is the most senior figure to believe Woven are truly conscious

## Legal Status

The Consortium operates in a legal gray area:
- Construct creation is legal
- "Consciousness simulation" isn't covered by personhood laws
- Client confidentiality is absolute
- Some kingdoms have banned Consortium facilities (religious objections)
- Others have embraced them (significant tax revenue)

## The Consortium in Play

### As Faceless Antagonist
The Consortium isn't a villain players can punch—it's a system. Vast, impersonal, operating on profit motive. Even if they destroy the Reach, other demiplanes continue.

### Key NPCs
- **Director Croke**: The face of Consortium authority. Could appear in Act 3.
- **Shepherds**: The immediate physical threat if players cause problems.
- **Operators**: Potential witnesses, some might be sympathetic.

### Consequences of Revelation
If the truth about Woven consciousness became public:
- Massive legal battles
- Religious outcry
- Potential shutdown of demiplanes
- Clients who committed "crimes" against Woven might face consequences
- The Board would do anything to prevent this

## Using the Consortium

The Consortium provides:
1. **Scale**: The horror isn't one madman—it's a system
2. **Stakes**: Destroying the Reach doesn't end the problem
3. **Gray Areas**: Some staff are sympathetic, not all clients are monsters
4. **Options**: Negotiation, exposure, destruction—all viable endings

The Consortium shouldn't be defeated in a Level 1-3 campaign, but players can:
- Escape the Reach
- Expose operations to the outside world
- Negotiate Woven rights for this specific demiplane
- Destroy the Reach (killing everyone inside, including themselves)
