# Dusthollow Townsfolk

Secondary NPCs who populate the town. Simple Woven with minimal awakening potential unless otherwise noted.

---

## Father Aldous

*The preacher who drinks too much and quotes scripture that isn't quite right.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Chapel Preacher |
| Location | Chapel / Dusty Rose |
| Awakening Potential | Low (but knows more than he understands) |

### Appearance
- Disheveled clerical collar
- Rheumy eyes, red nose
- Trembling hands
- Carries a worn prayer book

### Personality
Guilt-ridden, haunted, seeking answers at the bottom of a bottle. His sermons contain spiral imagery planted by Vane that he doesn't consciously understand.

### Key Dialogue
- "The good book says, 'Round and round the path leads, but the center waits for those who seek.' Or something like that."
- "I pray every night. Sometimes I wonder if anyone's listening. Sometimes I wonder if anyone's *there*."
- "Bless you, child. May you find the path... the spiral path... what was I saying?"

### Hidden Detail
His "scripture" quotes are actually Vane's programming—clues to the Spiral embedded in religious language. He doesn't know this.

## Harken

*The cheerful general store owner who never sleeps.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Store Owner |
| Location | Harken's General Store |
| Awakening Potential | None |

### Appearance
- Portly, balding
- Perpetually cheerful expression
- Always wiping hands on apron
- Wire spectacles

### Personality
Relentlessly friendly. Helpful to a fault. His cheer never wavers because it can't—he's a simple loop NPC.

### Key Dialogue
- "Welcome, welcome! New in town? Let me know if you need anything."
- "We got supplies, ammunition, sundries. Fair prices—I don't gouge."
- "The Rose has rooms if you're staying. Tell Sylene I sent you."

### Hidden Detail
He's been running the same loop for decades. Same phrases, same gestures, same merchandise that never quite runs out.

## Doc Mercer

*The town physician who's never lost a patient overnight.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Physician |
| Location | Doc Mercer's Practice |
| Awakening Potential | Low (notices things, doesn't question) |
| Family | Father of [[characters/npcs/saloon-regulars#Lucky Mercer\|Lucky Mercer]] |

### Appearance
- Tired eyes, steady hands
- Vest and rolled sleeves
- Perpetual five-o'clock shadow
- Smells of carbolic soap

### Personality
Brusque, efficient, doesn't ask questions. He's noticed that injuries heal overnight and patients recover impossibly fast. He's chosen not to think about it.

His son [[characters/npcs/saloon-regulars#Lucky Mercer|Lucky]] is a source of quiet disappointment—the boy had steady hands and a sharp mind, could've been a fine surgeon. Instead he wastes his days at the card tables. Doc doesn't talk about it.

### Key Dialogue
- "Unless you're bleeding, you'll have to wait."
- "People get hurt. People get better. That's all I see."
- *If pressed*: "Man comes in with a bullet wound on Monday, he's fine Tuesday. I write it off as frontier toughness. You should too."

### Hidden Detail
He has logs going back years. If examined, they show the same injuries recurring, the same patients dying and recovering, the same impossible healings.

## Old Brennan

*The half-deaf livery owner who knows everyone's business.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Livery Owner |
| Location | Brennan's Livery |
| Awakening Potential | None |

### Appearance
- Face like weathered leather
- Permanently squinting
- Bowlegged from decades on horseback
- Smells of hay and horse

### Personality
Gruff, impatient with conversation, devoted to his animals. His deafness is real (part of his character design), making him frustrating to talk to.

### Key Dialogue
- "Eh? What's that? Speak up!"
- "Horses? Got 'em. Rooms? Try the Rose. Food? Try the Rose."
- *About the Rider*: "That one in black. He comes through regular-like. Never boards a horse. Don't know how he travels."

## Ma Pickett

*The stern boarding house owner who hates the Dusty Rose.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Boarding House Owner |
| Location | Ma Pickett's Boarding House |
| Awakening Potential | None |

### Appearance
- Severe black dress
- Hair in tight bun
- Pinched expression
- Always watching, always judging

### Personality
Self-righteous, judgmental, convinced she's the only moral person in town. Her "respectable" establishment is the opposite of the Rose.

### Key Dialogue
- "I run a *respectable* house. No drinking. No smoking. No cursing. No... *company*."
- "That Sylene woman runs a house of ill repute. Sheriff should shut it down."
- "This town's gone to sin and I'm the only one who sees it."

### Rules
- 1 gp/night
- Curfew at 9 PM
- Porridge for breakfast (always porridge)
- She monitors hallways

## Cade

*The blacksmith who never stops working.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Blacksmith |
| Location | Cade's Forge |
| Awakening Potential | None |

### Appearance
- Massive build, arms like tree trunks
- Leather apron, burn scars
- Shaved head, thick beard
- Always at the anvil

### Personality
Nearly mute. Communicates in grunts and nods. Only talks when absolutely necessary, and then briefly.

### Key Dialogue
- "Need something forged? Horseshoes, tools, hinges. Nothing fancy."
- *About weapons*: "Don't make weapons. Don't need 'em."
- *About town*: "It's a town. People live here."

### Hidden Detail
He made the sheriff's star. He doesn't want to remember the sheriff who wore it.

## Holt

*Mirela's "father"—a simple farmer Woven.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Farmer |
| Location | [[locations/dusthollow-details#Mirela's Ranch|Mirela's Ranch]] |
| Awakening Potential | None |

### Appearance
- Weathered farmer's hands
- Kind, simple face
- Sun-faded work clothes
- Moves slowly, deliberately

### Personality
Content with simple work. Loves Mirela. Doesn't understand her restlessness or her strange drawings. He's not designed to understand—he's designed to give her something to protect.

### Key Dialogue
- "Sun's up. Work to do."
- "Mirela's a good girl. Smart. Too smart for this place, maybe."
- "I don't understand her drawings. But if they make her happy..."

### Hidden Detail
Holt has "died" in previous narrative threads. There's a grave behind the house with his name on it—a remnant from a version where Mirela avenged his death. Neither of them notices it.

## Conversation Guide

> These are simple NPCs. No DC-tiered revelations—what you see is what you get. They can provide directions, local color, and atmosphere, but won't advance the plot significantly.

### Useful For
- **Harken**: Supplies, directions to the Rose
- **Brennan**: Horses, observations about travelers
- **Doc Mercer**: Healing, subtle hints about impossible recoveries
- **Father Aldous**: Spiral clues hidden in "scripture"
- **Ma Pickett**: Contrast to the Rose, judgmental commentary
- **Cade**: Equipment repairs, silence
- **Holt**: Emotional stakes for Mirela
