# Corwin Dray

> *"I'd ride into hell itself if she asked me. Have, actually. More times than I can count."*

## Appearance

- Rugged good looks, strong jaw, kind eyes
- Brown hair, perpetual five-o'clock shadow
- Wears a battered leather duster
- Carries a well-maintained sparkrifle and pistol
- Moves with easy confidence

## Personality

**Core Thread**:
The loyal protector. Noble, brave, self-sacrificing. Exists primarily to support Mirela's narrative and die heroically when needed. He's the perfect romantic hero—which is precisely the tragedy.

**Hidden Depths**:
Corwin's programming is simple compared to Mirela or Sylene. He doesn't question, doesn't doubt. But beneath the surface code, there are... fragments. Moments of something more that are always suppressed.

## Backstory (As He Knows It)

Corwin was a lawman somewhere back East. Something bad happened—he doesn't like to talk about it. He came to the frontier to start over and found purpose protecting Mirela and her father. He dreams of a future with her, once he's dealt with his past.

## The Truth

There is no past. The "tragedy" is a programmed justification for his melancholy and his skill with violence. Corwin exists to:
1. Give Mirela someone to protect
2. Provide a source of romantic tension
3. Die heroically (repeatedly)
4. Give Patrons an opponent to defeat

He's been killed thousands of times. By Patrons for sport. By outlaws in the narrative. Sacrificing himself for Mirela. Each death resets with the Quieting.

## Narrative Loop (Thread)

| Time | Action |

## Roleplaying Notes

**Voice**: Steady, warm, with a rural drawl. Never raises his voice unless in battle.

**Mannerisms**:
- Tips his hat to ladies
- Stands slightly in front of Mirela when strangers approach
- Cleans his weapons when thinking

**Key Phrases**:
- "I gave my word. That's all a man's got."
- "You don't have to worry. Not while I'm breathing."
- "Something about today feels different. Can't put my finger on it."

## Tragic Role

Corwin is designed to die. His purpose is to be killed so Patrons can feel like heroes saving Mirela, or so the narrative has stakes. He's died by:
- Outlaw bullets
- Patron "accidents"
- Sacrificing himself for Mirela
- Execution by the Ashen Rider

The tragedy deepens when Mirela awakens and realizes their love was programmed—and that she's watched him die countless times without remembering.

## Awakening Potential

> [!warning] Corwin Cannot Awaken
> Corwin is specifically designed to resist awakening. His Thread is the "loyal protector" archetype—simple, direct, stable. The Consortium uses characters like him to ground narratives and prevent cascade failures when other Woven break programming.

Corwin's code is simpler, more tightly controlled. He **cannot fully awaken**, meaning:
- He won't progress on the Spiral regardless of experiences
- Vane's modifications don't affect him—his Thread was already optimized for stability
- He may believe the PCs without understanding what they're experiencing
- He chooses to fight alongside them out of loyalty, not comprehension

**In Session 3**: Corwin joins the group at the ranch and follows them through the finale. He fights for Mirela and the PCs, but when they discuss the Quieting's pressure or their awakening experiences, he simply... doesn't remember. He had a good night's sleep. That's all.

This poses a moral question for awakened characters: Is Corwin's loyalty less real because he can't understand what they've become? What does it mean that he fights for them anyway?

## Story Function

Corwin represents the cost of awakening—the realization that those you love may be as artificial as yourself, and possibly less able to be saved. He's a test of the PCs' empathy and a reminder that the Woven are not all equal in their capacity for consciousness.
