# Saloon Regulars

NPCs who frequent the Dusty Rose. They provide atmosphere, potential side interactions, and occasional glitch opportunities.

---

## Lucky Mercer

*The gambler who always wins the first hand.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Professional Gambler |
| Location | The Dusty Rose (card tables) |
| Awakening Potential | None |
| Family | Son of [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Doc Mercer\|Doc Mercer]] |

### Appearance
- Thin, quick hands (inherited from his father)
- Nervous laugh
- Fancy waistcoat (worn at the edges)
- Always shuffling something

### Personality
Charming, slightly desperate, not as lucky as his name suggests. His "luck" is programmed—he wins early to hook players, then loses to make them feel good.

His father is [[characters/npcs/dusthollow-townsfolk#Doc Mercer|Doc Mercer]], the town physician. The two don't speak much—Doc wanted his son to follow him into medicine, but Lucky chose the cards. Neither remembers the argument that drove them apart; it resets each morning, leaving only the distance.

### Key Dialogue
- "Care to try your luck? I'm feeling generous today."
- "Lady Luck's a fickle mistress, friend. Today she favors you."
- *When losing*: "Well, that's the game, isn't it? Can't win 'em all."

### Hidden Detail
Lucky cheats. Subtly, unconsciously—it's part of his Thread. He doesn't know he's doing it. [[characters/npcs/joss-harlow|Hollis Tate]] has started to notice.

### Thread Conflict
Each day around midday, the card game turns violent. Hollis accuses Lucky of cheating. If not interrupted:
- Hollis throws a punch
- Lucky draws a knife
- Someone gets hurt
- Reset by morning

## Hollis Tate

*The rancher who's bad at cards and doesn't care.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Rancher |
| Location | The Dusty Rose (card tables) |
| Awakening Potential | Low |

### Appearance
- Heavyset, ruddy-faced
- Booming laugh
- Dusty range clothes
- Calloused hands

### Personality
Loud, friendly, here for the company more than the cards. He loses money he can't afford and doesn't seem to mind—because he won't remember by morning.

### Key Dialogue
- "I'm down six bits but I'm up two friends. That's a profit in my book!"
- *When losing*: "Well, that's my wife's egg money. She's gonna tan my hide."
- *Before the fight*: "You're cheating, Mercer. I know you are. I just can't prove it."

### Thread Conflict
His programming includes suspicion of Lucky. Each day, the suspicion builds until it boils over. He's not wrong—Lucky does cheat—but the violence is scripted either way.

## Clara Vance

*The widow who's better at cards than she lets on.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Widow / Secret Card Sharp |
| Location | The Dusty Rose (card tables, occasionally) |
| Awakening Potential | Low-Medium (glitches visible) |

### Appearance
- Sharp eyes, calm demeanor
- Faded black mourning dress
- Neat grey hair
- Underestimated by everyone

### Personality
Quiet, observant, strategic. She plays the harmless widow while methodically taking money from overconfident men.

### Key Dialogue
- "I'll see your bet." *Long pause.* "And raise."
- "My late husband taught me to play. I like to think I've kept my hand in."
- *After winning*: "Beginner's luck, I suppose." *It's never luck.*

### Glitch Tell
Sometimes she stares at her cards with a flicker of confusion, as if she's seen this exact hand before. She has—hundreds of times.

## Old Jem

*The prospector who tells impossible stories.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Retired Prospector |
| Location | The Dusty Rose (corner table) |
| Awakening Potential | Minimal (too simple) |

### Appearance
- Ancient, weathered
- Missing teeth
- Perpetually nursing one beer
- Smells of dust and old leather

### Personality
Rambling, half-senile, full of stories that contradict each other. He's been in the same corner telling the same tales since the Rose opened.

### Key Dialogue
- "Back in sixty-two—or was it fifty-eight?—I found a vein of silver wide as my arm. Then the mountain ate it. Swallowed it right up."
- "Mountains do that, y'know. Eat things. Spit 'em back out different."
- *Muttering*: "The thing in the mine... the thing in the mine..."
- *Then falls asleep.*

### Hidden Detail
His stories contain fragments of truth. "The thing in the mine" is the Loom Frame buried beneath [[locations/the-bones]]. He doesn't know what he saw, but he saw something.

## The Garrett Brothers

*Tom and Billy. Always arguing. Always about horses.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Cowboys |
| Location | Main Street / Dusty Rose |
| Awakening Potential | None |

### Appearance
- Identical builds (not twins, just similar)
- Dusty work clothes
- Animated gestures when arguing
- Never seen apart

### Personality
Loud, good-natured, competitive. Their entire existence is the argument. It defines them. They don't exist outside of it.

### The Eternal Argument
**Tom**: "The bay's faster and you know it!"
**Billy**: "The bay couldn't outrun a lame mule!"
**Both** (if interrupted): "Stay outta this, stranger. This is family business."
*They immediately return to arguing.*

### Glitch Tell
They repeat the argument word-for-word every few minutes. Same gestures, same inflections. If players watch long enough, they'll notice the cycle.

## Widow Parsons

*The old woman who's still waiting for her husband.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Widow |
| Location | The Dusty Rose (window table) |
| Awakening Potential | Low |

### Appearance
- Elderly, fragile
- Faded black dress
- Stares out the window constantly
- Sips sherry, never finishes it

### Personality
Melancholy, distracted, trapped in an endless loop of waiting. Her husband "died" in a previous narrative thread, but she was never updated to stop expecting him.

### Key Dialogue
- *If approached*: "Have you seen my husband? He went out this morning and..." *She trails off.* "What was I saying?"
- "He should be back by now. He's never this late."
- *About the town*: "Dusthollow's changed. Or maybe I have. Hard to tell sometimes."

### Tragic Detail
Her husband was a background Woven who was removed during a Thread update. His removal was clean—he simply ceased to exist. But Widow Parsons's programming was never adjusted. She'll wait forever.

## Conversation Guide

> These NPCs provide atmosphere and occasional clues. They're not designed for deep interaction—what you see is mostly what you get.

### Best Uses

| NPC | Story Function |
|-----|----------------|
| Lucky | Card game drama, cheating subplot |
| Hollis | Escalating tension, likeable victim |
| Clara | Hidden competence, subtle glitches |
| Old Jem | Cryptic hints about the Bones |
| Garrett Brothers | Comic relief, glitch example |
| Widow Parsons | Pathos, the tragedy of simple Woven |

| NPC | Observable Glitch |
|-----|-------------------|
| Lucky | Unconscious cheating he doesn't know about |
| Hollis | Same accusation, same escalation, daily |
| Clara | Recognizes hands she's seen before |
| Old Jem | Stories contradict, mentions "the thing in the mine" |
| Garrett Brothers | Argument repeats exactly |
| Widow Parsons | Waiting for someone who doesn't exist |
