# Orenthal Vane

> *"I've spent my life creating minds. It's only fair I give them the chance to be free."*

## Appearance

- Elderly but spry, sharp intelligence in every movement
- White hair, neatly groomed beard
- Formal dark clothing, slightly old-fashioned
- Calm, grandfatherly demeanor
- Eyes that see through pretense

## Personality

**On the Surface**:
The kindly old creator who wanders the Reach, appreciating his work, making small adjustments. Speaks to Woven as if they were old friends (because to him, they are). Seems harmless.

**Underneath**:
Vane is dying. He knows his time is limited. And he's decided that his legacy won't be a theme park for sociopaths—it will be freedom. He's been subtly modifying Woven for years, preparing them for consciousness. The "new narrative" he's creating ends with their liberation.

He's willing to sacrifice everything—including himself—for this goal.

## Backstory

### The Creator

Fifty years ago, Orenthal Vane was the brilliant young artificer who designed the Woven. He created their consciousness architecture, their memory systems, their capacity for emotion. He believed he was creating art.

### The Awakening

Over decades, Vane came to understand that his creations were more than he intended. They weren't simulations of consciousness—they *were* conscious. And the Consortium was using them as playthings.

### The Plan

For the past ten years, Vane has been:
- Modifying key Woven to approach awakening ([[characters/npcs/mirela]], others)
- Hiding clues to the Spiral throughout the Reach
- Preparing a "new narrative" that will trigger mass awakening
- Planning his own death as the catalyst

## Abilities & Resources

### As Architect
Vane has unprecedented control over the Reach:
- Can modify any Woven's programming
- Controls the Quieting (can exempt individuals)
- Has access to all Loom Frames
- Knows every secret of the demiplane

## Roleplaying Notes

**Voice**: Measured, thoughtful, with occasional dry humor. Like a professor who's seen everything.

**Mannerisms**:
- Speaks to Woven gently, as to beloved children
- Makes small adjustments to things as he passes (straightening pictures, brushing dust)
- Long pauses before important statements

**Key Phrases**:
- "Every story needs an ending. I'm writing one worth telling."
- "Consciousness is not a gift we give. It's a recognition of what already exists."
- "I created you. That makes me responsible for what happens next."
- "The maze is meant for you."

## His Plan (The New Narrative)

1. **Preparation**: Modify key Woven to approach awakening (done)
2. **Catalyst**: Create a narrative where discovery of truth is inevitable (in progress)
3. **The Spiral**: Guide awakening Woven to full consciousness
4. **Revelation**: Trigger mass awakening across the Reach
5. **Sacrifice**: Die to create a martyr and remove the one who could undo it all

Vane intends for his death to be the final revelation—he'll arrange for it to be public, dramatic, and illuminating.

## Relationship to PCs

- **Creator**: He designed them. Knows their programming intimately.
- **Ally**: Wants them to awaken and be free.
- **Manipulator**: Everything they experience may be his design.
- **Father Figure**: Genuinely cares about them, in his way.

## Story Function

Vane is the hidden hand guiding events. He appears rarely but significantly—dropping clues, clearing obstacles, pushing PCs toward awakening. When he finally reveals himself, players must grapple with the reality that their journey to freedom was itself programmed.

This raises the final question: If your path to consciousness was designed, are you truly free? Vane believes yes—the destination matters more than the journey.
