# Dusty Rose Staff

NPCs who work at the Dusty Rose saloon. Most are simple Woven, though some have more depth than expected.

---

## Silas

*The bartender who pours the exact same pour every time.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Bartender |
| Location | The Dusty Rose |
| Awakening Potential | None |

### Appearance
- Heavyset, walrus mustache
- Permanently tired eyes
- White apron, rolled sleeves
- Moves with practiced economy

### Personality
Professional, laconic, observant. He's seen everything and is surprised by nothing. His emotional range is narrow by design.

### Key Dialogue
- "What'll it be?"
- "Tab's due end of the week. Don't make me remind you again."
- "You look like you've had a long ride. Whiskey fixes that."
- *About himself*: "Me? I've been here long as the Rose has stood, I reckon. Don't think about it much."

### Glitch Tell
He pours the exact same pour every time—same angle, same speed, same amount. A motion repeated thousands of times identically.

## Emmett

*The piano player who only knows one rotation of songs.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Piano Player |
| Location | The Dusty Rose |
| Awakening Potential | Minimal (but cracks show under pressure) |

### Appearance
- Gaunt, thinning hair
- Nicotine-stained fingers
- Vacant expression when playing
- Becomes confused when interrupted

### Personality
Emmett barely exists outside his function. When playing, he's focused. When stopped, he's lost—he doesn't know what to do with himself.

### Song Rotation (45 minutes, repeats)
1. "My Darling Clementine"
2. A melancholy waltz (untitled)
3. An upbeat dance number
4. "Streets of Laredo" (funeral song)
5. The waltz again
6. Two minutes of silence
7. Repeat

### Key Dialogue
- *If asked for a request*: "I... I don't..." *Hands twitch toward keys.* "I only know these ones, friend. Always have."
- *If stopped from playing*: *Stares at hands, confused.* "What do I... what am I supposed to..."

### Glitch Tell
- Same wrong note in "Clementine" every time
- Fingers move before the sound
- During the silence, he doesn't blink

## Ruby

*The bold one. Red hair, loud laugh, no shame.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Working Girl |
| Location | The Dusty Rose |
| Awakening Potential | Low |

### Appearance
- Red hair (dyed)
- Freckles
- Bold dresses, low cuts
- Infectious laugh

### Personality
Forward, friendly, unbothered by rejection. She's comfortable in her role and doesn't question it. Her confidence is part of her programming.

### Key Dialogue
- "Well now, look what the wind blew in. You look like you could use some company, stranger."
- *If declined*: "Your loss, handsome. I'll be around if you change your mind."
- *If declined rudely*: "Suit yourself. Some men don't know quality when they see it."

---

## Pearl

*The quiet one. Watchful. Knows more than she says.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Working Girl |
| Location | The Dusty Rose |
| Awakening Potential | Low-Medium (observant by design) |

### Appearance
- Pale skin, dark hair
- Quiet, watchful eyes
- Simpler dress than Ruby
- Often in shadows

### Personality
Reserved, analytical, notices everything. She was designed to be "the mysterious one," but her observation subroutines sometimes pick up things they shouldn't.

### Key Dialogue
- "You're not from around here." *A statement, not a question.* "I can always tell."
- "The man in black? He's been coming here for years. Never touches anyone. Just watches."
- *If pressed*: "Some things don't add up in this town. I've stopped trying to make them."

### Hidden Depth
Pearl has noticed patterns. She doesn't understand them, but she's filed them away. She could be a source of clues if players earn her trust.

---

## Delilah

*The experienced one. Den mother to the others.*

### Quick Reference
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Role | Working Girl (Senior) |
| Location | The Dusty Rose |
| Awakening Potential | Low |

### Appearance
- Older than the others (appears 40s)
- Elegant, refined
- Knows everyone by name
- Maternal warmth

### Personality
Wise, warm, protective of the younger women. She acts as intermediary between clients and girls, and between girls and [[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]].

### Key Dialogue
- "First time at the Rose? Let me buy you a drink. Sylene likes to know who's new in town."
- "Ruby's enthusiastic but means well. Pearl's shy but worth knowing. And I'm here if you need someone to talk to."
- *About the town*: "Dusthollow's a good place. Quiet. Same faces, same stories. Some folks find that comforting."

## Conversation Guide

> See [[offshoots/dusty-rose-activities]] for detailed interaction options with these NPCs.

### Social Dynamics
- **Ruby** approaches strangers first
- **Pearl** hangs back but notices everything
- **Delilah** manages relationships and information
- **Silas** serves drinks and deflects questions
- **Emmett** plays unless interrupted
- **[[characters/npcs/sylene|Sylene]]** oversees everything

### What They Know
| NPC | Useful Information |
|-----|-------------------|
| Silas | Who drinks what, who owes tabs, recent arrivals |
| Emmett | Nothing (too simple) |
| Ruby | Surface gossip, who's interested in whom |
| Pearl | The Ashen Rider's patterns, things that don't add up |
| Delilah | Everyone's business, the Rose's history, Sylene's moods |
