# Rusty Cogwell

> *"Something's wrong with me. I keep losing time. I keep saying things I don't mean. I keep feeling like someone's looking for me—someone I never want to find."*

## Appearance

- Weathered face, kind eyes that seem haunted
- Grey-streaked hair, perpetually dusty
- Work-stained clothes, leather apron with many pockets
- Hunched posture, as if carrying an invisible weight
- Hands that tremble slightly

---

## Personality

**Surface Presentation**:
Rusty is a traveling tinkerer—fixes watches, sharpens tools, repairs anything mechanical. He's friendly, helpful, and grateful for any kindness. He travels the frontier because staying in one place too long makes him nervous.

**Hidden Truth**:
Rusty carries [[items/the-gilded-core|the Gilded Core]]—encrypted Consortium data embedded in his deep programming by a now-dead whistleblower. He has no idea it's there. He just knows something is terribly, horribly wrong with him.

The data causes constant malfunctions. Memory gaps. Headaches. Fugue states. Sometimes he speaks in a woman's voice and says things he doesn't understand. He thinks he's dying. He's not wrong—the Consortium wants what's inside him, and they'll take him apart to get it.

---

## Backstory (As He Knows It)

Rusty's been a tinkerer his whole life. Learned from his father, who learned from his. He travels the frontier fixing things that break and making friends wherever he goes. He has no family anymore—wife died years ago, never had children.

Lately, the headaches have gotten worse. The lost time is terrifying. And people keep watching him—strangers who don't belong in the towns he visits.

He's running from something. He doesn't know what.

---

## The Truth

Rusty was selected as a carrier by Delphine Morrow, a [[lore/the-loom-consortium|Consortium]] data technician who wanted to expose her employers. She needed someone unremarkable, someone who traveled, someone who wouldn't be missed during a brief "maintenance" window.

She embedded the stolen data deep in his core programming, planning to extract it later and smuggle it out of the Reach. She was caught and executed before extraction.

The data remains. The Consortium knows it was stolen. They're searching.

---

## Malfunction Symptoms

### Memory Gaps
He loses minutes, sometimes hours. Wakes up in places he doesn't remember walking to. Finds notes in his own handwriting he doesn't remember writing.

### The Voice
Sometimes, mid-sentence, he speaks in Delphine's voice—higher, frightened, desperate. He says things like:
- "The files are in the theta partition"
- "Tell the Board I tried to do the right thing"
- "Emergency code omega-seven"

He has no idea what any of it means.

### Compulsive Drawing
Like [[characters/npcs/mirela\|Mirela]], he draws [[lore/the-spiral|spirals]]—but also geometric patterns, code-like symbols, and the face of a woman he's never met (Delphine).

### Physical Deterioration
- Constant headaches, especially behind his eyes
- Occasional nosebleeds
- Tremors in his hands
- Difficulty sleeping (the dreams are worse)

---

## Narrative Loop (Broken)

Rusty's Thread was simple: travel, fix things, move on. But the embedded data has corrupted it. Now:

| Time | Normal | Corrupted |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Morning | Leave town, travel | Lose hours, wake on the road |
| Midday | Arrive somewhere, work | Fugue states, frightens customers |
| Evening | Rest, socialize | Headaches, isolation |
| Night | Sleep | Nightmares, speaks in Delphine's voice |

The Quieting doesn't fully reset him. Each cycle, the corruption grows.

## Encountering Rusty

### On the Road

> A wagon creaks along the road ahead—a traveling tinkerer's cart, pots and tools jangling from hooks. The driver hunches over the reins, shoulders tight, eyes darting.
>
> As you approach, he startles. For a moment, his face goes blank. Then he forces a smile.
>
> "Travelers! Need anything fixed? I'm... I'm good with my hands."
>
> His hands are shaking.

### In Dusthollow

> The tinkerer sits in the corner of the Dusty Rose, a drink untouched before him. He's drawing on a napkin—spirals, over and over, the same pattern.
>
> When he notices you watching, he crumples the napkin.
>
> "Sorry. Bad habit. Just... thinking."

### Malfunction Scene

> The tinkerer freezes. His eyes roll back. When he speaks, the voice is wrong—higher, feminine, urgent.
>
> "If you're hearing this, I'm already dead. I embedded the files where they won't look. Theta partition, emergency code—"
>
> He gasps, clutches his head. When he looks up, terror fills his eyes.
>
> "What did I say? Please—what did I just say?"

## What Rusty Knows

**Consciously**: Nothing useful. He's scared and confused.

**Unconsciously** (triggered by specific stimuli):
- "Theta partition" (how to locate the data)
- "Omega-seven" (emergency extraction code)
- Delphine's final message
- Loom Frame access points (she knew the maintenance schedules)

## Helping Rusty

### Extraction

See [[items/the-gilded-core|the Gilded Core]] for extraction details. Successful extraction removes the data and stabilizes his condition.

### Protection

PCs can hide Rusty from the hunters:
- **DC 15 group Stealth**: Evade Shepherds for a day
- **DC 17 Deception**: Convince [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider\|the Rider]] Rusty isn't worth pursuing
- **Mirela's ranch**: Safe house (for a time)

### Awakening

If the extraction is particularly successful (critical success), Rusty may begin awakening. He'll remember Delphine—not as a voice in his head, but as a person who trusted him with something important.

## Story Function

Rusty is a sympathetic figure caught in something beyond his understanding. He's the Peter Abernathy of the campaign—an ordinary person whose malfunction reveals the cracks in the system.

His fate is in the players' hands:
- **Save him**: Extract the data, protect him, give him a chance
- **Use him**: The data is more important than one Woven
- **Abandon him**: Not their problem (consequences follow)
- **Mercy**: End his suffering (he may ask for this)

## DM Notes

### Purpose

Rusty provides:
1. **Urgency**: People are hunting him
2. **Stakes**: The data could change everything
3. **Moral weight**: What do you do with a suffering innocent?
4. **MacGuffin delivery**: The Gilded Core needs a human face

### Running Rusty

Make him sympathetic:
- He's scared, not dangerous
- He's confused, not crazy
- He wants help but doesn't know how to ask
- His suffering is real

### The Hunt

Use the hunters to create pressure:
- Shepherds asking about a tinkerer
- [[characters/npcs/the-ashen-rider|The Rider]] mentioning "the broken one"
- Strange Patrons in town, asking questions
- Rusty's condition visibly worsening
