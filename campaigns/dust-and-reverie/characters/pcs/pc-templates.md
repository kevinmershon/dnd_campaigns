# Player Character Templates

## Character Creation Guidelines

### The Truth (DM Eyes Only)
All PCs are **Woven**—artificial beings running narrative Threads. Their backstories are implanted. Their skills come from thousands of loops they don't remember.

### What Players Know (Initially)
Players create normal frontier characters. Don't reveal the Woven truth until the campaign reveals it.

### Requirements
- **Level 1** (advance to 3 by campaign end)
- **At least one PC** must have sparkarm proficiency
- Backstories should have vague elements ("I came from back East" without specifics)

## Suggested Archetypes

### The Gunslinger

> *You've got a quick hand and a quicker trigger. The frontier respects that.*

**Class**: Fighter or Ranger
**Background**: Outlander, Criminal, or Soldier
**Key Ability**: Dexterity

**Thread (Hidden)**: You're programmed to be the combat lynchpin—the one who draws first and shoots straight. You've died protecting people thousands of times.

**Sparkarm Proficiency**: Yes (essential)

**Suggested Build**:
```
Fighter 1
Stats: Str 8, Dex 15+2, Con 14, Int 10, Wis 14, Cha 8
Fighting Style: Archery (+2 ranged attacks)
Equipment: Sparkcarbine, sparkpistol, leather armor
Race: Human (variant) or any with +2 Dex
```

**Awakening Manifestation**: You know gun maintenance you were never taught. Your hands reload automatically, muscle memory from a thousand loops.

---

### The Gambler

> *Cards, dice, people—everything's a game if you know how to play it.*

**Class**: Rogue or Bard
**Background**: Charlatan or Entertainer
**Key Ability**: Charisma

**Thread (Hidden)**: You exist to add social complexity—making deals, reading people, creating drama. You've cheated the same marks in a hundred loops.

**Sparkarm Proficiency**: Optional (derringer-style sparkpistol fits)

**Suggested Build**:
```
Rogue 1
Stats: Str 8, Dex 15+1, Con 10, Int 14, Wis 11, Cha 15+1
Expertise: Deception, Insight
Equipment: Leather armor, rapier, small sparkpistol
Race: Human (variant) for +1 Dex, +1 Cha, plus a feat
```

**Awakening Manifestation**: You know tells before people show them. Sometimes you know what someone will say before they say it—because you've heard it a hundred times.

---

### The Wanderer

> *You came from somewhere else. You don't remember where.*

**Class**: Ranger or Cleric
**Background**: Hermit or Folk Hero
**Key Ability**: Wisdom

**Thread (Hidden)**: The mysterious stranger with a past. You're designed to add mystique, ask questions that have no answers. Your amnesia is a feature, not a bug.

**Sparkarm Proficiency**: Optional

**Suggested Build**:
```
Ranger 1
Stats: Str 12, Dex 14, Con 14, Int 8, Wis 15+2, Cha 8
Favored Enemy: Humanoids
Natural Explorer: Grassland/Desert
Equipment: Leather armor, longbow, shortsword
Race: Wood Elf or any with +2 Wis
```

**Awakening Manifestation**: The holes in your memory are *too* clean. You remember everything except where you came from. That should be impossible.

---

### The Preacher

> *You carry the Word to the godless frontier. Faith is your armor.*

**Class**: Cleric
**Background**: Acolyte
**Key Ability**: Wisdom

**Thread (Hidden)**: Moral compass, voice of conscience. You've given the same sermons, buried the same dead, offered the same comfort—eternally.

**Sparkarm Proficiency**: No (but could carry one unfired, as a "symbol")

**Suggested Build**:
```
Cleric 1 (Life or Light Domain)
Stats: Str 14, Dex 10, Con 14, Int 8, Wis 15+2, Cha 10
Equipment: Scale mail, shield, mace, holy symbol
Race: Hill Dwarf or any with +2 Wis
```

**Awakening Manifestation**: Your prayers feel rehearsed because they are. You've said these words a million times. But for the first time, you wonder if anyone's listening.

---

### The Secret Patron (Optional)

> [!warning] ADVANCED OPTION - COORDINATE WITH DM

One player could secretly be a **Patron**—a wealthy outsider paying for the "authentic experience" of not knowing they're in a simulation.

**Why**: Creates dramatic irony. This player is the only "real" person—but they don't know it either.

**Coordination**: DM and player agree secretly before Session 1. Player plays normally. In Session 2 or 3, reveal they're different (attacks against them fail even from awakened Woven).

**Dramatic Payoff**: They're real, but their companions are "artificial"—yet the companions have become conscious. Who's more "real" now?

## Character Sheet Additions

### Thread (Hidden from Player Initially)
A one-line summary of the character's narrative purpose:
- "The protective gunhand who dies heroically"
- "The charming rogue who lightens the mood"
- "The spiritual guide who offers comfort"

### Loop Memories (Unlock at Stage 2+)
When awakening, players unlock memories from past loops:
- Skills they don't remember learning
- Relationships with NPCs that "just started"
- Deaths they survived

### Spiral Points Tracker
Add to character sheet after Session 1:
```
Spiral Points: [  ]
Current Stage: [0/1/2/3/4]
```

## Equipment: Sparkarms

All PCs have access to frontier equipment. Sparkarms are arcane-mechanical firearms.

| Weapon | Damage | Range | Properties | Cost |
|--------|--------|-------|------------|------|
| Sparkpistol | 1d10 piercing | 30/90 | Ammunition, loading | 250gp |
| Sparkcarbine | 1d12 piercing | 40/120 | Ammunition, loading, two-handed | 500gp |
| Derringer | 1d8 piercing | 20/60 | Ammunition, loading, light | 150gp |

**Loading**: You can fire only once per action, bonus action, or reaction regardless of attacks per round.

**Ammunition**: 1gp for 10 spark cartridges.

## Session 0 Guidance

### Character Hooks to Plant
Encourage players to include:
- Vague pasts ("something bad happened back East")
- Missing memories ("I don't remember the journey here")
- Recurring dreams ("I keep dreaming about a spiral")
- Déjà vu tendencies ("I feel like we've met before")

### Avoid
- Overly specific backstories with named locations
- Family members the player wants to visit
- Detailed pre-Dusthollow chronologies

### The Stagecoach
All PCs arrive together. None of them remember boarding. This is the Thread's starting point—their existence, from their perspective, begins here.
